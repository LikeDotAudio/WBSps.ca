GENERAL

M924 POWER SUPPLY



The M924 power supply is designed to house third party power supplies which comply with worldwide regulatory body safety standards.

The M924 provides +/-23 volts DC at currents totalling 5.8 amps maximum. Two diode isolated outputs per polarity are provided. Each output has a singular capacity of 4 amps.

For redundant operation, a connector is provided on the rear of the chassis which allows multiple M924 to be operated in parallel.
An LED indicator for each of the two bipolar outputs tallies the status of each fused output. An extinguished LED is indicative of an open fuse.

Front panel test points are provided. The test points are located after the power supply and before the fuses functionally; and provide a useful trouble shooting aid.

Please refer to section 5 of  the manual for further details.
