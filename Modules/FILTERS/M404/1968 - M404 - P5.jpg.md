The equalizer section is interposed via SW4  The switching configuration permits the HF CUT (low-pass) and the LF CUT (high pass) to be inserted separately or collectively Thus with SW4 in the HI position the unit functions as low pass filter with SW1 (HF CUT) producing the prescribed curve selections and SW3 (LF CUT) removed from the circuit_ In this mode SW3 is available for the pre-set Conversly, with SW4 in the Lo position, SW3 is effective and SW 1 may be adjusted without interacting with the high-pass function With SW 4 selected to HI/Lo both filter selections are effective and cumulative characteristics may be obtained at both frequency extremes
