SOLO/cUE Output is provided and appears at pins E at the module connector This output is designed as S0K ohm current source and operates into the zero impedance summing node of the SOLO/cuE summing bus The SOLO/cuE switch SW6 also prov ides dc control contact to effect control of external circuitry _
AuX Output
An Aux output is provided to feed an auxiliary mixing bus . With the PRE/ Post switch Sw7 the signal can be picked up either ahead of the attenuator (PRE) or after the attenuator (Post) 1SdB loss pad in the PRE position of SW7 reduces the signal Tevel in the PRE- position to that of the POST-position (nominal attenuator in-hand setting is 1SdB )
AuX level control R87 provides level control for the AuX channel and switch SW1o is the AUX channel assignment switch.
The AuX output, appearing at pins & at the module connector is designed as 20K ohm current source and operates into the zero impedance summing node of the AuX summing bus
PEAK Indicator
The signal for the PEAK indicator is picked up at the output of the output amplifier and is rectified with diodes CR7 and CR8 . u8 with its associated components forms voltage comparator which compares the rectified audio signal with an externally supplied reference voltage With the reference voltage ranging from to approximately +10 volts, calibration of the overload point from -6dBm to +24dBm is possible. PEAK overload is indicated with LED DS2 .
Power Supply Decoupling
CRI CR2 , C1 to C4 with R1o and R11 provide the decoup ling network from the +/ - 25 volts power supply bus to operate the internal amplifier circuitry at nominal +/ - 16 volts
CRI and CR2 guard against the inadvertent application of reverse power supply voltages.
