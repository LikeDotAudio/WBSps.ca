```text
COMPRESSOR/LIMTER MODEL M4o6
FUNCTIONS OF CONTROLS: The M406 has three groups of concentric controls to be used throughout the operation of the unit. These transient-free controls may be pre-set or adjusted during the operation of the unit.
Upper Concentric_ group: Inner (small) knob: THRESHOLD This iS an input gain control designed to adjust the input level to the compressor and control amplifier . Since the compressor circuit always commences to compress at a specified level and introduces fixed degree of compression (depending on the slope 2/1, 3/1 , etc.), an input control must be provided to accommodate the variations of signal amplitude at the input _ The Threshold control provides this adjustment. Note; If the Threshold control is reduced to zero (cCw) there will be no Signal output from the M406.
Outer (large) knob: OUTPUT . This iS an output gain control designed to adjust the output level of the compressed program material to the required level of the system The Output control operates independently and does not affect the setting of the input control_ Note: If the Output control is reduced to zero (CCW) there will be no Signal output from the M406_
Centre Concentric group: Inner (small) knob: GR & VU SELECTOR  This selector switch connects the external VU meter to the compressor electronics In the VU position the meter is connected to the amplifier output terminals to provide a VU indication of the outgoing compressed program material _ In the GR position the meter is transferred to the control amplifier to indicate the amount of gain reduction in db. A subsidiary gain reduction scale is provided on the meter scale.
Outer (large) knob: COMPRESSION _ The five position Compression selector determines the ratio of compression for the control amplifier. The options are 2/1 3/1 5/1 _ 10/1 and L (limit)
```
