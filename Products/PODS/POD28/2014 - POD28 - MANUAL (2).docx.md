HD-SDI DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER


This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers.  PODS are compact, rugged and affordable. They are suited for tabletop use or rack-mountable singly or in pairs, in any combination.

___________________________________________


POD28 is a six output digital video distribution amplifier. It features cable equalization and re-clocking techniques that enable the unit to reliably recover the incoming digital audio signal.

POD28 can receive and distribute digital video signals, over coaxial cable (BELDEN 8281 or equivalent). An adaptive cable equalizer provides a complete solution for equalizing high-bit-rate digital data transmitted over long transmission lines. The equalized data is retimed at one of the  standard  data rates (143, 177, 270, 360 Mbps or 1.485 Gbps) and is recommended for signals transmitted over cable runs up to 300 metres (1000 ft.) in 75Ω installations.

All inputs and outputs to POD28 are made via 75Ω BNC connectors located on the rear panel.
