GENERAL

M8208 REMOTE LEVEL AMPLIFIER




The M8208 Remote Level Amplifier allows remote level control of an audio signal with a remotely located potentiometer.

A transformerless, actively balanced input amplifier with state of the art common mode rejection (greater than 80 dB from 20 Hz to 20 kHz); permits the M8208 to handle signals in a myriad of harsh environments with excellent results.

The signal level may be adjusted remotely over a 65 dB range with little signal degradation. This allows the signal level to be optimally set to maintain system headroom requirements. The output stage of the M8208 is highly compliant (+30 dBu maximum output), assuring maximum dynamic range possible at line output levels.
The M8208 Remote Level Amplifier has been conveniently packaged for integration into the WBS D8212 Audio Distribution System. The M8208 may be mounted in an MF82 frame with additional M8200 series amplifiers to provide a versatile audio system.
Input

The input signal to the amplifier is applied at connector pins 1 and A, and the input shield to pin B.

R1 and R2 set the amplifier input impedance to 30,000 ohms. Integrated circuit U1 with it's associated components make up the input preamplifier circuit.

Diodes CR1 through CR4 protect the preamplifier input from excessive input signals. Trimmable capacitor C5 provides high frequency common mode rejection adjustment, while R6 provides low frequency rejection adjustment.

Sensitivity control R13 controls the feedback around preamplifier U1. The preamplifier output is coupled through C12 to the voltage controlled amplifier (VCA) consisting of U2, U3 and associated components.

VCA

The DBX2151 is a current input, current output voltage controlled amplifier. A control voltage of zero volts at pin 3 of U2 results in a current transfer ratio of 1 (i.e. no gain or loss).

For alignment purposes, this current relationship is realized by removing link LK1. A (-13) dBu signal present at U1 pin 7 is the nominal operating level. By observing the amplifier output at pins 5 and E; trim potentiometer R17 (symmetry) is adjusted for minimum distortion. Potentiometer R16 (OFFSET) is trimmed for less than 5 mV of DC output off set at U3 pin 6.

Control Amplifier

The VCA control voltage is developed by U6D. A two-slope linear approximation to a  audio-taper potentiometer is provided by Q5, R46, R47, R49 and R50. This allows a standard 5K ohm linear potentiometer to be used as a level control actuator. Integrated circuit U6C provides a polarity reversal to U6D's output. Link LK1 is present to allow convenient removal of the control voltage; which is particularly useful for alignment purposes. Diode CR5 prevents the VCA from going into a positive gain operating state.

Output Amplifier

An actively balanced output amplifier is composed of two sections; the upper half comprising U4 and associated components, and the lower half consisting of U5 and it's related components.

Two direct coupled actively balanced outputs are provided at module connector pins 4 & D and 5 & E; output 1 and output 2 respectively.

Output 1 is a zero ohm output feed, while output 2 has a 60 ohm output impedance set by R33 and R34.

The output amplifier is capable of a  maximum output of +30 dBu into a bridging load of  1500 ohms or greater. A single 600 ohm load may be driven to a +26 dBm level.



Power Supply Decoupling

The ±22 volt DC supply voltage appearing at amplifier connector pins 15 & S and 13 & P are isolated from the amplifier circuitry by fusing resistors R60 and R61. The incoming ±22 volt supplies are filtered by C38 and C39, after which they connect directly to the output amplifiers (U4 and U5). The VCA current to voltage amplifier (U3) is powered from the ±22 volt power supplies also; R22, R23, C17 and C18 further decouple the power to U3.
Transistors Q6, Q7 and R62 through R66 couple the ±22 volts down to ±18 volts for use by preamplifier U1 and control amplifier U6.

Three terminal regulators U7 and U8 provide an absolute reference voltage for the control amplifier and the VCA.

The M8208 draws a quiescent 40 mA from the ±22 volt power supply. At full output into a single 600 ohm load, the supply current is 75 mA.
