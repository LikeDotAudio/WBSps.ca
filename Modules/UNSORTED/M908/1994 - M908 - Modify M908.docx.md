### Page 1 (Word document)

```text
Pierre:

Here is the information to modify the M908 module, I have also included the schematic showing were to add the resistors. If you have any questions please give me a call.

Bob Murphy:  Ward Beck Systems;


Procedure for adding 30 ohm resistors to output of M908 module:


1: Lift the Plus side of C28, C34, C63 and  C69 for output  "A" , and C105, C111, C140 and C146 for output "B" out of the Motherboard of the M908 module:

2: Add a 30 ohm 1/4 watt resistor between the plus side of each capacitor that was lifted out of the motherboard and the plus side of the pad for each capacitor on the Motherboard.
```
