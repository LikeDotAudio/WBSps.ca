## What it is

The October 2012 idea of a Raspberry Pi behind every LCD TV in a mall, controlled from an openGear frame. The flow diagram is redrawn as a working network: add monitors, compare unicast, multicast and uploaded files, switch content, and try the RFID-targeting idea.

## In the simulation

- Flow diagram redrawn and clickable
- Unicast vs multicast vs local files
- Server-port load and the “infinite monitors” claim
- Content changes wait for the next key frame
- Notes vs diagram disagreement
