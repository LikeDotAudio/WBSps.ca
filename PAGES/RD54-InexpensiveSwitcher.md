## What it is

The October 2012 idea of a low-cost switcher on one openGear card with a wide custom rear. Size the rear and matrix, then run takes, locks and renames from three user panels: the multi-user problem the meeting notes called the hard part.

## In the simulation

- Multi-slot rear drawn at openGear slot pitch
- Matrix size from connector type and rear width
- Three user panels with ownership and locks
- Takes wait for the next frame or field
