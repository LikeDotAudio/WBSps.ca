## What it is

A tour of the folder’s small 2005 boards, drawn from their Gerbers. The largest, WBS_IO2, turns out to be the 554951-3 communication interface that links two R2K consoles and a custom R257 turret; its real firmware drives the simulation. Also an RS-232/RS-422 converter, a CS8412 DIP-to-SOIC carrier, the WBS I/O-1 board and the R257 turret boards.

## In the simulation

- Five boards rendered from Gerbers
- 554951-3 polling cycle from the real firmware
- Custom turret with setup mode
- RS-232 to RS-422 bit timing
- CS8412 adapter pin tracer
