M405P PORTABLE EXTENDED RANGE VU METER

The M405P Extended Range VU Meter is a battery operated, portable instrument designed for the measurement of transmission, noise and VU levels throughout an audio system.  The instrument produces a 0 VU indication on a 3-1/2" meter for input levels from -50 dBm to +30 dBm over a 20 Hz to 20 kHz bandwidth.

An input attenuator, calibrated in 2 dB steps, accommodates the full 80 dB range in one continuous 300 degree rotation.  The M405P can operate in the 600Ω terminated or Hi Z bridging mode.  In addition, a selectable 60 Hz notch filter allows measurements in the presence of hum.  The power ON/OFF switch includes a TEST position to check the charge on the internal batteries.

Inputs  to the M405P are via an XLR-3(F) connector or a 1/4" TRS jack.  A front panel 1/4" jack allows for aural monitoring of the measured signal with a high impedance (typically 400Ω) headset.

The M405P is factory fitted with 9V rechargeable ni-cad batteries.   Optionally, the unit may be powered by disposal alkaline cells.  When the unit is equipped with rechargeable cells, an AC power adapter can charge the cells while simultaneously powering the M405P.
