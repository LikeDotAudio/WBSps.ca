LF CUT X 100 Control- This is concentric control shared with the IN /OUT function The LF CUT function is affected by the larger outer control knob The control produces rapid roll-off commencing at the low frequency extremes to achieve high- pass filter characteristics The frequency designations indicated on the photo-etched escutcheon must be converted X 100 to correspond to the operating frequency The nomenclature applies to the ~3db point on the attenuation curve E G  7=70h2 , 1-l00hz and 5= 500hz , etc
HI HI/LO Lo Control- This three position function selector permits either the HF CUT or the LF cut to operate independently such that when one control is in use the other may be re-assigned to produce different effect and then re-entered into the program circuit at the desired moment The centre position of the control allows for both filter controls to be inserted cumulatively to create pass-band Of frequencies such as telephone effect.
IN/OUT Control- The smaller inner concentric knob is two position selector and is used to insert or remove the equalizer circuits _ In the CCW position (OUT) the M404 operates as conventional amplifier (Note: the entire electronics is not disconnected, only the active equalizer section is removed from the program circuit) In this mode the equalizing effects may be pre-set or adjusted without interfering with the flat frequency response of the M404 _ At the prescribed time the selector may be turned CW to the IN position and the pre-set response characteristics will modify the program transmission_
MA INTENANCE: The M404 is designed for dependable, trouble_ free service; however , as with all high quality electronic equipment, regular schedule of inspection should be established_ Any accumulation of dust should be periodically removed with soft brush and vacuum hose
To gain access to the  component boards and switch decks it is necessary to remove the metal side cover which is to the life side of the etched escutcheon _ This is accomplished by removing the two 2-56 flat-head screws on the left side Of the M404 escutcheon and the two 4-40 round-head screws located on the bottom of the unit. Do not loosen the 4-40 screw immediately adjacent to the M404 connector this secures the frame ground lug internal to the equalizer
