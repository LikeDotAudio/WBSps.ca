DESCRIPTION: The M4O6B Compressor / Limiter is a plug-in, high performance module designed to be compatible with the Ward-Beck group of modular console components Two concentric controls, a single rotary threshold control and an alternate-action insert pushbutton are provided to adjust the operating characteristics of this high speed Compressor/ Limiter.
FEATURES:
Operational amplifier design
5 selectable compression slopes
The M4O6B operates with unity gain. A rotary stepped input threshold control determines the degree of compression (sets the compressor operating point) . The threshold point may be adjusted from _12dBm to 8dBm. Other controls include independent adjustments for Compression Ratio , Gain Reduction/VU metering, Recovery Time and De-Essing: An alternate-action pushbutton activates the Compressor control circuit, when depressed , and an illuminated status lamp indicates compression action:
Comprehensive control functions including Threshold, Compression Ratio, Recovery Time , De-ess_ GRIVU and IN/OUT selector. Fast attack-time
