                              STEREO INPUT MODULE
The R221 accepts two balanced stereo console, inputs, A or B, and has individual front
panel accessible screw driver adjustments to trim the level of the left (lower trim) and
right (upper trim) channels. A MO NO pushbu tton switch will sum the left and right
stereo signal. This fea ture is particularly us eful when only a m ono signal is available
at the input or either of the channels of the stereo signal is missing. The MONO
function may be selected by pressing the front panel pushbutton or it may be forced via
an internal jumpe r.

Stereo balanced insert points are provided prior to the FADER and CHANNEL ON
switch. Convenient insert bypass jumpers are located on the module circuit board.

The pre-FADER, pre-CHANNEL ON , CUE switch allows the operator to preview an
incoming signal on the cue speaker located in the console meter bridge.

The direct audio FADER controls the overall signal level throu gh the m odu le. A
reference indicating mark at the 12dB in-hand position is provided. The CHANNEL ON
control routes the signal to the output stage of the module.

The signal may be assigned to any or all of stereo PROGR AM 1, 2, 3, 4 or M ON O M IX
1,2 mixing buses. All mixing buses are balanced. Send levels to the MON O MIX 1and
2 buses may be adjusted via front panel potentiometers. Each input module also has
a stereo DIRECT output that follows the CHANNEL O N switch.

All selec t, function and assignment switches are equipped with LED indicators. The
LEDs illuminate to indicate that the switch has been activated. A separate channel on
LED indicator in parallel to channel on switch indicator is provided.

Each R221 input m odule has four programmable input and four programmable output
controls for ex terna l control. The m odule may also be configured to activate the MUTE
A, B, C, AUTO CUE or TIMER R ESET buses. An RS485 serial I/O port on the R221
allows som e m odu le functions to be und er se rial con trol.
