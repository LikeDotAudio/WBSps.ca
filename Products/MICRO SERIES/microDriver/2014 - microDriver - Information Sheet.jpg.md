Product Description The microDriver is miniature stereo power amplifier on a printed circuit board. Powered from an external +24 Volt source, the unit is designed to drive headset or small high efficiency speakers_ The compact size of the microDriver makes it ideal for integration into third party equipment such as studio panels consoles etc;
The microDriver is equipped with standard 1/4" TRS jack, which may be used to mount the assembly to metal plate_ Connections the  microDriver are   made via Panduit; crimp type connectors Two 12" umbilicals, terminated with the proper Panduit mating connector type are supplied as part of the microDriver The audio and power inputs to microDriver are via Panduit connector P2_ 1OK dual audio taper potentiometer is recommended for volume control on Panduit connector P1. The microDriver can be ordered just as the board and potentiometer (microDriver), housed in metal enclosure designed for under desk mount (microdriverB), or boxed under desk mount; with XLR/.25" TRS audio & power jacks (microDriverC).
