## What it is

High-density dual-card audio rear: two 9-pin Phoenix blocks per card, each with one input, three balanced outputs and a ground. Built for two AA6201A analog DAs as dual 2×3. The netlist fits 887R, the T6403 value; the pick-and-place file fits the allocated 604R.

## In the simulation

- ISS1 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- 16 pairs traced to P101/P102
- AA6201A dual 2×3
- ID 887R duplicates the T6403
