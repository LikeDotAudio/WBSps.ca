M52OOA CONTROLLER MODULE
The M5ZOOA is the central microprocessor based, data processing unit for the ST series of Stereo Television consoles
This controller performs numerous supervisory control functions within the console system. It controls the mute logic facilities for the mixing channels integrated channel machine control starthstop functions; and control of the console monitoring channels M525 )
The M52OOA integrates and controls the following optional features of the St console series; the M524A programmer unit and the M526 overload status assembly .
Six dual bit parallel multiplexed address/data ports are made available for feeding data in and out of the control system_ Two cata ports are available for discrete control inputs and outputs Sixteen functions per port are made available. The inputs for these discrete functions are isolated through optocouplers and the outputs are isolated with dry contact relay closures_
The logic family of integrated circuits is known as the 74HC series which is the latest in high speed cMOS technology The logic levels for the data ports for this family of integrated circuits and the data ports of the M52OOA are nominally 3.5 volts or greater for logic Tevel and volt or less for logic level ; this is for volt supply
ninth data port, which has access to the internal bus structure of the M52OOA , is made available to allow for system expansion _
AI data ports are on 37 pin D ' female connectors, while the internal data bus port is accessed through 37 pin 'D' male connector
The M52OOA is packaged in standard nineteen inch rack mount assembly occupying one rack unit of vertical space (1.75 inches ) and is sixteen inches deep
The M52OOA is powered by an external 12 volt unregulated power supply The power to the control ler is via 15 pin D ' connector
