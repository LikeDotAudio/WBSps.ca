POD26
AUDIO LEVEL AMPLIFIER

This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers.  PODS are compact, rugged and affordable. They are suited for tabletop use or rack-mountable singly or in pairs, in any combination.
          
_________________________________________


POD26 is a six input/output audio level amplifier. Six separate screwdriver adjustments, on the front panel, allow the user to trim the levels of the six outputs independently.  Input and output connections to POD26 are via screw terminals located on the rear panel.
