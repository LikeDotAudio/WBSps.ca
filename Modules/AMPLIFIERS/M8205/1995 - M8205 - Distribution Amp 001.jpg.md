M8205 M8205B
Audio Distribution Amplifiers
General Description Already recognized internationally as the ultimate distribution amplifiers, the M8205 and M8205B offer outstanding performance, superior build-quality, incredible reliability in compact, cost-effective packages   In short affordable excellence_
sensitivity controls An ultra low noise preamplifier circuit results in Signal to noise ratios of greater than 104 dB at unity gain, while itheuleptconalyE providing actively balanced inputs with high common mode rejection  Powerful output circuitry provides eight actively balanced outputs with a maximum Of +28 dBu capability with all outputs simultaneously loaded.
Designed to provide eight actively balanced splits from single input signal, both DA's are virtually transparent to the program path: The M8205 offers 600 ohm outputs for systems applications requiring matched load distribution standards, while the M82OSB presents low Z outputs, 60 ohms, most suitable for systems applications with voltage distribution standards_
The modules mount in a standard Ward-Beck MF-82 31/2" rack-mounting frame and are fully compatible and may be mixed and matched with all 8200 series Preamplifiers, Amplifiers, Oscillators and other associated modules: Twelve DA's may be accommodated in one MF-82 frame along_ with dual redundant power supplies permitting high density package offering 12 inputs and 9 outputs.
The units are equipped with high quality, conductive plastic, 30 dB continuously variable
