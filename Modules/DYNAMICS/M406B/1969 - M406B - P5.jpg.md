VU / GR: This is small concentric knob atop the compre- ssion control. This selects an external VU meter to indicate the output level of the compressor In the "VU" position, it is calibrated to read "0" when the output level is OdBm . In the GR" position (gain reduction) the meter will read 0 when compression begins the meter will deflect downward giving an indication in aB of the amount of compression or gain reduction being applied to the incoming signal_
Recovery: Determines the rate at which the compressor recovers from its compressed condition. The control is labelled 1, 2 , 3 4 , 5 positions corresponding to %4 , 12 , 1, 3 _ & 5 seconds
De-ess-: This is the small concentric knob atop the recovery control The control is labelled 0 2 , 4, 6 , 8 , which indicates in aB the amount of additional high frequency compression provided when the compression switch is set to the 2/1 slope. Note: The purpose of this is to help reduce balance sounds from persons voice and should be used only on the 2/1 compression slope with short recovery time position or 2 )
