## What it is

An MLC6 multichannel level control built into an AMS8-style chassis with two speakers and an AMS8E-81 power amp. Only the June 2006 metalwork survives: the bottom pan, its parts layout and an unnamed front strip with six channel holes and a 25-LED master encoder.

## In the simulation

- Bottom pan and front strip drawn from the DWGs, to scale
- Assembly tutorial: fit speakers, amp, encoder and strip
- Master encoder with its 25-LED ring
- Speaker fold-down and amplifier headroom
- Mis-named and duplicated drawing files
