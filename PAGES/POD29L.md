## What it is

POD29 variant with an XLR rear: two 5-pin outputs carrying chassis, +24 V, 0 V, −24 V and +48 V phantom. Its sister POD29E puts ±12 V on the same panel with pin 5 unused. Only a bottom-plate drawing is in its own folder; the panels come from the POD29X silk and drawings, dated May 2015.
