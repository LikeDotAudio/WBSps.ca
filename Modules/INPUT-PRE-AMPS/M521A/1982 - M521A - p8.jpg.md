GENERAL
M521A STEREO INPUT MODULE
Direct left and right channel outputs are provided at the module connector after to the Balance control These direct feeds may be optionally used for multitrack applications or to feed the inputs of an external switching system.
The output from this module may be assigned to any or all of four stereo submaster mixing buses The bus selectors are preceded by Balance control, which gives the operator the ability to balance up level variations between the left and right channel signals being fed to the submaster mixing buses
The channel Solo take off point follows the setting of the Balance control  The operator is thus able to hear the effects of this control when the channel is soloed into the console monitoring system .
The Channel On and Solo functions of the M521A Input Module are made via solid state FET switches providing for very reliable and silent SW itching functions The Channel On and Solo control functions may also be controlled external to the module by the mixing console microprocessor based control system.
Four Auxiliary outputs are provided_ each with separate Tevel control assignment switch and pre/post fade selector Auxiliaries and feed stereo buses, while Auxiliaries and feed mono buses
mono post fade feed is brought out to the edge connector for use with an external channel assign module.
LED Program and Overload level indicator monitors the audio Levels at the prefade insert points of the module. The green LED of this two colour indicator will Muminate when the audio Tevel is at or above the nominal operating level of the module (0 dBm) If the audio level approaches the overload point then the green indicator extinguishes and the red indicator iHuminates The overload point is set approximately 22 dB above the insertion point operating levels .
The Channel On and Solo control functions, their tallies, as wel l as the tallies of input source selections, subassignments and that of the mixing attenuator being active are all fed to the console control system by way of the module control assembly SA 4-077
