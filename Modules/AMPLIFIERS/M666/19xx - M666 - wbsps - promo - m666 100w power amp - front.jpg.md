DESCRIPTION: The M666 100 watt audio power amplifier is an ac operated, plug-in module designed for professional applications in the broadcast, motion picture and sound recording industries. The unit represents the continuing development and expansion of the well proven WBS 600 series of modular components and offers a wider scope of selection for monitor amplifiers from this design category. The circuit utilizes differential operational amplifiers to produce an ultra- reliable transformerless design capable of developing a minimum of 100 watts rms to drive an 8 ohm loudspeaker directly Other Load impedances (4, 16 ohms, etc:) may be accommodated with appropriate power output inter- polation. The amplifier does not sustain damage even when deliberately shorted at full power output:

Compact design mounts in standard WBS 57 inch MFGA rack frame
Self contained power supply
Operational amplifier transformer- less IC design with unique short- circuit protection

1Ok bridging input balanced/ unbalanced
