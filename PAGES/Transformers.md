# Ward-Beck transformers

Ward-Beck designed and drew its own audio transformers. The type numbers are a family code: **60-n input**, **70-n output** and **80-n power** transformers. Many of the early drawings (1966–68) are drawn or checked by [RB (Rodger Beck)](Person-RB.md). Each entry shows the drawing(s), the date on the title block, and the modules the type is used in (from parts lists, filed drawings and manuals).

[Input 60-n](#input) · [Output 70-n](#output) · [Power 80-n](#power)
