This is one of many products in the MP SERIES line of classic analog moving needle meters, housed in a high quality two-rack unit assembly, from WARD-BECK SYSTEMS.

___________________________________________


The MP4 is part of the Ward-Beck’s MP Series of classic analog moving needle meters, housed in a high quality 2RU assembly. Internally powered from AC mains, the MP4 meters features VU and Peak meters adhering to the highest broadcast specifications. The MP4 custom panels contain four meters of your choice, in the combination required to suit your metering needs.

MP4(VU) Classic Rackmount Analog Needle Meter Panel - Quad VU

MP4(2P2V) Classic Rackmount Analog Needle Meter Panel - Dual PPM + Dual VU
MP4(3P1V) Classic Rackmount Analog Needle Meter Panel - Three PPM + One VU
