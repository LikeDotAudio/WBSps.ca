## What it is

openGear card that embeds up to four groups of audio into HD/SD-SDI: eight external AES inputs (CS8416 receivers, AD1895 rate converters) or the incoming embedded pairs, chosen per AES pair, are written into the HANC by a Cyclone II EP2C35. An AT90CAN128 running Ward-Beck’s own main loop reads the receivers and a 128-bit FPGA status word, drives twelve LEDs and talks to DashBoard; an M6204AE variant carries a CAT-559 Dolby encoder module.
