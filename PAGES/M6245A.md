## What it is

Power-bay board for the MF6220 openGear frame: passes AC to a 12.5 V brick, ORs its output onto +12 V, makes −7.5 V with a PTN78020A wired as an inverter, and reports temperature, fan and current to the frame controller over 1-Wire (DS2438).

## In the simulation

- Netlist-matched to the MF6220 PSU connector
- Polyfuse trips at frame loads
- 1-Wire poll at AN126 bit timing
- Current shunt not in the load path
- TC670 fan alarm with no clear line
