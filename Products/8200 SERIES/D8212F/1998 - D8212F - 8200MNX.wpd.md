﻿8200 Series Distribution System

General 

The 8200 Series Distribution System delivers a strategic, modular solution for the distribution of analog audio, digital audio, and video signals.  The basic system comprises of a choice of three MF82 frames which will house any combination of 8200 Series cards to suit you system design goals.  Readily available inputs and outputs to 8200 Series cards within the MF82 frames are made via nine different plugable and easily interchangable terminal interfaces.

Frames

Three basic frames
MF82/4

The MF82/4 frame occupies just one vertical rack unit of space and can house four 8200 series cards, and one power supply.  

MF8212

The  MF8212 occupies two vertical rack units of space and can house twelve 8200 series cards, along with redundant power supplies.  

MF8210

The MF8210 occupies two vertical rack units of space and can house ten 8200 series cards, along with redundant power supplies.

Trim controls are conveniently position along the front edge of the cards for easy adjustment.  The ingeniously designed hinged/quick release front panel may be removed and replaced in a matter of seconds for access to the controls.
Two separately fused primary AC power connections are available at the rear of the standard MF-82 mounting frame.  The system operates on 115/230V, 50/60 Hz requiring 130 VA maximum.  The separate AC power connections ensure the highest overall system reliability possible. 

Terminal Interfaces

Located on the rear of the MF82 frames are respective berg headers for each amplifier slot.  There are nine different terminal interface assemblies available that conveniently attach to the berg headers on the rear of the frames to allow readily available input and output connections.

Grounding

At the rear of the MF-82 mounting frame a separate binding post is provided for the mechanical frame chassis and one for the system electrical ground.  These two points are isolated from each other and should eventually connect together at the audio system station ground point.
The chassis connection should be connected to all other mounting frames and rack cabinetry either directly from the binding post, or via the neutral "U" ground connection ( the green wire) integral to the primary AC  wiring.
The use of separate chassis and ground wires returned to a central station ground point will assure a system with the minimal amount of AC and possible RF currents circulating in the audio ground system.  
HINT : Keep audio systems ground connections free from any AC, video, control or possible RF ground returns.
