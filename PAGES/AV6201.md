## What it is

openGear composite-video distribution amplifier (sold as AV6201A and Cobalt 9231): differential input, GB4571 clamp, two-stage cable equaliser, eight 75 Ω outputs. An AT90CAN128 busy-waits on an LMH1981 sync separator once per field and reports to DashBoard over CAN. Modelled pass by pass from the V101 firmware and the ISS3 netlist.

## In the simulation

- Fields, lines and sync pulses through the analog path (IN vs OUT, cable, EQ, hum, clamp)
- LMH1981_VIDEO_DETECT busy-wait and the 11-bit line-count word
- Real ISS3 gerber card, card edge, T6201/T6301/T6401 rear modules and DashBoard-style window
- PARAMETER_UPDATE, heartbeat and DashBoard messages on a 1 Mbit/s CAN timeline
- Findings: Cobalt build, commented-out pushbuttons, R15 vs the EQ jumper, unsaved Notify
