# M450B · RTO-144 Connections

M450B - Program / Summing Amplifier · ELCO 00-6007-015-940-002 · 15 contacts, single row

![Rear connector](connector.svg)

![Inputs and outputs](io.svg)

## Audio connections

| Port | Signal | Dir | Pin + | Pin − |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | SUMMING INPUT | in | 3 | 5 |
| A2 | PROGRAM INPUT | in | 4 | 6 |
| B1 | OUTPUT | out | 12 | 13 |

| Rail | Volts | Max A | Supply | Ground |

| VCC-AUDIO | 25 |  | 1 | 8 |
| VEE-AUDIO | -25 |  | 15 | 8 |
