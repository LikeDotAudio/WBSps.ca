## Audio connections

| Port | Signal | Dir | Pin + | Pin − |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Mic In | in | 1 | A |
| A2 | Line | io | 10 | L |
| A3 | Right OUT | out | 16 | T |
| B1 | Insert Send | out | 14 | R |
| B2 | Insert Return | in | 13 | P |
| B3 | CHON OUT - OUT | out | 12 | N |
| B4 | Fader Return | in | 11 | M |
