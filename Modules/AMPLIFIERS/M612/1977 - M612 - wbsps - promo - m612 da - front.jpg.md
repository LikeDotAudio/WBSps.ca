M612 AUDIO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER
DESCRIPTION: The M612 audio distribution amplifier is an ac operated, plug-in unit designed to bridge a single source and provide twelve uniform, highly isolated outputs for distribution throughout an audio system_
FEATURES:
Integral bi-polar power supply for maximum reliability.
This ultra-reliable design utilizes a bi-polar power supply to operate balanced differential, operational amplifiers. The absence of conventional transformers and coupling capacitors results in exceptionally high performance characteristics particularly with respect to isolation between outputs and system noise and distortion.
IC balanced circuits
Selectable gain
Twelve 600 ohm balanced outputs are provided directly at the module output connector, exhibiting 7Odb of isolation between circuits across the entire audio spectrum External resistive splitter networks are eliminated. Wiring is made directly to the MF6 frame receptacle
70db isolated between outputs 20hz 20Khz
A preset front panel gain selector switch and a #6db vernier potentiometer adapt the M612 to operate from unity gain to +38db, sufficient gain to bridge 30dbm audio source and provide twelve +8dbm outputs_ Balanced test jacks permit examination of both the input and output signals_ Other front panel facilities include a power switch, neon power indicator, ac primary and dc fuses
20K bridging input
The modest size of 3.4W X 3.4H X 13.7D allows five M612 distribution amplifiers to be accommodated in one standard rack mounting (19W X 3.5H) MF6 equipment frame.
Low absolute output noise
