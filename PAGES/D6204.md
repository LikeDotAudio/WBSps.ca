## What it is

The D6204A turns one 75 Ω AES3 input into two balanced stereo analog pairs and four AES outputs (Ross DAC-8416 / Cobalt 9262). This model runs the V200 AVR firmware and the D6201A-derived FPGA, on the real ISS4 board drawn from its Gerbers. It also settles which of the duplicated #PROBLEM folders is authoritative.
