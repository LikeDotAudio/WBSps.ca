## What it is

A one-line R&D item (Peter, 2010) asking whether WBS rear modules should move to Amphenol high-density BNC. The model compares connectors per openGear slot and frame, and shows what an imperfect 75 Ω connector does to a 3G-SDI eye.

## In the simulation

- Two openGear rear slots at 1:1 scale
- BNC vs HD-BNC density per slot, frame and RU
- 3G / HD / SD eye with connector echo
- Return-loss arithmetic live
- Privacy-safe summary of the contact sheet
