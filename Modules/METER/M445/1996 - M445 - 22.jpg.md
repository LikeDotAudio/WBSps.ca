The SAl4-304 provides physical mounting for the rotary encoder; and the front panel LCD display. To minimize interconnections between the microcontroller on the main circuit board and the LCD; & serial interconnect scheme has been implemented.
A serial code generated by the microcontroller is received at Pl on 2 This code containing the sensitivity data, is decoded by Ul, a liquid crystal display driver: The MM5453 stores the received data, and latches it to the LCD display until new data is clocked in. Resistor Rl and capacitor Cl provide timing for a free running oscillator which drives the LCD backplane.
Rotary encoder  PUI, generates quadrature signal when rotated. When rotated in & clockwise direction; the output of pin 1 will lead pin 2 by 45 degrees: Pin 2 leads pin 1 when PUI is rotated anti-clockwise.
