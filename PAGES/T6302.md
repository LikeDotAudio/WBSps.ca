## What it is

Single-card rear for the D6203A A/D and D6204A D/A converters: six BNCs for AES and reference, four Phoenix blocks for balanced analog audio. ISS3 (2009|42) adds 75 Ω across the series inductors on four BNCs; ISS1 wired positions 9–10 to single-ended pins.

## In the simulation

- ISS3 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- D6203A and D6204A pinouts
- Analog levels into 600 Ω / 10 kΩ
- ISS1 → ISS2 terminal-block rewiring
- Card-side ID read
