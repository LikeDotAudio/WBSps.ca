2. The card-edge drawing names the yellow LED “CASCADE ON LED”; the PCB silk next to it says only “ON”. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/M6204 - #Master #OPENGEAR/M6204 - #Engineering/M6204-card-edge.cdr`
3. The T6305 drawing labels both BNC 8 and terminal block 9 “AES IN8”, has no AES IN6 and no SDI output. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/M6204 - #Master #OPENGEAR/M6204 - #Engineering/M6204A-T6305.cdr`
4. M6204A-T6301.cdr (2007-06-26) and the 2012 rear-module guide draw the same T6301 layout; the guide adds the T6308A. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/M6204 - #Master #OPENGEAR/M6204 - #Engineering/M6204A-T6301.cdr`
5. Inkscape’s CDR import drops the BNC drawings of the 2007 rear-module files; the archive’s LibreOffice rendering (M6204A-T6305.pdf) keeps them and is what the T6305 panel uses. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/M6204 - #Master #OPENGEAR/M6204 - #Engineering/M6204A-T6305.pdf`
