## What it is

A 1 RU “Select Series” monitor speaker: two balanced inputs, a TDA1521 power amplifier, CH 1/CH 2 level knobs, a STEREO/MONO switch and a front headset jack that mutes the speakers. Built from the 1998 ISS.3 schematic, the manual, the 2016 panel drawings and the real silks.

## In the simulation

- Real QSP-2 front and rear silkscreens on the ISS 4 / ISS.5 panel drawings
- STEREO (one knob) vs MONO (two knobs) routing
- Headset jack normals mute the speakers
- Speaker power and TDA1521 clipping
- Two schematic pages redrawn with real part names
