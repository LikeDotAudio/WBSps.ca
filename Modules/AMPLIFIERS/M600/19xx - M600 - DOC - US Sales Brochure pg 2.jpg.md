> WBS MBS L M621 20W MONITOR AMPLIFIER DESCRIPTION The M621 20 watt audio power amplifier is an ac operated, plug-in module designed for professional applications in the broadcast, motion picture and sound recording industries: The unit represents the continuing development ad expansion of the well proven WBS 600 series of 3-1/2 inch modular components and offers a wider scope of selection for monitor amplifiers from this design category. The circuit utilizes differential operational amplifiers to produce a ultra-reliable transformerless design capable of developing a minimum of 20 watts rms to drive a 8 ohm loudspeaker directly. Other load impedances (4, 16 ohms, etc ) may be accommodated with appro- priate power output interpolation. A unique protection circuit incorporated within the M621 activates instantaneously during adverse amplifier load conditions or at a pre- determined power output level. The amplifier does not sustain damage even when deliberately shorted at full power output. A combination of conditional frame strapping ad a variable front panel level control permits the M621 t0 operate over an input signal range of ~20dBm to 18dBm: The M621 presents a permanent 20k ohm impedance at the input ad is capable of operating from a balanced or unbalanced source. The unit exhibits exceedingly uniform frequency response with low noise ad distortion over the frequency range 30Hz to Z0kHZ An internal muting relay is included to provide amplifier cut-off when a external (closure to ground) command is initiated via muting logic control circuitry, etc. The modest size of 5.1W x 3.4H x 13.70 inches allows three M621 monitor amplifiers to be accommodated in one standard rack mount- ing (19W x 3.5H) MF6 equipment frame: FEATURES 2Ow rms direct output into 8 ohms Compact design mounts in standard WBS 3-1/2 inch MF6 rack frame.
