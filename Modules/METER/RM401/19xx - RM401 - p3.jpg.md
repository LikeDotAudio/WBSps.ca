VU and Power Supply Module M4Ol-3
The M401-3 accommodates the system level selector switch SW1 , the VU meter, and the centralized plus and minus 24 volt dc supply and AC and DC system fuses
The schematic drawing for the module is shown in  dwg #M40- 02-003 During program operation zero indication on the VU meter produces an output level corresponding to the indication and position of SW 1 SW 1 compensates the system for unity gain and provides for input/output levels of -16, -10 0 , +4 and +8 dbm .
Power Trans former Tl, CRL through CR4 and filter capacitors C2 and C3 produce the unregulated voltage for the system Each individual module contains dual (plus and minus) regulated supply to prevent interaction between units and to provide constant voltage over wide range of input line voltage conditions
