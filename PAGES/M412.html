The M412 multi-frequency reference oscillator is designed to be incorporated within a system and to provide selected frequencies with stable output amplitude at Iine and microphone levels Thus, the performance of an audio console r complete system may be rapidly assessed prior to operation: In addition, the reference tone provides a convenient method for multi-track alignment or set-up in the recording mode.

FEATURES: 10 selectable frequencies with balanced output 3 outputs 8dBm direct, 8dBm and ~6OdBm via audio switch The compact M412 is compatible with other Ward-Beck modular component designs for audio console and systems applications.
