M48OB INPUT MODULE
The MID-FREQUENCY cut/boost control R34 derives its signal across the output amplifier , formed by u7 Q5, 06 and its related components Depending on the setting of R34 , an in-phase or out-of- phase proportional signal is fed to the state variable band-pass filter formed by u4, u5 and its associated components_ The band-pass center, frequency is determined by C26 and C31 in conjunction with the setting of frequency control R4S/R61 . A frequency range from 400 Hz to 6400 Hz is covered _ The bandwidth of the filter may be altered by Q switch SWS _ The Q-factor is set to 5 for HIGH 0 and 1.5 for Low Q
The band-pass filter output is fed back to pin 3 of U7 , where it is added or subtracted from the main signal depending upon its phase relationship Thus, it affects a cut or boost of the audio signal in the mid-frequency region of the audio spectrum _
Both the HIGH FREQUENCY and the Low FREQUENCY sections of the equalizer provide SHELVING type of equalization with a maximum cut or boost of 10 dB at the respective corner frequencies (10 kHz and 100 Hz) The MID FREQUENCY section provides a bell-shaped peaking response curve with a maximum cut or boost of 10 dB at the selected center frequency _
With the EQUALIZER INSERT switch SW4 released the filter amplifiers are inactive and U2 and u7 pass an unequalized signal _
Output Amplifier
u7 , 05, 06 and u6, 03_ 04 with their associated components form an amplifier with an actively balanced output_ This output is sub- sequently routed to the ATTENUATOR INSERT point, appearing at module connector pins 16 & T  It is also routed to the SOLo/cuE switch SW6, the PRE/POST switch Sw7 of the AUX ou tput and the PEAK indicator circuit.
Master Assignment
The output from the mixing attenuator is connected to pins 10 8 L where it is routed to the master assignment switches SW8 and SW9 for M1 and M2 assignment respectively _ M1 and M2 outputs are designed as 30k ohm current sources to operate into zero impedance summ ing nodes _
