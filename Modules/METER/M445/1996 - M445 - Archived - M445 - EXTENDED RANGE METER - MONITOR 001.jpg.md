M445 Extended Range Meter Monitor System
General Description
Ward-Beck Systems Ltd. proudly introduces the M445 Extended Range Meter Monitor System. This next generation audio meter offers the function of a rack mounted monitor system and the flexibility of a precision portable extended range meter all in one unique package 
The meter normally measures the stereo signal in the left and right mode (L and R): To monitor phase integrity, the sum and difference (L+R, L-R) mode is selected. The circuit being measured may be bridged Or terminated by a 150 or 600 Ohm load A bbuilt-in 75 Hz high-pass filter may also be inserted into the audio metering path to eliminate low frequency components such as hum:
When it is "docked" , the M44S is a sophisticated monitoring system for plant audio levels Stereo speakers driven by integral amplifiers provide "confidence" monitoring: The rear panel audio inputs are normalled through the front panel audio jacks. When the instrument is first docked, the rechargeable ni-cad batteries are quick-charged_ Once fully rejuvenated; a trickle charge; under the supervision of powerful battery management system is maintained. This automatic charge ensures that the unit is always ready for portable measuring duties.
