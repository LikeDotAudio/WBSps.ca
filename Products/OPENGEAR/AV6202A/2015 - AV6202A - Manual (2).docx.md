Introduction
Overview
The AV6202A is a utility  analog video distribution amplifier with a differential input designed for broadcast use.  It can provide up to eight outputs when used with a single card rear module and up to four outputs when used with a double card rear module in a OG3-FR-C frame. The AV6202A can be addressed through Dashboard to report card status. The AV6202A is housed in the openGear OG3-FR-C series frames.

Unpacking
Unpack each AV6202A you received from the shipping container, and check the contents against the packing list to ensure that all items are included.  If any items are missing or damaged, contact your sales representative or Ward-Beck Systems Inc. directly.
Rear Module Options 
There are two rear module options available for the AV6202A Utility Analog Video distribution amplifier, that are both used with the 20-slot OG3-FR-C frame. The T6301A rear module occupies two slots and provides I/O for one AV6202A card.  For higher density applications the T6401A occupies two slots and provides I/O for two AV6202A cards.  All connections are made on BNC type 75 Ohm video connectors

Figure 2. Rear Modules

Board Installation
Use the following procedure to install the AV6202A in an openGear OG3-FR-C Series frame.
Refer to the User Manual of the openGear OG3-FR-C series frame to ensure that the frame is properly installed according to instructions.  
When using the AV6202A with the T6301A for maximum I/O please note that the even numbered slots are to be used.   Plug the AV6202A modules into slots 2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16,18, and 20 for a maximum of 10 cards.  Slot 1 is the left most slot as you look into the frame from the front.
When using the AV6202A with the T6401A for maximum density you may insert the card into any slot for up to 20 cards per frame.
After selecting the desired frame installation slot, hold the AV6202A card by the edges and carefully align the card edges with the slots in the frame.  Then fully insert the card into the frame until the rear connection plugs are properly seated on the midplane and rear module.
This completes the procedure for installing the AV6202A in an openGear OG3-FR-C Series frame.
