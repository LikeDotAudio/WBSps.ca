## What it is

The rack frame (sold as the MF251) that holds one or two M8246 supplies for the R2K console, plus six ancillary slots for 8200-series DAs. Two AC inlets and fuses on the rear; a blank front for the empty supply bay. The archive copy is the 2012–2013 metalwork redraw set.

## In the simulation

- Rear housing converted from MF250M5, to scale
- Slots and bays placed from MF251.PCB
- Dual AC feed, fuses and hot-pull redundancy
- Outer brackets (MF250M9) and blank panel
- Template ghosts and a wrong-part layout file
