Introduction
Overview
The M6203A-3G is a one input eight output SD/HD/3G SDI Distribution Amplifier designed for broadcast use. It features cable equalization and reclocking techniques that enable the unit to reliably recover the incoming digital audio signal. M6203A-3G can receive and distribute digital video signals, over coaxial cable (BELDEN 8281 or equivalent). All inputs and outputs are located on the T6201A, T6301A, or T6401A rear modules.  The M6203A is recommended for cable input/output up to 300 meters with standard definition (270M), 100 meters with high definition (1.5G), and 50 meters using 3G. The M6203A-3G is housed in the openGear OG3-FR-C series frames.

Static Discharge
Whenever handling the M6203A and other related equipment, please observe all static discharge precautions as described in the following note:

Unpacking
Unpack each M6203A you received from the shipping container, and check the contents against the packing list to ensure that all items are included.  If any items are missing or damaged, contact your sales representative or Ward-Beck Systems Inc. directly.
Rear Module Options 
There are two rear module options available for the M6203A-3G HD/SD-SDI 3G Reclocked Distribution Amplifier. On the 20-slot OG3-FR-C frame the T6301A-3G rear module occupies two slots and provides I/O for one M6203A-3G card.  For higher density applications the  T6401A rear module occupies two slots and provides I/O for two M6203A-3G cards.  All connections are made on BNC type 75 Ohm video connectors.

Figure 2. Rear Module 
Board Installation
Use the following procedure to install the M6203A-3G in an openGear DFR-8300 Series frame.
Refer to the User Manual of the openGear OG3-FR-C series frame to ensure that the frame is properly installed according to instructions.
When using the M6203A-3G with the OG3-FR-C frame, please note that the even numbered slots are to be used.   Plug the M6203A-3G modules into slots 2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16,18, and 20 for a maximum of 10 cards.  Slot 1 is the left most slot as you look into the frame from the front.
When using the M6203A-3G for highest density ( 20 1 x 4 DA) any slot may be used..
After selecting the desired frame installation slot, hold the M6203A-3G card by the edges and carefully align the card edges with the slots in the frame.  Then fully insert the card into the frame until the rear connection plugs are properly seated on the midplane and on the rear module.

This completes the procedure for installing the M6203A-3G in an openGear OG3-FR-C Series frame.
