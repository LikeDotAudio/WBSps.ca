AIP8-2A 

A Brief Overview

The AIP8-2A is a two rack-unit aural and visual monitor designed to display multi-channel pre-and post-processed audio signals.  Eight PPM/VU LED bargraphs display signal activity, while a sophisticated four speaker system allows operators to listen, with great clarity, to the signal being monitored.

Inputs:

The AIP8-2A accepts AES or embedded audio signals.  When AES is selected, the two digital audio inputs are connected to the rear panel unbalanced BNC connectors.  The stereo input (ST) is connected to IN1 on the rear panel BNC connector and the descriptive video input (DV) is connected IN2 on the rear panel BNC connector.  The ST input will be metered on METER #1, looking from left to right.  The DV input will be metered on METER #2.

When SDI is selected, the AIP8-2A will monitor and meter the assigned ST and DV embedded AES signals.  The ST and DV inputs must be PCM.  To assign the ST and DV input to one of the eight possible embedded AES signals, press and hold the SDI button for approximately 5 seconds.  SD Setup will be displayed in the screen along with the assigned ST input (AES 1).  To change the assigned ST input turn the VOLUME knob clockwise or counter-clockwise until the desired AES input is displayed.  Once the desired AES signal is displayed turn the ST knob clockwise and the assigned DV input will be displayed.  The same process is used to change the assigned ST input in used to change the assigned DV input.  Once the ST and DV inputs have been change to the desired assignment turn the ST knob clockwise once again and EXIT will be displayed.  Press the ST knob and you will return to normal operation.  If no options have been modified at any time during the SDI Setup the AIP8-2A will time-out and return to normal operation.


Outputs:

The unit provides two copies of the ST and DV output AES signals.  The front panel ST and DV knobs allow for +/-15 dB gain to the respective ST and DV outputs.  The ST outputs are on OUT 1 and 3.  The DV outputs are on OUT 2 and 4.  The AES signals are provided as balanced or unbalanced signals on Phoenix™ pluggable terminals or BNCs respectively.  

The unit will provide a re-clocked MD-SDI output of the input signal on the BNC connector labelled SDI OUT.  An additional ST and DV pre-processed AES outputs will be available on the BNC connectors labelled AES1 and AES2 respectively.

Field configuration

Using a combination of the eight segment LED dot-matrix readout, the eight lighted pushbuttons and the two rotary shaft encoders with integral pushbutton switches, Ward-Beck has developed an intuitive scheme for operators to configure the AIP8-2A to address their monitoring needs.  The unit is capable of monitoring the pre-processed ST and DV inputs in addition to the post-processed ST and DV outputs. Pressing the front panel ST or DV buttons located in the IN section will route the respective input to the speakers and display the phase relationship between the LEFT and RIGHT channels on the horizontal phase meter located on the bottom LED meter display.  Likewise, pressing the front panel ST or DV buttons located in the OUT section will route the respective output to the speakers and the horizontal phase meter.  Turn the VOLUME knob to adjust the speaker level.  Pressing the PHASE button will invert the RIGHT channel of the ST input.

It is possible to route only the LEFT or the RIGHT channels of the ST input to the ST output.  Press the ST knob and the assigned source for the ST output will be displayed.  Turn the ST knob clockwise or counter-clockwise to change the ST output source to any of the following: ST LEFT, ST RIGHT or ST L/R.  The resulting configuration will be metered on the ST output meter (looking from left to right:  METER #3).  As with the ST input, it is possible to assign the source for the DV output.  Press the DV knob and the assigned source for the DV output will be displayed.  Turn the DV knob clockwise or counter-clockwise to change the DV output source to any of the following:  DV LEFT, DV RIGHT, DV L/R or ST IN.  The ST IN is a mono mix of the ST output (ST MONO will be displayed during normal operation).  The resulting configuration will be metered on the DV output meter (looking from left to right:  METER #4).  To exit either ST or DV assignment mode, press the respective knob at any time to save the selection and return to normal operation.  If no options have been modified at any time during the assignment mode the AIP8-2A will time-out and return to normal operation.
