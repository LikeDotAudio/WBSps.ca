M648 Manual
Check the module for correct assembly with special attention given to the following:
a)
Check that the "high" side of the AC Line input is connected between P1-7 and the end terminal of F1 (not the front ring)
b )
Check that filter capacitors C2 and C3 are wired for the correct polarity. The positive terminal should connect to the rectifier cathode 
c)
Confirm the correct connections to series pass transistor Q1 _

b )

a)
