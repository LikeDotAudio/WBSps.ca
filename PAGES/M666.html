Description — 287 words · 19xx - M666 - wbsps - promo - m666 100w power amp - front.jpg, page 1

The M666 100 watt audio power amplifier is an ac operated, plug-in module designed for professional applications in the broadcast, motion picture and sound recording industries. The unit represents the continuing development and expansion of the well proven WBS 600 series of modular components and offers a wider scope of selection for monitor amplifiers from this design category. The circuit utilizes differential operational amplifiers to produce an ultra- reliable transformerless design capable of developing a minimum of 100 watts rms to drive an 8 ohm loudspeaker directly Other Ioad impedances (4, 16 ohms, etc:) may be accommodated with appropriate power output inter- polation.

A unique protection circuit incorporated within the M666 activates instantan- eousiy during adverse amplifier load conditions or at a predetermined power output level.
