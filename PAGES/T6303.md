## What it is

Single-card rear with ten 3-pin Phoenix blocks for balanced audio: two single-ended pairs on the BNC1/BNC2 pins and eight AES pairs. ISS1 (2006|39) had the inputs on the BNC9/BNC10 pins; ISS2 moved them to the top. ISS4 (2012|45) is drawn from its Gerbers.
