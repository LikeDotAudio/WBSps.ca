The active circuitry may be inspected by removing the instrument back plate Disconnect the ac line cord and remove the four 4-40 screws that secure the back plate to the meter case Exercise care when removing the back plate in order to prevent damage to the ac wires connecting from the plate receptacle to the instrument power supply.
The  component board may be moved outward for inspection by removing the two nuts which secure the PC board to the meter terminals In thi manner access may be gained to the foil side of the PC board

If fault is suspected, measuring the dc voltage present at the transistor electrodes may assist to determine the cause When comparing the measurements listed in the voltage chart consideration should be given to the fact that, for direct coupled sections, fault in one circuit may cause abnormal conditions in other circuit areas within the same section
If it is necessary to interchange component to determine the cause of fault, first remove the solder from t he printed circuit foil fillets associated with the suspected defective component To do this, light soldering iron should be used (25 watts) in combination with an inexpensive vacuum action type hand desoldering tool The desoldering tool is designed for component reworking and comprises teflon tip and cylinder equipped with spring plunger which, when loaded and released, creates high impulsive vacuum to draw the molten solder off from the printed circuit board and into the cylinder
