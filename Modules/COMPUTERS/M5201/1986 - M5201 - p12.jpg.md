M5201 MACHINE CONTROL MODULE
Option M5201/1 Display_Facility
When WBS R58024 Stereo Routing Switcher is incorporated with the ST consoles; alphanumeric displays which indicate routing switcher selections may be installed inline with the associated console mixing channels.
The drive and character generation for the displays is integral to the M5201 Machine Control Module _ Typically up to twelve sets of displays may be driven
Connector P9 prov ides the required serial data and clocking drives for the twelve sets of displays_ The drive signals are divided into three sets of four displays each . The data interconnect to the externa 5A91-039 display assembly is via single pair shielded cable (up to fifteen feet in length) comprising data/clock Tine, column clock/reset Tine, and common return Mine.
