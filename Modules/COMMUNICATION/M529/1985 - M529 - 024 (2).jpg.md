u2 amplifies the output of U1 and feeds the audio signal to the full-wave rectifier CRI/CR2 The developed error voltage is stored on capacitors C8 and C9, which in turn is applied to Q1's gate, controlling its resistance . `
AGC control R24 sets the balance between the oscillator positive and negative feedback. With the AGC function disabled R24 is adjusted So that the oscillator output level is just at the point of clipping AGC level control R4o is then set to optimize the output Level versus frequency selection. R36 is adjusted with the oscillator selected to 30 Hz for symmetrical full-wave rectification at CRI/CR2 cathodes_
The oscillator output is coupled through C14 to the output level trim control R42 R42 is accessible through the front panel of the M529 module for fine adjustment of the output level
