04 / Chassis Construction & Layout Conventions

Industrial design patterns for MF-6B rack integration

1. Consistent Left-Edge Telemetry

Every module reserves the left vertical axis (x = 32–36px) for the tri-color status indicator stack, enabling instant visual fault scanning across densely populated 3½" broadcast frames.

● OVLD (Red) ● NORM (Green) ● DC (Amber)
Top-to-bottom priority: Fault → Signal → Rail

2. Self-Powered RF Enclosure

Fully enclosed metal housings pair slotted top ventilation louvers with custom top and bottom extruded aluminum lips to deliver maximum RF immunity in high-density studio environments.

Individual slow-blow (SB) bayonet fuse anchored bottom-right

3. Recessed Calibration Controls

All gain, trim, and remote sensitivity adjustments utilize screwdriver-slotted multi-turn trim pots rather than protruding rotary knobs, preventing accidental level shifts during live operation.
