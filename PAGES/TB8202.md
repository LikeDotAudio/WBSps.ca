## What it is

Rear termination for the M8202 remote-sensitivity analog DA: balanced input, eight 60 Ω splits and the REM IN terminals for a 10 k remote pot. Seven of the eight splits share one card driver through per-leg build-out resistors on the module; OUT 4 is wired straight to a second card driver.
