## What it is

The 2013 project to take at least 4 inches out of the AMS16 and share metalwork across the 2RU units. The real bottom-pan hole patterns are drawn to scale: cut the 12-inch pan and see what has to move, then compare it with the 8-inch AMS16-8 pan the project produced and the RD68 prototype hole schedule.

## In the simulation

- AMS16A2, AMS16-8 and XTM4 pans to scale from the drawings
- Interactive cut: which standoffs and screws must move
- Prototype hole schedule matches the issued 8-inch pan
- Shared RLM24 side plate = the “standard metal work”
- Project dates from the DWG files
