<details><summary>Installation — 266 words · 1968 - M404 - P4.jpg, page 1</summary><p>The M404 is designed to mount directly on mixing console front panel The dimensions are such that the unit may be mounted in line with standard straight line mixing attenuator associated with each mixing channel cutout 1.56w X 6L and two tapped holes Will be required as shown in drawing 402-08-001 _ The escutcheon of the M404 will overlap the cutout and holes External connections are made to the 14 pin female ma ting connector in accordance with drawing 402-09-001 and Table 1 For optimum results, use insulated, shielded twisted pairs for both input and output wiring .</p><p>The optional external insert switch should be wired with shielded twisted pairs and located not more than 24 inches from the module Dress all audio cables apart Erom ac and power supply Wiring_ Ground shields at one end only and use separate ground return wire to the system common ground for the power supply and shields as shown in the System Wiring Diagram 404-09-002 _ The metal frame of the M404 equalizer should be bonced to the system ground _ In this case it is permissible to loop through each unit with common ground wire connected via pin &quot;N&quot; When used in system, such as an audio mixing console, the M404 equalizer should be operated from separate power supply than the ones used to provide dc power to program amplifiers, cue amplifiers or other similar units operating at high output levels The latter units produce large current swings and the possibility of instability, noise or crosstalk should be avoided in systems which combine high gain and wide-band amplifiers</p></details>
- source: [1968 - M404 - P4.jpg](../Modules/FILTERS/M404/1968%20-%20M404%20-%20P4.jpg), p. 1

### Operation (1)

<details><summary>Controls — 135 words · 1968 - M404 - P6.jpg, page 1</summary><p>The M404 has four operating controls These transient-free controls may be pre-set or adjusted during the operation of the unit_ The selectable frequencies associated with the HF and LF cut controls may be used independently or cumu latively to achieve multiple or combined effects, depending on the position of the HI-LO/Lo-Lo selector switch_ HF CUT X 1K Control This control produces rapid roll-off commencing at the high frequency audio spectrum to achieve low-pass filter characteristics The frequency designations indicated on the photo-etched escutcheon  must be converted X IK (1000) to correspond to the operating frequency_ The nomenclature applies to the ~3db point on the attenuation curve E.G.</p><p>12=12,000 hz , 5=5,000 hz and 5=500 hz , etc _ In the OFF position the equalizer exhibits flat frequency response characteristics within 0 . 5db to 20Khz</p></details>
