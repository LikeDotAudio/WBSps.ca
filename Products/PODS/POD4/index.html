Operation (2)

Operation — 165 words · 1996 - POD4 - POD4 - TRY.wpd, page 1

FROM 110 VOLTS ac:

■ Two jumpers must be present, one connection Y1 to Y2, the other connection Y3 and Y4. This corresponds to JP1 and JP3 being in place.

FOR

FROM 220 VOLTS ac:

■ One jumper must be present, connecting Y2 and Y3. This corresponds to JP2 being in place.

Gain(bridging): +64dB. Frequency Response: ±0.2 dB, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Noise: -126dB EIN. Total Harmonic Distortion: Less than 0.25%, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Dynamic Range: 88dB. Input Impedance: 1500 ohms or greater. Source Impedance: 150 ohms or less. Common Mode Rejection: Greater than 60 dB at 60 Hz. Power Requirement: 110 or 220 volts ac, 60 or 50 Hz; 5 Watts. Ambient Temperature: 0o to 50o Celsius. Relative Humidity: 20 % to 90 %, non-condensing. Dimensions: 8.69" wide x 1.73" high x 7.0" deep (221 mm x 44 mm x 178 mm).
