HI-LO PASS EQUALIZER MODEL M404
GENERAL: The M404 is compact, active-lossless equalizer module designed for use with professional audio mixing consoles in the sound recording and broadcast industries The unit is plug-in and designed to mount directly on the console control panel in line with standard straight line mixing attenuator
Individual controls provide ten cut-off frequencies for the high and low frequency spectrum  The rate of attenuation is 18db octave third control selects either the high or low frequency function individually or centre position which enables both frequency sections to be employed cumulatively to produce range of combined pass-band effects An In-Out" switch is provided on concentric selector for instant equalized unequalized program comparison _
Low Frequency cut (high-pass) is effective at the following 3db points: OFF , 7Ohz , 100hz , 150hz , 250hz , 500hz , 1Khz , 2Khz_ 3Khz , 4Khz and 5Khz High Frequency cut {low-pass) is effective at the following 3db points : 5oonz , 1Khz , 2Khz , 3Khz , 4Khz , SKhz, 6Khz , 8Khz , 10Khz , 12Khz and OFF
The M404 has 35db gain and is entirely balanced utilizing an electrostatically/magnetically shielded input transformer and solid-state actively balanced output circuit Each equalizing characteristic is achieved by selecting only resistive elements in conjunction with unique FET high impedance circuit The use of integrated circuits and FET devices is responsible for the many features offered and the excellent performance characteristics in this compact des ign
A low noise pre-amplifier and an output amplifier with line level capability permit the M404 to be used as combination equalizer /mix amplifier or as unity gain (external resistor network) equalizer for insertion at any point within an audio system.
```
