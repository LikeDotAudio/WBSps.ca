## What it is

The cable-equalizing, hum-clamping version of the M8251 video DA. It keeps the M8251’s EL2099 output stage and eight 75 Ω outputs and adds an OPA650 equaliser and an LM1881-keyed back-porch clamp. Laid out in Tango in Nov 1997|30; the OrCAD sheet titled “ANALOG VIDEO DA — EQULIZING/CLAMPING” was revised in 2001|34.

## In the simulation

- Board drawn from the real M8252.PCB Tango layout
- Cable EQ: dial out up to 300 m of loss at 3.58 MHz
- LM1881 back-porch clamp kills 60 Hz hum, line by line
- BNC1 / BNC3 rear modules and the looping input
- Findings: M8251 title block and 12 stray frame connectors left in the files
