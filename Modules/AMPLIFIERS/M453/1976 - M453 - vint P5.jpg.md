M 453
CIRCUIT DESCRIPTION
The M453 3 watt cue amplifier provides three 20K ohm bridging inputs each of which is isolated from one another In addition , sum input is provided which is intended to receive the cue feeds from the M441 input modules
21 and 22 form an actively balanced input which provides the necessary longitudinal balance characteristics . The input amplifier is capable of handling an input level up to +l8dBn without over load.
The amplifier level control is located after the preamplifier This control may be extended via pins and 8 to permit the adjustment of the amplifier output level up to distance of 10 feet This external control operates in series with the control located on the module. For this external control type of operation, the module level control should remain in the full clockwise position_
23 forms the driver amplifier for the output power transistor 03 through Q6 . The output signal is coupled through to pin S . CR3 to CR6 form the overdrive protection circuit to prevent excessive power dissipation if the amplifier is inadvertently shorted while signal is present .
Q1 and Q2 form regulator circuit which provides the bias voltage (approximately 1OV to pin 3 of z1 , 22 and 23)
The amplifier will operate from nominal 24 to 36 V dc unregulated supply which has the negative side on the common terminal The unregulated dc voltage i5 applied to pin X and fed through fuse Fl and polarity reversing protection diode CRl .
