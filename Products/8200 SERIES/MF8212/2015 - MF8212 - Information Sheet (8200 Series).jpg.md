WBS
Ward-Beck Systems Inc. First By Design
MF8212 Rackmount Frame For 8200 series 12 card capacity
POWER SUPPLY "0824 ASP
Product Description The MF8212 frame is part of Ward-Beck's 8200 Series of cards and frames. It is the only silent frame in the broadcast industry, making it perfect for production studios ad mobile trucks. It can house Up to twelve cards and two power supplies, and occupies two vertical units of rack space: Headers for each amplifier slot are accessible from the rear of the frame Terminal interface assemblies for the appropriate amplifier card plug into these headers to allow for input and output connections_ When distributing signals on BNC terminations_ the MF8212 frame provides space for terminating input and six outputs per card:
As an option the MF8212 frame can accept an AES and video reference input: These two inputs are on 75 Ohm BNC connectors,; located on the optional MBZREF rear assembly: The hinged, quick release front panel facilitates rapid access controls and tallies located the front edge of the amplifier cards. During installation and setup, the panels may be easily removed to gain access to cards, their controls and tallies
Power supply redundancy in the MF8212 frame is carried through the primary mains to the separate switchmode supplies (M8245A power supply modules). Two separately fused AC power cords are provided. The switchmode supplies operation on 115V to 230V 50 or 60Hz without any special setup and require 130 VA maximum.
SPecifications GENERAL Power Requirements; 110/230v 50/60Hz 130v^ Weight (frame only: 16 Ibs. (7,65kg) Dimensions wide * 3.5 in high * 13.25 in, deep (482mm *89mm *336mm) Card Capacity; 12 8200 Series Cards Power supply required: M8245A power supply module (two required for redundancy)
