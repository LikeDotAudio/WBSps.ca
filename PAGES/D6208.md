## What it is

Unreleased openGear design: eight AES inputs, each with a CS8416 receiver and AD1895 SRC, an EP2C35 FPGA, a CAT-559 Dolby decoder module and video genlock. Only the issue-1 P-CAD files exist; the model is built from the netlist and its ECO log, with no firmware.
