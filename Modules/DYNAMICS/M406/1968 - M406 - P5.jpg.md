Set the GRNU switch to the GR position and the IN/OUT switch to the "in position.
To permit the M406 to operate correctly with both expansion and compression, it should operate about a point of reference up the slope of compression. Typically, this means at the mid point or an amount equal to the desired expansion. The M406 has a 25db compression range; therefore the mid point is 12db from the threshold of compression. This will permit 12db additional compression with 12db Of expansion. For a 2/1 compression ratio the reference point occurs with gain reduction reading of 6db, for 3/1 8db for 5/1 9.6db and for 10/1 10.8db.
Adjust the THRESHOLD control until the desired gain reduction is indicated by a downward deflection of the meter pointer measured on the green GR scale
Set the RECOVERY switch to produce the most acceptable results for each particular application_ The recovery time will determine the rate that the lower program material will expand to the appropriate position on the compression slope_ Position achieves the most rapid recovery and position 5 produces the longest time constant_ It is recommended that for the compression mode RECOVERY 3, 4 and 5 positions be used , with the' longest time constant (pos.5) reserved for program material which contains higher level of background noise
In the limit mode, L and 10/1 the M4O6 may be applied to prevent overmodulation. For this application only a minimal amount of gain reduction should be use d, less than 5 or 6db _ With the M406 adjusted to limit the upper peaks of the program content and the GR meter indicating less than Sdb, the RECOVERY switch may be selected to positions or 2 thus effecting more rapid recovery time.
