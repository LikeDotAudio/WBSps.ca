## What it is

Single-card rear for the M6205 embedded audio processor: seven BNCs and three Phoenix blocks (two GPI/Os, metadata). The PNP file loads the allocated 1K91; the netlist, edited after the Gerbers, says 5K36 and 4n7.

## In the simulation

- T6307 ISS1 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- Routes traced through 4 copper layers
- M6205 pin-out
- Netlist edited after production
- Different layout from the T6304
