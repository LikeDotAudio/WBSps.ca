VMS33
VIDEO AND AUDIO
MONITORING SYSTEM

Just one of many products in the SELECT SERIES line of high quality and high performance broadcast products from WARD-BECK SYSTEMS.

The VMS33 offers a compact solution for audio and video monitoring.   Combining the features of three high resolution 16:9 LCD displays, three 28 segment stereo VU/Peak Program  meters and three 5 segment correlation meters in a three rack unit panel, it is the most versatile audio and video monitoring system available.

The VMS33 comes standard with three NTSC video, three stereo analog and three AES  inputs .  PAL displays and digital video inputs are available options. 

Features include sync sensing, auto shut off and manual shut off to prolong LCD life.  The unit is equipped with an internal power supply and a fused IEC power disconnect and operates with AC power ranging from 90 to 230 Volts at 60 or 50 Hz. .

The unique telescoping and tilting front panel affords the user an unobstructed view in virtually any situation.  The VMS33 is ideal for equipment rack and operating desk locations.

The video screens in the VMS33  have a typical lifetime of 15,000 hours. The VMS33 is equipped with 2 automatic features and 1 manual feature designed to preserve the life of the screens.

1.  Auto Synch Detect- the video screen will automatically be powered down if no video synch is detected.

2.  Auto Off Mode- all 3 video screens will be turned off after a preset time period. The video off pin on the rear panel DB-9 (pin 1, pin 6 is GND) acts as an on/off control, that is, a closure to ground turns all 3 screens off and overrides the Auto Off.  The front panel push button will act as an alternate action on/off control will also override the Auto Off.  Once the video returns the timer also resets.

3.  Manual Off Mode- when Auto Off Mode is disabled, the video off pin on the rear panel DB-9 (pin 1, pin 6 is GND) acts as an off control, that is, a closure to ground turns all 3 screens off.  The front panel push button will act as an alternate action on/off control. The front panel push button overrides the rear panel control.
