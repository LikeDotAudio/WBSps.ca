Operation (1)

Operation — 261 words · 1995 - POD1 - POD1 - POD1MAN.wp, page 1

FROM 110 VOLTS ac:

■ Two jumpers must be present, one connection Y1 to Y2, the other connection Y3 and Y4. This corresponds to J2 and J3 being in place.

FOR

FROM 220 VOLTS ac:

■ One jumper must be present, connecting Y2 and Y3. This corresponds to J1 being in place.

Power Requirement: 110 or 220 volts ac, 60 or 50 Hz; 5 Watts. Ambient Temperature: 0o to 50o Celsius. Relative Humidity: 20 % to 90 %, non-condensing. Dimensions: 8.69" wide x 1.73" high x 7.00" deep (221 mm x 44 mm x 178 mm).

Balanced to Unbalanced Conversion

Sensitivity: 0 to +8 dBu for -10 dBu output. Frequency Response: ±0.2 dB, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Noise: Greater than 70 dB below -10 dBu output (20 Hz to 30 kHz). Total Harmonic Distortion: Less than 0.02%, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Dynamic Range: Greater than 100 dB. Input Impedance: 30 000 ohms or greater, balanced. Source Impedance: 600 ohms or less, balanced. Output Impedance: Less than 47 000 ohms, unbalanced. Load Impedance: 5000 ohms or greater, unbalanced.

Unbalanced to Balanced Conversion

Sensitivity: -10 dBu input for 0 dBu to +8 dBu output. Frequency Response: ±0.2 dB, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Noise: Greater than 85 dB below 0 dBu output (20 Hz to 30 kHz). Total Harmonic Distortion: Less than 0.02%, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Dynamic Range: Greater than 110 dB. Input Impedance: 47 000 ohms or greater, unbalanced. Source Impedance: 5000 ohms or less, unbalanced. Output Impedance: 60 ohms, balanced. Load Impedance: 600 ohms or greater, balanced
