## What it is

Single-card rear for the D6207A Dolby E / AC-3 decoder: eight BNCs for AES in and out, two Phoenix blocks (reserved and metadata). The netlist carries 5n6/3n9/3n3 inductors; the later pick-and-place file loads 4n7 everywhere.
