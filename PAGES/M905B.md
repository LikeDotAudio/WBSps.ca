GENERAL</p><p>M905B STUDIO MONITOR MODULE</p><p>The M905B Studio Monitor Module provides the monitoring and talkback facility for two remote studios within the Renaissance Radio Console system.  Typically these would be used for a news booth and an announce booth with two headphone monitoring positions (in addition to a monitor speaker output).</p><p>The M905B is capable of selecting one of 10 stereo line level sources for monitoring in the remote studios.  All audio switching is realized within the module for muting and talkback using solid-state active switching (no relays are employed).  The source selection is tallied with an LED indicator which is integral to the pushbutton.</p><p>Each of the two studios controllable from the M905B has three level controls associated with it.  There is a control for the monitor speaker feed (Monitor Level), the announcer headset level (Announcer), and the guest headset level (Guest).</p><p>The Monitor Level can be muted by using the console muting buss structure.  Each studio is individually assignable to one of the three muting busses within the console.  Muting does not effect the Announce or Guest Headset feeds.  A front panel LED indicates when the associated studio has enabled the mute buss in the console.</p><p>An input is provided on the M905B for sending talkback audio to a selected studio (via a momentary pushbutton).  The tally lamp within the talkback button is uncommited and may be used for externally signalling the console operator from the remote location.  Enabling the talkback will shift a mono sum of the selected source to the left speaker of the studio (This level may be diminished with internal controls).  Talkback is then placed on the right channel of the monitor feed.</p><p>The Announcer may also have the talkback audio assigned to his headset in the same fashion as the monitor speakers.  Printed circuit board links provide this feature.</p><p>In all cases, the Guest Headset feed is unaffected by either muting or talkback operations.</p><p>The M905B may be used in place of an M905A monitor module.  However, the cue speaker will not function.  The Studio 1 Monitor and Announcer controls will effect the Control Room Monitor speakers and the console Headset Level respectively.  Please note that in this configuration, it will not be possible to drive low impedance headsets.</p><p>All outputs are actively balanced and are configured for a nominal output level of 0 dBu.</p><p>The M905B is packaged in the standard Renaissance modular package, 1.50&quot; wide x 16.2&quot; high x 6.6&quot; deep.</p><p>The module derives it&#x27;s power from the console system ±24 volts.
