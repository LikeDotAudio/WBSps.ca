## What it is

First-generation Ward-Beck openGear rear module: ten BNCs, each through a 4n7 series inductor to the standard MEC1 pins, with isolated shells on positions 1, 2, 9 and 10. Passive; the card reads its type from two ID resistors (3K57 / 30K9).

## In the simulation

- Real ISS1 Gerbers drawn at 1:1
- Route every BNC to its P101 pin
- Mate with D6201A, M6202A or AV6201
