Dual Gated Attenuator M401-2
The circuit schematic is shown in drawing B401-02-002 The operation of the dual voltage regulators in this module is identical to the M4O1-1 module. Two audio gates comprising switched FET devices Q9 and Q12 with associated control amplifiers and status indicators are accommodated on one plug-in printed circuit card module.
Each FET gate forms an "L" attenuator with the series drain resistor The audio signal path is via input pin "F through two solid-state gate circuits and out Via pin "H Q9, Q10 and Q11 form the first gate and Q12, Q13 and Q14 form the second gate The NPN-PNP configurations following each gate produce high input impedance wide dynamic range isolation amplifier With Q9 or Q12 switched "offu high impedance is presented to the series attenuator resistors R41 and R48 _ With Q9 or Q12 switched "on" an amount Of attenuation equal to the ratio of R41 to R43 and R48 to R5O is introduced
Since each FET gate is activated by an independent control amplifier with individual threshold adjustment sequential or random noise attenuation pattern may be established. The unit is factory shipped with each gate adjusted to produce 2db of attenuation. Hence,with three dual gated attenuator modules 2db Of noise suppression may be achieved and the thresholds may be set to operate simultaneously or in a sequence which produces the most indiscernable effect.
The audio signal causing gate Q9 to activate is introduced to the control amplifier Via pin 2. Q1 is an emitter follower which acts as a buffer between the audio sample and the control amplifier. R3 , R4 and C1 form high pass filter and Rs_ R6 and C2 form low pass filter 21 is & high gain integrated circuit audio amplifier transformer coupled to rectifiers CR1 through CR4.
