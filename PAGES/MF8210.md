## Footnotes

1. Each silk sheet holds two inks: the legend in CorelDRAW rich black (#1B1918) and the mechanical outline in pure black (#000000). — `#MF8200 #SERIES/D8210 & D8212 - #Master #MF82/D8210 & D8212 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/MF8212RM2-SILK(CURVE).cdr`
2. The two rear-cover silks swap REF IN and REF OUT: NOV.06/15 reads REF OUT, REF IN; JUN.22/16 reads REF IN, REF OUT. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/D8210 & D8212 - #Master #MF82/D8210 & D8212 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/MF8212RM2-SILK (CURVE).cdr`
3. The PSU door has 14 vent slots (“13 PITCHES” on the drawing), and its PS2 lamp hole sits at 59 % of the width, not mirrored to PS1. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/D8210 & D8212 - #Master #MF82/D8210 & D8212 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/82-077-SILK (DOOR CURVE).cdr`
4. The DA door silk carries only the WBS mark and two screw targets; the card slots have no legends. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/D8210 & D8212 - #Master #MF82/D8210 & D8212 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/8212-020-SILK (CURVE) .cdr`
5. Each silk exists twice, as live text and as a (CURVE) copy; the MF8212RM2 curve version is stored under two spellings, “SILK(CURVE)” (JUN.22/16) and “SILK (CURVE)” (NOV.06/15), which are different issues. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/D8210 & D8212 - #Master #MF82/D8210 & D8212 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016`
6. The same drawing numbers exist as older issues in MFXX (DA FRAME) #PROBLEM; only MF82SILK.CDR and one other file are byte-identical. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/MFXX (DA FRAME) - #Engineering #PROBLEM #MF82`
