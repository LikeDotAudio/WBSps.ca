### Page 1 (OCR)

```text
CIRCUIT DESCRIPTION: Refer to schematic diagram 402-02-001 The M402 has three main active circuit sections low noise preamplifier, equalizer networks and output amplifier The equalizer section has Unity gain and is switched in and out between the preamplifier and output amplifier via switch SW6 _
With SW6 selected to the "out" position the preamplifier is connected directly to the output amplifier and the equalizer functions as conventional amplifier with flat response characteristics _
The input signal is applied to the preamplifier via the 14 pin connector and transformer T1 The input may be connected on the mating plug to operate from 150 or 600 ohm source impedances. The Signal is applied to common emitter amplifier_ Q1 base Via T1 secondary. Q1 is direct coupled to emitter follower Q2. R4 and R5 set the gain of the preamplifier and introduce negative feedback through T1 secondary
C5 is the preamplifier output coupling capacitor and connects to terminal B of the equalizer 14 pin connector SW6 is interconnected between the preamplifier and output amplifier Via terminals E and J This arrangement allows the use of a remote or external insert switch which may be connected using shielded cable of Up to 24 inches in length _ Thus, the equalizer insert function may be grouped with other illuminated assignment pushbuttons incorporated within custom mixing consoles_
Terminal M is the input to the integrated circuit balanced output amplifier R9O through R93 and C34 through C36 form compensation network to limit the response outside the audio spectrum C37 is the coupling capacitor to the non-inverting and inverting input to 23 and Z4 respectively.
C48 and C49 couple the actively balanced audio output from Z3 and 24 and present a +24dbm capability output Via terminals P and R balanced to ground
The equalizer section is interposed Via SW6. Capacitor C6 couples the signal from SW6 to the input of the equalizer section. C6 in conjunction with resistors R1?2 through R17 form 6db/octave high pass filter Attenuation i5 achieved in 8db steps producing 3,6,9,12 and 1Sdb positions at 1OOhz_
```
