### General description (1)

<details><summary>Description — 232 words · 1982 - M611 - M611A Power Supply - 004.jpg, page 1</summary><p>M611/M611A POWER SUPPLY The M611/m6I1A Power Supply is designed to be used as a primary power supply for WBS TTL Logic Cards utilizing 5 V regulators_ One output delivers 12 volts at a maximum load of 6A _ A second output, rated at 20 volts, 250 mA is provided to supply a negative biasing voltage. Two power supply versions are available.</p><p>The M611 operates from a 115v AC, 60 Hz power source while the M6IIA operates from 230v AC 50 Hz . Primary taps at the power transformer permit ac input variations of +/ - 10% to be accommodated_ Two separate secondary windings in conjunction with two bridge recti- fiers are utilized to provide complete electrical isolation between the two outputs.</p><p>The primary and secondary fuses are conveniently located at the front panel _ A power ON/OFF switch and a LED indicator are also mounted on the front panel AMI connections are made via the MF6 frame receptacles_ The MF6 connectors are wired for the required ac and dc operating conditions of the M611/M6IIA module Modules may therefore be interchanged without affecting the operation of the system _ The module size of 5.1 X 3.4 X 13.7 inches allows three M611/M6IIA power supplies to be accommodated in one standard rack mounting (191 X 3.5H) MF6 equipment frame_ Perforations in the mounting frame and module assure adequate vertical ventilation by natural convection 2-1</p></details>
