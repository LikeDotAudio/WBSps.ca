04 / Broadcast Engineering & Visual Design Rules

Fundamental design principles governing Ward-Beck precision instruments

1. NO METER COVERS

Meter chambers must have completely open, unoccluded viewports. Never place dark tinted plastic covers, solid molded rectangles, or artificial bezels over the meter scales or needles. The dial faces, tick marks, and needle pointers must remain 100% visible at all angles.

Open Aperture • Clean Flush Rims • fill="none"

2. Ballistics Compliance (ANSI vs IEC)

VU meters follow ANSI C16.5 / IEC 60268-17: 300ms rise time to 99% with 1.0–1.5% overshoot. Peak Program Meters (PPM) follow IEC 60268-10 Type I (DIN 5ms attack) or Type II (BBC 10ms attack) with 2.8s/24dB recovery.

ANSI C16.5 VU • IEC 60268-10 DIN/BBC PPM

3. Zero Reference Calibration

Standard 0 VU reference corresponds to +4 dBu (1.228 Vrms into 600Ω). In digital broadcast workflows, 0 VU is calibrated to either –20 dBFS (SMPTE RP155) or –18 dBFS (EBU R68).

+4 dBu = 0 VU = -20 dBFS / -18 dBFS

4. Phase Correlation Spectrum

Phase correlation row consists of 8 calibrated segments: from +1.0 (0° full mono compatibility, green #22c55e) through 0.0 (90° independent stereo, amber #f59e0b) to –1.0 (180° total cancellation, red #ef4444).

Cosine Phase Spectrum: +1.0 to -1.0

5. Authentic Ward-Beck Silkscreen

Chassis typography uses high-legibility grotesk letterforms (Helvetica / DIN / Arial Narrow Bold). Legends are uppercase with expanded letter-spacing (0.8–2.0px) matching Toronto broadcast manufacturing heritage.
