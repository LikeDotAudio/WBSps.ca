## What it is

The twelve-pot remote panel for the M8202 remote-sensitivity DAs: each 10 k pot is wired through a 38-pin EDAC plug to a card's REM IN. Both AutoCAD wiring drawings are redrawn from their R12 files; turn pots, trace pins and watch twelve cards follow.

## In the simulation

- Both R12 wiring drawings redrawn
- Pot ↔ EDAC pin tracing, clickable
- Twelve M8202 cards follow their pots
- Shield used as the HIGH conductor
- Broken-wiper fault
