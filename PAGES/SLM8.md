## What it is

A desk-top meter on a 15° stand: four AES inputs shown as eight 57-step VU + PPM bars behind a silk-screened lens, with LOCK, PCM, Dolby E and Dolby D indicators. Built from the lens silk, the 2011 LED pick-and-place data, the metalwork drawings, the software listing and the SLM manual.

## In the simulation

- Real lens silkscreen with 464 LEDs placed from the pick-and-place file
- VU bar + PPM dot with Type 1/2 decay and 3 s peak hold
- LOCK and PCM / Dolby E / Dolby D indicators
- Mechanical assembly: enclosure, rear cover, lens, 15° base
