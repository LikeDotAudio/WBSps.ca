|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Summing IN 1 | in | P2-1 | P2-2 |
| A2 | Summing IN 2 | in | P2-3 | P2-4 |
| A3 | OUT 1 | out | P2-11 | P2-12 |
| A4 | OUT 2 | out | P2-15 | P2-16 |
