```text
M4O2B EFFECTS EQUALIZER
DESCRIPTION: The M4O2B Effects Equalizer is a plug-in, high-performance module designed to be compatible with the Ward-Beck group of modular console components_ Frequency selection and degree of equalization are controlled by concentric rotary switches. Individual low and high frequency controls are provided and in addition, multiple frequency presence boost and cut, and high pass filter facilities are included.
FEATURES:
Active circuitry uses operational amplifiers introduces no loss
10dB boost, 15dB cut for low and high frequencies
The multi-frequency correction controls may be used singularly or cumulatively to produce a wide range and combination of effects. The transient free controls may be pre-set or operated directly during program transmission_ A unique lap switch inserts or removes the equalized elements from the system program path:
5 selectable presence frequencies with 10dB boost and cut in 2dB steps
The extensive use of operational amplifiers combined with active filter networks is responsible for the many features offered and the excellent performance characteristics of this compact design . An actively balanced output configura- tion permits a generous margin of signal headroom
High pass filter eliminates low frequency program material noise.
Unique lap switch inserts/removes equalizer circuit in transient-free mode: Panel status lamp indicates equalizer function Low profile, compact design, mounts directly on console control panel in-line with mixing attenuator
The M4O2B is entirely balanced and lossless. The active circuit operates with 38dB gain, to enhance the flexibility of this unit as an equalizer/mix-amplifier within a console system: The use of a simple external resistive network adapts the M4O2B for unity gain operation.
```
