## Footnotes

1. Despite “CURVE” in its name, the file keeps every legend (model line, OUTPUT 1–4, + − G, mains text) as live Arial text; only the PODS logo and “by Ward-Beck Systems” are curves. — `#POD #SERIES/POD29 - #Master/POD29 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/POD29-FRONT&REAR-SILK (CURVE).cdr`
2. libcdr drops the live text's alignment (OUTPUT n was centred), so each line is placed on the word boxes of the file's own PDF export. — `#POD #SERIES/POD29 - #Master/POD29 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/POD29-FRONT&REAR-SILK (CURVE).pdf`
3. The front has a single Ø 6.5 mm power-LED hole, twice the 3.3 mm tally holes of POD27/28, probably for a panel-mount LED holder (inferred). — `#POD #SERIES/POD29 - #Master/POD29 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/POD29-FRONT&REAR-SILK (CURVE).cdr`
4. Each OUTPUT cut-out (18.5 × 12.4 mm) has two flange-screw holes 25.4 mm apart. The plugin had drawn the plugs 32 px high and the IEC inlet 46 px right; both now sit on the drawing. — `#POD #SERIES/POD29 - #Master/POD29 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/POD29-FRONT&REAR-SILK (CURVE).cdr`
5. The rear has the same small hole above the right fixing screw as POD27/28, where the POD28 drawing calls it the ground-stud insert. — `#POD #SERIES/POD29 - #Master/POD29 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/POD29-FRONT&REAR-SILK (CURVE).cdr`
6. The rear mains text reads “90-230 VAC 50-60 Hz, 2 A.S.B.”, while the POD29L/E rears read “100-230 VAC, 50-60 Hz”. — `#POD #SERIES/POD29 - #Master/POD29 - #ARTWORK #DA #2016/POD29X-REAR-SILK(CURVE).cdr`
