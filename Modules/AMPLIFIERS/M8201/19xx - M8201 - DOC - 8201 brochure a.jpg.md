M8201
Announce Channel Control Module
General Description The M8201 is a high performance, actively balanced line amplifier with integral solid-state switching and tallying circuits which may be operated by remote control: The module is ideally suited for remote control ON/OFF switching of an announce booth microphone and preamplifier combination, to activate or mute the audio signal path: The M8201 is exceptionally versatile and may be utilized in conjunction with other WBS 8200 series compatible components to configure complete remote audio mixing system or automation system.
The unit mounts in a standard Ward-Beck MF-82 31/2" rack-mounting frame and is fully compatible with the D8212 DA package.It can be mixed and matched with all 8200 series Preamplifiers, Distribution Amplifiers and associated modules.
