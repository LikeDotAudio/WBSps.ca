GENERAL</p><p>M922/M922A EQUALIZER MODULES</p><p>The M922/M922A Stereo and Mono Equalizers operate in conjunction with the M901 series of input modules.</p><p>A High-Pass filter with a 75 Hz corner frequency may be inserted separately, pre-equalizer, or not at all. Insertion is tallied by the LED located in the insertion switch.</p><p>The equalizer separates the audio into three separate bands. Low frequencies from 20 Hz to 500 Hz are adjustable over ±15 dB of range. The low frequency section imparts a shelving response to the audio spectrum.</p><p>High frequencies are also equalized with a shelving characteristic. Continuously adjustable boost or cut (+15 dB) is provided. The high frequency section can tailor the signal response from 1.8 kHz to 15 kHz.</p><p>The mid frequency section controls equalization of signal from 400 Hz to 6.4 kHz, and  has a bell-shaped characteristic curve. This section has a ±15 dB adjustment range. The quality factor (&quot;Q&quot;) is 2.</p><p>The equalizer insertion switch is provided with an integral LED to tally it&#x27;s operation. All insertion switching in the M922/M922A is performed using solid-state analog switches. Input shield connections may be made at pins 3 and 6.</p><p>Operational amplifier U1A converts the balanced input signal to unbalanced; buffering the equalizer circuity from the signal source.</p><p>High Pass Filter</p><p>Amplifier U1B; along with resistors R15, R16, R17, R18 and capacitors C5, C6 and C7 form a high pass active filter. This unity gain filter imparts an 18 dB per octave response to signals lower than the 75 Hz corner frequency.</p><p>Equalizer Source Selector</p><p>Multiplexer U2 and amplifier U3A form a virtual ground current mode selector. The equalizer can process signals unfiltered, or after they have passed through the high pass filter. Amplifier U3A provides a stiff signal drive to the equalizer network.</p><p>Combining amplifiers</p><p>Amplifiers U3B, U3C, and U3D are combining amplifiers which take the main channel audio signal and add the outputs from the equalizer filters. The equalizer filters reside on the SA14-152 card.</p><p>The equalizing characteristic divides the audio spectrum into three bands. The low frequencies from 50 to 200 Hz are mixed by U3C.</p><p>U3D combines audio components over the high frequency range from 1.6 kHz to 15 kHz. The mid-frequency portion of the audio spectrum over the range of 400 Hz to 6.4 kHz is mixed by U3B.</p><p>Low Frequency Equalizer</p><p>The cut/boost control R2A bridges between the input and output of  mixing (combining) amplifier U3C.</p><p>The armature of R2A feeds a portion of the audio signal  to the low frequency shelf control (R4A). When this control is in the centre detent position, no correction or equalizing signal is fed to the filter network on the SA14-152.</p><p>The output of the filter network is injected back into the summing amplifier U3B; where it is proportionally added or subtracted from the main channel signal. Addition results in a boost condition, while cut is the result of subtraction.</p><p>High Frequency Equalizer</p><p>The high frequency cut/boost control R3A feeds an error signal to the high frequency filter on the SA14-152.</p><p>Similar to the mid and low bands, the output of the high frequency filer on the SA14-152 is added or subtracted by the main channel signal.
