## What it is

The engineering parent of the D6201A (1×8) and D6201B (dual, 2×4 or 1×8 with SRC) openGear AES distribution amplifiers: one ISS5 board, two firmware builds. The model runs the real V1.04 / V1.02–V1.03 code paths — AT90CAN128 super-loop, bit-banged CS8416 and FPGA control, DashBoard over CAN — on the card drawn from its gerbers.

## In the simulation

- Build switch: the same ISS5 board as D6201A or D6201B
- Card from the ISS5 gerbers with live LEDs, EQ switches, DIP and H1–H4
- DashBoard window fed by simulated CAN heartbeats and replies
- One second: 4 900 loop passes, SPI, timer ticks, CAN and AES3 bits
- EQ, SRC and tone paths traced through D6201A.bdf
- Findings: a tone that does nothing, random heartbeats, unsaved EQ
