Compact but utilizes full size blue-ribbon connectors for easy installation
The basic system accommodates ten single modules ( 1.7 inches) or any combination of multiple width units (3.4,6.8 inches etc,) not exceeding total of 17 inches_ A maximum of ten extruded guides ad ten receptacles with floating bushings may be installed. The frame receptacles may be inverted to provide keying which prevents accidental interchange of non-similar plug-in units within the system.
Module designation incorporated in front support bar
Frame receptacles may be keyed
A grooved designation strip permits the identification of each module position with respect to product type and function; the frame designation aligns and corresponds with the front panel designation on the individual modules The unique guide assembly provides positive alignment and restricts module movement in both horizontal and vertical planes_
Rigid design but employs light- weight aluminum construction
Module styled blanks may be secured to the front positions ad, in addition, cable brackets, component tray and cover accessories may be installed at the rear of the frame
