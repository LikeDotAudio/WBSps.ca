## What it is

Engineering’s 2011–2012 component study: two outside boards tabulated part by part, a shortlist of regulators, clocks, SDI and audio parts, and an oscillator quote. The model redraws the studied board, chains the SDI-to-audio parts, and runs the LDO, buck, VCXO and cable-reach numbers from the datasheets.
