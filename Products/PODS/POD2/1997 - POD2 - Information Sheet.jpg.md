Product Description
POD2 is unity gain six by one stereo audio switcher. Connections to POD2 are made on the rear pane two rows of screw terminal blocks. POD2 operates either a5 one of six switcher or, in the SUM mode; selective multi-input mixer The unit is placed in the SUM mode by depressing the SUM button. Sources one through six may now be mixed by depressing the appropriate numbered buttons: To return to the one of six selection mode_ press the SUM button first and then the numbered button of the desired selection. POD2 may also be used in conjunction with POD9 as an audio follow video switcher. When POD2 and POD9 are in the audio follow video mode_ any switch activated POD2 will result in the corresponding switch occurring on the POD9 and vice versa_ POD2 may also be remotely controlled using its RS422 serial interface.
