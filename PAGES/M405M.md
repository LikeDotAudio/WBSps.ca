### Specifications (1)

<details><summary>Technical Data — 301 words · 1995 - M405M - 14-292-02-003 - 3 P1 CONNECTOR 3-1 - LAYOUT.wp, page 1</summary><p>M405M EXTENDED RANGE VU MONITORING SYSTEM</p><p>Range:			-10 dBu to +10 dBu in five steps.</p><p>Input Selection:		Pushbutton select between two sources.</p><p>Source Impedance:		600 ohms or less, balanced.</p><p>Input Impedance:		Greater than 20K ohms.</p><p>Attenuator Tracking:		±0.25dB per step, non-cumulative.</p><p>Meter Ballistics:		Complies with ANSI C16.5.</p><p>Frequency Response:		±0.25 dB 30 Hz to 15 kHz.</p><p>Monitor Output:		±1 dB from 100 Hz to 5 kHz.</p><p>Total Harmonic Distortion Headset Output:		Less than 1%, from 50 Hz to 10 kHz. Speaker Output:		Less than 1%, 50 Hz to 10 kHz</p><p>Noise:			40 dB below   0 VU reference. GENERAL</p><p>M405M EXTENDED RANGE VU MONITORING SYSTEM</p><p>The M405M has been designed to measure and monitor the average level of an audio signal. This instrument is self-contained and mounts in an EIA standard 19&quot; equipment rack. It is powered by a 24 volt DC wall mount power supply.</p><p>A &quot;range&quot; attenuator, providing 5 steps (-10, 0, +4, +8, +10 dBu), accommodates audio input levels from -10 to +10 dBu for a 0 meter reading. The M405M accepts two inputs (A or B) at the rear of the instrument. Selection between the A or B inputs may be made by a pushbutton selector on the front of the unit.</p><p>An internal speaker, complete with level control, is mounted in the front panel to permit the user to listen to the source being metered.  A headset jack is also provided on the front panel for headphone monitoring.
