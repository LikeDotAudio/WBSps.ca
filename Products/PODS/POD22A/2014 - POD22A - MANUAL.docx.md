STEREO LOUDNESS METER

This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers.  PODS are compact, rugged and affordable. They are suited for tabletop use or rack-mountable singly or in pairs, in any combination.

___________________________________________

POD 22A is a half rack, stereo audio loudness meter.  It can be used to monitor analog or AES digital audio signals.  Comprising a 57 segment stereo bargraph meter POD 22A displays both VU and Peak Program information simultaneously.  A pair of Sticky™ peak LEDs can be set to display peaks that exceed a preset limit.  Also included is a seven segment phase correlation meter and six status LEDs to display sample rate, lock to signal and error.

More than a meter, this DSP based product that accepts either stereo analog or AES digital signals, includes a pair of A to D and D to A converters.  The processing power of the DSP incorporated in POD 22A allows for level adjustment and frame delay of the signal being metered (optional).

A rear panel dip switch allows the user to configure the metering parameters of POD 22A, please refer to the  DIP SWITCH SETTINGS table in this manual.  POD22A provides a direct balanced stereo analog audio output and both a balanced and unbalanced AES digital audio output of the signal being metered on the rear panel.  

When fed by a digital audio signal auto detection determines whether the signal  is balanced (110 Ohms) or unbalanced (75 Ohms) and will adjust the impedance`s to match.  Please ensure that only one digital signal is connected to POD 22A in the event that both the balanced and unbalanced ports have a signal connected simultaneously, random selection between the two sources will occur.  Once the signal is locked, POD 22A will display LOCK and the sampling rate of the AES signal.  If the signal is lost or corrupted POD 22A will display ERROR.  

Balanced AES and analog signals are connected to POD 22A on convenient plug gable screw terminals.  Unbalanced digital connections are made on 75 Ohm BNC connectors.
  
The rear panel dip switches allow for selection of the source type (analog/digital), the configuration of the meter ballistics and calibration levels, as well as, an optional serial control.  The optional serial control and a secondary source type selection (analog/digital)  is made available via the RS485 serial port.  Consult factory for more information.

A valuable addition to the POD  family of half-width rack mounting components POD 22A can be mounted singly or in pairs or may be mounted in combination with any other POD product.
