M8245A
POWER SUPPLY MODULE

Power supply for the 8200 series of top-quality modular card frames. Modules can be a mix of Analog Audio and Video as well as AES/EBU Digital audio.          
___________________________________________

The M8245A is the switchmode power supply module for the Ward-Beck MF82 frames, which are part of Ward-Beck's 8200 Series of cards and frames. +22V, -22V regulated outputs at 4A and 3A respectively. The M8245A is powered from AC mains 85 to 264V, 47 to 63 Hz. AC current 3.3 A maximum. 

The M8245A is CSA-approved, runs with low noise, and is interchangeable between frames. One power supply is required per frame. Two power supplies are required for redundancy.


Features

• Switchmode power supply 
• Regulated DC output +22V @ 4A Max, -22V @ 3A Max. 
• Wide range of AC input 85 to 264 V, 47 to 63 Hz 
• Crowbar overload protection
