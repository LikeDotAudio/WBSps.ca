M605 AUDIO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER
DESCRIPTION: The M6O5 audio distribution amplifier is an ac operated, plug-in unit designed to bridge a single source and provide four uniform, highly isolated outputs for distribution throughout an audio system. An additional direct output permits up to four splits to be obtained external to the amplifier_
FEATURES:
Self-contained power supply for maximum reliability
This ultra-reliable design utilizes a self-contained bi-polar power supply to operate balanced differential, operational amplifiers. The absence of conven- tional transformers and coupling capacitors results in exceptionally high per- formance characteristics, particularly with respect to isolation between outputs and system noise and distortion.
IC balanced design
70db isolation between outputs 20hz-20Khz
Four 600 ohm balanced outputs are provided directly at the module connector exhibiting 7Odb of isolation between circuits across the entire audio spectrum. External resistive splitter networks are eliminated; wiring is made directly to the MF6 frame receptacle
40K bridging input
The M6O5 operates with unity gain and may be used with system levels from 1Odbm to +24dbm since the absolute output noise is better than _7Odbm_ f3db vernier potentiometer is conveniently located on the module front panel and permits precise adjustment of system levels. Other front panel facilities include balanced inputloutput test jacks, neon power indicator, ac primary and dc fuses.
Low absolute output noise
Unity gain with #3db front panel trim control
