M8202
Remote Sensitivity Distribution Amplifier
General Description The M8202 amplifier is designed to terminate incoming remote line feeds Of various levels and to provide make-up gain or gain adjustment to standard plant levels from an operator' s work station located up to 1,000 ft. (300M) from central equipment room. The unit includes a unique, integral voltage controlled amplifier that allows an operator to adjust the sensitivity of the amplifier from a remote location in order to compensate for variations in incoming line levels Typically, the M8202 remote sensitivity amplifier is installed in a rack-frame in the central equipment area while the audio level control may be conveniently positioned in front of
an operator/ supervisor located remotely at a work station or audio control console_
Input line level variations of up to 30 dB may be accommodated utilizing a single-turn remote potentiometer with calibrated scale- Optimal dynamic range is maintained over the full range of sensitivity adjustment_
The unit mounts in a standard Ward-Beck MF-82 31/2" rack-mounting frame and is fully compatible with the D8212 DA package: It can be mixed and matched with all 8200 series Preamplifiers, Distribution Amplifiers and associated modules.
