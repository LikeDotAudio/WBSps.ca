```text
Lower Concentric Group Inner (small) knob: IN/OUT _ This knob is a two position selector and is Used to insert or remove the equalizer circuits. In the CCW position (OUT) the M402 operates as conventional amplifier (note: the entire unit is not disconnected, only the active equalizer networks). In this mode the equalizing effects may be pre-set or adjusted without interfering with tne flat response of the M402. At the prescribed time the selector may be turned CW to the IN position and the pre-set response characteristics will modify the program transmission
Outer (large) knob: F; The low frequency knob provides 3Ohz boost in the CW positions and 1OOhz cut in the CCW positions_
MAINTENANCE: The M402 is designed for dependable, trouble- free service; however as with all high quality electronic equipment, regular schedule of inspection should be established. Any accumulation of dust should be periodically removed with a soft brush and a vacuum hose
To gain access to the component boards and switch decks it is necessary to remove the metal side cover which is to the left side of the etched escutcheon_ This is accomplished by removing the two 2-56 flat-head screws on the left side of the M402 escutcheon and the two 4-40 round-head screws located on the bottom of tke unit. Do not loosen the 4-40 screw immediately adjacent to the M402 connector; this secures the frame ground lug internal to the equalizer
The PC boards may be examined by placing the equalizer on a flat work area with the cover removed, the connector positioned to the left and with the control knobs positioned away from the inspector_ The amplifier and equalizer boards are located in position by retaining grooves fabricated from aluminum tabs spot welded to the equalizer case _ Both PC boards may be extended by the length of the cable assembly by gently lifting the boards alternately out of the retaining grooves The boards may be supported by moving each assembly over to the right and temporarily resting the edge of the PC board on the metal enclosure_ Care should be exercised to prevent damage to the interconnecting cable assemblies and upon reassembly, the flexible wires should be channeled Under the PC board notch-outs to prevent crimping.
```
