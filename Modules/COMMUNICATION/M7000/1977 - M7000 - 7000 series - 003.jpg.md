The M712 comprises a basic 12 X 1 balanced audio matrix_ 24 M712 crosspoint cards may be accommodated in the MF7 frame
Using state-of-the-art techniques , dual operational amplifiers produce a solid-state, ba lanced, transient- free audio switch_ 12 balanced cross-points are accommodated on a single PC card in conjunction with a unity-gain summing amplifier _
The summing amplifier incorporates a direct input terminal which may be used to combine the output of other matrix groups
30k ohm balanced bridging is presented by each crosspoint input to prevent source loading by multiple outputs
The solid-state crosspoint exhibits 100 dB of attenuation in the off mode and the summing amplifier ensures 100 dB of isolation between sources The summing amplifier produces a balanced 600 ohm output with +18 dBm level capability_
Each crosspoint may be adapted to function "normally OFF switch ON" "normally ON switch OFF or in either mode with selected loss (part mute, etc) by selecting links or installing an appropriate resistor on the PC card_ In either mode , operation of the crosspoint is achieved by closure to ground of a single control wire
