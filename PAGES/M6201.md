## What it is

The M6201 was designed as an “Embedded Audio AGC”: de-embed SDI audio, level it with AGC, compressor, gate and leveller in a SHARC DSP, and re-embed it, with an optional Dolby decoder and optional video delay in DDR memory. Only the functional drawing and the P-CAD design survive; the model runs the idea with design-intent controls.
