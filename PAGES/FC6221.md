## What it is

The “#PROBLEM” board decoded: an AT90CAN128 card for slot 21 of Ward-Beck's MF6220 openGear frame — CAN to the cards, 1-Wire to the frame EEPROM and power supplies, I²C and switched power to the fan board, FAULT/ALARM and a buzzer. No firmware exists, so the model follows every net of the 2007 netlist.

## In the simulation

- Every edge-connector finger traceable to its net
- Fan-board power switching and the polyfuse limit
- PSU fault → alarm chain
- Bus budget: CAN, I²C, 1-Wire over one second
- Findings: the BOM's MLC-8 title, dead Ethernet pins, the C6201 concept link
