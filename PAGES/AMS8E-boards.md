## What it is

The AMS8E boards are the shared hardware of the AMS8 monitors and their descendants: the AMS8E-1 SHARC/FPGA main board, the AMS8E-4 LED meter board, the AMS8E-5 control board and the AMS8E-8 speaker amp. The boards are drawn from their Gerbers and placed on a test bench. The meter multiplex and the amplifier follow the firmware and the archive’s test sheets.

## In the simulation

- Main and meter boards drawn from the production Gerbers
- Live LED multiplex at 80 Hz (AMS8-1) or 256 Hz (32ME)
- Measured power and amplifier test sheets
- Pick-and-place program errors located
- Obsolete driver swap and BOM slips
