## Footnotes

1. The D6202A card-edge drawing is the dual card: it shows Lock2, No Lock2, EQ2 and an EQ2 switch, none of which are fitted on a D6202A. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/D6202A - #Master #OPENGEAR/D6202A - #Engineering/Functional/D6202A-card-edge.cdr`
2. The D6202A card-edge drawing is identical to the D6201B one apart from the ejector label. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/D6201  # Problem is this the same as A #OPENGEAR/D6201 - #Engineering/D6201A/Functional/D6201B-card-edge.cdr`
3. The T6403A legend says “OUT 3 Card 2” twice; position 9 should be OUT 4 Card 2. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/#OPEN GEAR - #Engineering/OPEN GEAR - #Engineering/openGear Rear Module Guides/D6202A - rear module options.cdr`
4. In the 2008 label sheets the Ω was set in the “WP Greek Helve” font and converts as Σ (“110ΣAES DA”); the assets were corrected to Ω. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/D6202A - #Master #OPENGEAR/D6202A - #Engineering/Functional/D6202A-T6203.cdr`
5. The T6204 drawing is titled “DUAL 110Ω AES DA” and lists IN2 on BNC 2, although the D6202A has one input. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/D6202A - #Master #OPENGEAR/D6202A - #Engineering/Functional/D6202A-T6204.cdr`
6. The T6203 label sheet reads “1x8 110Ω AES DA … SINGLE CARD TERMINATION DFR-8310”. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/D6202A - #Master #OPENGEAR/D6202A - #Engineering/Functional/D6202A-T6203.cdr`
7. The T6204 label sheet is for the MF6220 frame, the T6203 for the DFR-8310. — `#OPENGEAR #SERIES/D6202A - #Master #OPENGEAR/D6202A - #Engineering/Functional/D6202A-T6204.cdr`
