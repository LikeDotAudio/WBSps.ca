M497A DUAL MASTER MODULE

A separate MONITOR output is prov ided, designed to feed the M495 STEREO MONITOR module .as well as to drive a standard vu meter. RIGHT Channel and MONO Output The right channel of the M497 operates in a manner similar to the left channel as described above. - For the MONO output, both channels are combined and appear at pins 111M. The signal level of this output is such that, when connected to the summing input of a second M497A, it will produce an output of +8 dBm, with the left or right channel of the first unit operating at +8 dBm. Power Supply Decoupling An active power supply decoupling network consisting of 01 through 06 provides isolation between the module and the ±25 volt supply rails. Transistors 03 and 06 monitor the supply current > In the event of excessive power supply demands, such as from component failure or amplifier overdrive, the current flowing through 01 and 04 will be limited.
