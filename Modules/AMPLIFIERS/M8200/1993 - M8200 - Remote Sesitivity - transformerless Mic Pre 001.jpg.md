M8200 Remote Sensitivity Transformerless Microphone Preamplifier
General Description The first of its kind, the unique M8200 is a high performance transformerless preamplifier offering remote sensitivity control. Designed primarily to raise low level microphone signals to desired standard plant levels, its wide dynamic range makes it suitable for many other applications requiring gain adjustment: The remote sensitivity control utilizes a single shielded pair _ and will operate over a distance of 1,000 feet or more: The M8200 maintains its
exceptional performance characteristics over the entire sensitivity range: The unit mounts in a standard Ward- Beck MF-82 31/2" rack mounting frame, and is fully compatible with the D8212 DA package: It can be mixed and matched with the M8205 and M8206 Distribution Amplifiers and with other Ward-Beck Series 82 modules
