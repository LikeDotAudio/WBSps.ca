MINIATURE 30 WATT CLASS D AMPLIFIER WITH DIGITAL INPUT

This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers. The microAMP is compact, rugged and affordable.  


___________________________________________

The microAMP 30 Watt Class D Amplifier With Digital Input is part of Ward-Beck’s microPOD series of top quality, low-cost problem solving audio solutions. The microAMP is designed specifically as a ‘back-pack’ for existing passive monitor speakers when only 110 Ohm or 75 Ohm digital feeds are available. The microAMP mounts directly to the back of your passive monitors offering a cost effective solution when only digital signals are available. Give your existing passive monitors a new lease on life in a digital world. microAMPs are pro audio amplifiers, and are designed for use in studio, pro AV, mobile trucks and other broadcasting environments where quality is essential and budgets are limited. 

The microAMP comes designed for both 110 Om installations (microAMP/110) and 75 Ohm installations (microAMP/75).
