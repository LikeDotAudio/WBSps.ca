M4O6B COMPRESSOR/LIMITER
DESCRIPTION: The M4O6B Compressor /Limiter is a plug-in, high performance module designed to be compatible with the Ward-Beck group of modular console components_ Two concentric controls, a single rotary threshold control and an alternate-action insert pushbutton are provided to adjust the operating characteristics of this high speed Compressor/ Limiter .
FEATURES:
Operational amplifier design
5 selectable compression slopes
The M4O6B operates with unity gain. A rotary stepped input threshold control determines the degree of compression (sets the compressor operating point) . The threshold point may be adjusted from ~12dBm to 8dBm. Other controls include independent adjustments for Compression Ratio, Gain Reduction/VU metering, Recovery Time and De-Essing: An alternate-action pushbutton activates the Compressor control circuit, when depressed , and an illuminated status lamp indicates compression action.
Comprehensive control functions including Threshold, Compression Ratio , Recovery Time, De-ess, GRIVU and IN/OUT selector. Fast attack-time
Operational amplifiers and FET control circuitry combine to produce compact design with exceptional performance characteristics As a limiting device , the M4O6B may be adjusted to respond rapidly to remove the effects of peak overload and transient material_ In the compression modes the choice of ratios modify the dynamic range of the program material in accordance with the specified slopes The De-Essing control increases the M4O6B response to high frequency sibilant program material to minimize undesirable microphone essing effects:
