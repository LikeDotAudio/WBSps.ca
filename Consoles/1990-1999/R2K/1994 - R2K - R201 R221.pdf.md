```text
R201 & R221
                       STEREO INPUT MODULES
The R201 & R221 accept two balanced stereo console, inputs, A or B, and has individual front
panel accessible screw driver adjustments to trim the level of the left (lower trim) and right (upper
trim) channels. A MONO pushbutton switch will sum the left and right stereo signal. This feature
is particularly useful when only a mono signal is available at the input or either of the channels of
the stereo signal is missing. The MONO function may be selected by pressing the front panel
pushbutton.

Stereo balanced insert points are provided prior to the FADER and CHANNEL ON switch.
Convenient insert bypass jumpers are located on the module circuit board.

The pre-FADER, pre-CHANNEL ON, CUE switch allows the operator to preview an incoming
signal on the cue speaker located in the console meter bridge.

The direct audio FADER controls the overall signal level through the module. A reference
indicating mark at the 12dB in-hand position is provided. The CHANNEL ON control routes the
signal to the output stage of the module.

The signal may be assigned to any or all of stereo PROGRAM 1, 2, 3, 4 or MONO MIX 1,2 mixing
buses. All mixing buses are balanced. Send levels to the MONO MIX 1 and 2 buses may be
adjusted via front panel potentiometers only on the R221. Each input module also has a stereo
DIRECT output that follows the CHANNEL ON switch.

All select, function and assignment switches are equipped with LED indicators. The LED’s
illuminate to indicate that the switch has been activated. The R221 also has a separate channel
on LED indicator in parallel to the channel on switch indicator.

Each R201/R221 input module has four programmable input and four programmable output
controls for external control. The module may also be configured to activate the MUTE A, B, C,
AUTO CUE or TIMER RESET buses. An RS485 serial I/O port on the R221 allows some module
functions to be under serial control.




                       R201 & R221
              STEREO INPUT MODULE CONTROL
A-B SOURCE SELECT
Source pushbuttons A and B are electronically interlocked and select between stereo inputs A
and B respectively. Left and right trim controls are accessible from the front panel. These trim
controls are post input select.

MONO SELECT
Pressing the MONO pushbutton will mix the left and right channels (L+R) with a -6dB gain. This
mono mix is then fed equally to the left and right channels prior to the module insert out.

P1-P4 PROGRAM ASSIGN
Alternate action pushbuttons P1 to P4 assign the signal to the four stereo program mix buses.
The audio signal assigned to buses P1 to P4 is picked up post fader and is channel on
dependent.
```

```text
M1 and M2 MONO ASSIGN
Alternate action pushbuttons assign the signal to the two mono mix buses. The audio may be
picked up post-fader or pre-fader by setting programming jumpers and follows the channel on
control. The audio may be picked up pre-channel on or post-channel on by setting SW4 and
SW5 on the DIP Switch. Assignment to the mixing buses may be enabled or muted by setting
SW6 and SW7 on the DIP Switch. M1 and M2 may also be sent to the module direct output by
setting SW8 appropriately.

CUE ASSIGN
Assignment of the signal to the cue bus is via the alternate action CUE pushbutton. The end of
travel switch on the fader will turn the CUE off. The signal for assignment to the cue bus is picked
up pre fader and pre channel on. The cue audio is heard on the local console speaker. The
volume control for this speaker is located on the R206/R206A utility module. Pressing the cue
button also activates the auto cue feature in the console headset circuit. Auto cue superimposes
pre fader audio, from the cued input channel, over the dimmed monitor audio in the right headset
channel. The level of the left monitor audio in the headset remains unchanged. Cue speaker
muting can be selective effected by mute bus A, B or C.

CHANNEL ON
The channel ON pushbutton enables the audio signal to the program assignment pushbuttons,
P1 to P4, the mono assignment pushbuttons, M1 and M2 and the channel direct output. Pressing
the channel ON button generates a timer reset and mute control. The timer reset bus is
connected internally to the timer electronics and any module may be jumper programmed to reset
the timer. The mute control may be jumper selected to any or all of mute buses A, B or C. The
mute control may also be set to follow input selection A or B. The channel ON button normally
operates as an alternate action switch to turn the channel on (switch illuminated) and off, or the
channel may be programmed to always be on (switch illuminated) and the button may be used as
an event start control.

EXTERNAL CONTROL
For external control, the R221 input modules offer four control inputs and four control outputs. Up
to eight different pre-programmed operating modes are offered. The different modes are selected
by setting a series of DIP switches and include the definition of the operation of the channel ON
and OFF switches.

                           DIP SWITCH SETTINGS (REF DES=SW2)

SWITCH FUNCTIONS

                        SW1              Mode Selection
                        SW2              Mode Selection
                        SW3              Mode Selection
                        SW4              M1 Pre/Post Channel On
                        SW5              M2 Pre/Post Channel On
                        SW6              M1 Muted/Enabled
                        SW7              M2 Muted/Enabled
                        SW8              Direct Out Enable/Disable

MODE SELECTION

                        SW3      SW2     SW1
                        OFF      OFF     OFF              Mode0
                        OFF      OFF     ON               Mode1
                        OFF      ON      OFF              Mode2
                        OFF      ON      ON               Mode3
                        ON       OFF     OFF              Mode4
                        ON       OFF     ON               Mode5
                        ON       ON      OFF              Mode6
                        ON       ON      ON               Mode7
```

```text
M1 AND M2 CONTROLS

                        SW4
                        OFF             M1 Pre-Channel On
                        ON              M1 Post-Channel On

                        SW5
                        OFF             M2 Pre-Channel On
                        ON              M2 Post-Channel On

                        SW6
                        OFF             M1 muted
                        ON              M1 enabled

                        SW7
                        OFF             M2 muted
                        ON              M2 enabled

                        SW8
                        OFF             Direct output disable (M1 to direct out left
                                        M2 to direct out right)
                        ON              Direct output enable


Note: OFF position is down, ON position is up




MODES OF OPERATION:

MODE 0:         Control in 1- CH ON     Control out 1- CH ON + INA Tally
                Control in 2- CH OFF    Control out 2- CH ON + INB Tally
                Control in 3- A SEL     Control out 3- A Tally
                Control in 4- B SEL     Control out 4- B Tally

                Channel ON acts as an alternate action push button.

MODE 1:         Control in 1- CH ON     Control out 1- CH ON + INA event pulse
                Control in 2- CH OFF    Control out 2- CH ON + INB event pulse
                Control in 3- A SEL     Control out 3- A Tally
                Control in 4- B SEL     Control out 4- B Tally

                Channel ON\OFF button keeps the channel on all the time and is used as an
                event pulse generator. When bringing fader down, the end of travel turns the
                channel off.

MODE 2:         Control in 1- CH ON     Control out 1- CH ON + INA Tally
                Control in 2- CH OFF    Control out 2- CH ON + INB Tally
                Control in 3- A SEL     Control out 3- CH OFF + INA Tally
                Control in 4- B SEL     Control out 4- CH OFF + INB Tally

                Channel ON acts as an alternate action pushbutton.
```
