The output signal is coupled from the emitter of Q5 Via C33 to the "infout" selector switch SW6B,
The regulated 48 volts dc is applied to the active circuits Via pin H. Diodes CR2 and CRS prevent damage to the unit in the event of inadvertent polarity reversal of the 48 volt supply. Q6, Q7 and associated circuitry decouple the output amplifiers &nd provide a current limit to prevent damage to the integrated circuit components during a short circuit at the output with full drive at the input.
FUNCTIONS OF CONTROLS: The M402 has three groups of concentric controls to be used throughout the operation of the unit. These transient-free controls may be pre-set or adjusted during the operation of the unit. With the exception of the Mid- Low-Frequency boost and Presence Boost (these functions share five positions each side of a common selector switch) all functions are cumulative and may be used in combination to achieve multiple or combined effects
Upper Concentric Group Inner (small) knob: 6OIN/6OOUT. This knob i5 a two position selector and is used to insert the GOhz notch filter. Turning the knob CW places the filter in the circuit and the white dot on the knob aligns with the 60 IN position_
Outer (large) knob: HF  The high frequency knob provides 15Khz boost in the CW positions and 1OKhz cut in the CCW positions_
Centre Concentric Group Inner (small) knob: .7 , 1.4 , 2.5 , 3.4 , 5 . This knob determines the presence frequency and operates in conjunction with the CCW five positions of tne PRES switch.
Outer (large) knob: MLF = PRES. This knob produces two effects (separately). In the CW positions and to the right of 0 a Mid-Low- Frequency platform is produced below 1OOhz and in a shelf of 2db increments. In the CCW positions and to the left of 0 the degree of Presence is established. This works in conjunction with the frequency selector (inner small knob) The frequency is determined by the inner knob and the amount of boost, at the selected frequency , is determined by the outer knob (1Odb max. )
