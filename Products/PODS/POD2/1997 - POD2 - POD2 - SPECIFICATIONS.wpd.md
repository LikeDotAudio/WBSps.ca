﻿POD 2
STEREO AUDIO SELECTOR

POD 2 is a unity gain six by one stereo audio selector.  Connections to POD 2 are on the rear panel via two rows of screw terminal blocks.  The left channel connections are on the top row and the right channel connections are on the bottom row.

POD 2 operates as either a one of six selector or, in the SUM mode, as a selective multi-input mixer.  Selection of an input source is made by depressing the appropriate illuminated push button switch on the front panel.  Alternatively selections may be made from a remotely located control panel or POD 2 may be factory equipped with software to allow automation control via an RS 232 data port.

POD 2 may be used in conjuction with POD 9 as an audio follow video switcher.  

SPECIFICATIONS

Gain		Unity.
Max Output Level	+27 dBu, into a 600 Ω load
Frequency Response	+ 0.25 dB. 20 Hz  to 20 kHz
Noise	Better than -85 dBu
Total Harmonic Distortiion	less than 0.01%, 20 Hz to 20 kHz
Inter-Channel Crosstalk	greater than 80 dB, 20 Hz to 20 kHz
Stereo Separation	greater than 75 dB, 20 Hz to 20 kHz
Input Impedance	20 kΩ
Output Imedance	60 Ω
Power Requirements	110 Volts ac, 60 Hz, 12 VA
Ambient Temperature	0° to 50° Celsius
Dimensions	8.69" wide x 1.73" high x 7.0" deep
(221mm x 44mm x 178 mm)
Weight	1.37 kg
(3.01 lbs)
