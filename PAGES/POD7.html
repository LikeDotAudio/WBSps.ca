Operation (1)

Operation — 149 words · 1996 - POD7 - POD7 - POD7MAN.wp, page 1

FROM 110 VOLTS ac:

■ Two jumpers must be present, one connection Y1 to Y2, the other connection Y3 and Y4. This corresponds to JP2 and JP3 being in place.

FOR

FROM 220 VOLTS ac:

■ One jumper must be present, connecting Y2 and Y3. This corresponds to JP1 being in place.

Gain(bridging): Unity. Frequency Response: ±0.2 dB, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Noise: 90 dB Absolute. Total Harmonic Distortion: Less than 0.001%, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. Dynamic Range: 100 dB. Input Impedance: 30 000 ohms or greater. Source Impedance: 600 ohms or less. Common Mode Rejection: Greater than 75 dB at 60 Hz. Power Requirement: 110 or 220 volts ac, 60 or 50 Hz; 5 Watts. Ambient Temperature: 0o to 50o Celsius. Relative Humidity: 20 % to 90 %, non-condensing. Dimensions: 8.69" wide x 1.73" high x 7.00" deep (221 mm x 44 mm x 178 mm)
