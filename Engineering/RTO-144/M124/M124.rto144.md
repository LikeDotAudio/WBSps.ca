# M124 · RTO-144 Connections

## Audio connections

| Port | Signal | Dir | Pin + | Pin − |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | INPUT 600 Ω | in | 4 | 5 |
| A2 | INPUT 150 Ω | in | 1 | 4 |
| A3 | INPUT 37.5 Ω | in | 1 | 4 |
| B1 | OUTPUT | out | 13 | 14 |
