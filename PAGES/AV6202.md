## What it is

Composite-video DA (OEM Cobalt 9233): one differential input — or a frame reference for black burst — to eight 75 Ω outputs, with an LMH1981 sync separator and an AT90CAN128 reporting Video Status to DashBoard (SNMP from V200, 2012|45). Simulated from the V101/V200 firmware, the ISS1–ISS3 netlists and ECOs, and the ISS3 gerbers.

## In the simulation

- Card drawn from the ISS3 gerbers, with working jumpers and level pot
- Main loop blocking once per field to read the LMH1981 line code
- One second down to fields, lines and sync edges
- The V200 pformat latch and other firmware leftovers
- Black-burst distribution from the frame reference
