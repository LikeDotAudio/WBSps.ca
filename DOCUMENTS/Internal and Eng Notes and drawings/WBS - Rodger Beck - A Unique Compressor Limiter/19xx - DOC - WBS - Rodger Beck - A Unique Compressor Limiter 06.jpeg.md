To enable the system to exhibit a fast attack time the period of ramp voltage is greater than 1Mhz . The pulse width injected at the chopper varies from 30 n.sec to 970 n.sec.
Figure 12 shows the Simplified system. The error voltage is derived subsequent to the gain controlling element and consequently achieves the stability of a closed loop system_ The rectified error signal is processed through a low pass filter which determines the attack and recovery times for the compressor and limiter modes. The rectifier and dc control amplifier situated within a feedback loop, produces linear rectification by compensating for the non- linear diode characteristics present at voltages corresponding to the threshold of conduction. In addition, the compression slopes are accurately controlled by overall negative feedback which is applied around the rectifier and dc control amplifier _
A unique operational amplifier configuration is employed at the output to deliver +18 dbm 600 ohms line level . As shown in Figure 13, the upper amplifier operates in the inverting mode and the lower amplifier in the non-inverting mode A balanced output is established and a low impedance is presented at the terminals of the active circuit_ A real resistive impedance is provided via two 300 ohm resistors balanced to ground.
The design concept results in a compressor /limiter with a sharply defined threshold and multiple slope selection. The compression slopes are extremely accurate and effective for a 25db range_ The compressor mode offers slopes from ratios Of 2:1 to 10:1 and the limiter functions with a 30:1 ratio
CONCLUSION: The Ward-Beck M406 is a truly state-of-the-art development having attained, without compromise, all of the performance requirements necessary for indescernible automatic control of program transmission. The unit is entirely balanced, features five selectable compression ratios, operates with low distortion at full compression and line level output and occupies approximately the space allocated for a conventional straight line mixing attenuator .
