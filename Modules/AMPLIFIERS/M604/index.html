Maintenance & alignment (1)

Test Procedure — 132 words · 1967 - M604 - P005 - Setup and Test Procedure.jpg, page 1

Apply 28v dc slowly to test terminals, and note that the output current should be between 12 19 ma Check that regulator output at the emitter Q1 18 +16.5v dc Strap oscillator connector for 400 Hz output frequency Adjust R6 to give 0.35% to 0 .4% distortion. Also note that the output level control range should be between -13 dBm to +14 dBm at 400 Hz .

Strap oscillator connector at 1,000 Hz frequence output The output level control range should be -15 dBm to +10 dBm , The total harmonic distortion should be less than 0 . 5% (typically less than 0 .19) at +8 dBm output The output current should be approximately 18 ma at both frequency setting at +8 dBm output leve 1 Level change between frequencies 19 aB
