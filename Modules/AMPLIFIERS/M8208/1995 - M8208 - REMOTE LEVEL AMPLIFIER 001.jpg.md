M8208
Remote Level Amplifier
General Description
The M8208 Remote Level Amplifier is designed to distribute incoming line level audio feeds of various levels, and to provide level adjustment to maintain standard plant operating levels   Level control from a remote work station located up to 1000 feet (300 metres) from the amplifier is accomplished with single 5000 Ohm linear potentiometer:
The M8208 is capable of handling input signals with levels ranging from 0 to +8 dBu The low noise circuitry provides 110 dB of dynamic range at unity gain. State of the Art common mode rejection provided by the instrumentation amplifier input insures that the M8208 will work flawlessly in harsh environments. High compliance output drive from the compressor allows a maximum of +30dBu (+27 dBm) to be delivered to the load_
The unit includes a industry standard VCA that allows the audio level to be adjusted from a remote location_ Typically, the M8208 Remote Level Amplifier would be installed in a centrally located rack frame; while the audio level control may be conveniently positioned at a operator / supervisor workstation_
The M8208 mounts in standard Ward Beck MF-82 rack mounted frames  It is fully compatible with all members of the M8200 series, including Preamplifiers, Amplifiers and Oscillators
