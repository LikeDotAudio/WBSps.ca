## What it is

The T6303A folder is the 2013|46 procurement package for the T6303 ISS4 board: BOMs, a PCAD BOM, the pick-and-place file, the Gerbers and two contract-manufacturer quotes. The simulator builds the board from that package alone and checks it against the T6303 netlists.

## In the simulation

- T6303A-ISS4 GERBER.zip drawn 1:1
- Nets traced from the copper
- PNP and BOM ID values
- Same board as the T6303 ISS4
- AA6201A guide names the T6303A
