The location of components is shown in drawings 128-06-001 (amplifier) and 129-06-001 (equalizer) If fault is suspected, measur ing the dc voltage present at the transistor electrodes may assist to determine the cause When comparing the measurements listed in the voltage chart consideration should be given to the fact that, for direct coupled sections, fault in one circuit may cause abnormal conditions in other circuit areas within the same section.
If it is necessary to ir exchange component to determine the cause of fault, first remove the solder from the prin ted circuit foil fillets associated with the suspected defective component To do this, light soldering iron should be used (25 watts) in combination with an inexpensive vacuum action type hand desolder ing tool The desoldering tool is designed for component reworking and comprises teflon tip and cylinder equipped with spring plunger which, when loaded and released, creates high impulsive vacuum Zo draw the molten solder Off from the printed circuit board and into the cylinder .
Place the soldering iron and the desoldering tool on the solder connection to be desoldered. When the solder melts thoroughly, press the plunger release trigger Apply the soldering iron on the connection until the vacuum stroke has been completed If any of the 1 ead connections stick to the printed circuit board, free them with scribe or similar sharply pointed instrument .
