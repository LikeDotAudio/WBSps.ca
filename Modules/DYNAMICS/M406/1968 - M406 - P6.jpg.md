### Page 1 (OCR)

```text
INSTALLATION: The M406 is designed to mount directly on console control panel and in line with a straight line mixing attenuator A cutout 6L_ X .S6W inches and two 4_ 40 tapped holes will be required (see dimensional outline drawing)  The M406 escutcheon will overlap the cutout and holes
Connections are made to the 14 pin female mating connector in accordance with drawing 406-09-001 and the following table:
Function:
Termination
Input 150 ohms
Strap A to D and C to F , connect to A and C
Input 600 ohms
Strap D to F connect to A and C
Output 600 ohms
P and R
Common negative 48 volts B+ GND (chassis) Meter
6 and M
POWER REQUIREMENTS: The M4O6 requires 48 volts dc regulated 1OOmA from an M648 , M548 or equivalent.
```
