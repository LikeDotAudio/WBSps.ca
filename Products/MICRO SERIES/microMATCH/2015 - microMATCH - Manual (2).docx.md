STAND ALONE UNBALANCED TO BALANCED CONVERTER

This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers. The microMATCH is compact, rugged and affordable.  


___________________________________________

The microMATCH Stand Alone Unbalanced to Balanced Converter is part of Ward-Beck’s microPOD series of top quality, low-cost problem solving audio solutions. microMatch converts a consumer stereo unbalanced audio pair to professional balanced line levels with proper termination. It also provides a separate balanced mono mix output. microMatch is a professional level converter that is perfect for use in studios, pro AV, mobile trucks and other environments where quality is essential and space and budgets are limited.
