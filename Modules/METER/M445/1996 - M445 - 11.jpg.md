The M445 carton should contain:
The M445 is factory configured to return to +4 dBu reference sensitivity when initialized. Should YOur prefer 0 dBu or +8 dBu as the reference   sensitivity, you can configure the M445 to reset to this state by changing two internal shorting clips
This User's Manual 1 - aC adapter complete with connector 1- M445 meter 1- Rackmount speaker assembly
Simply remove the top cover of the meter: To set a 0 dBu initial reference, remove the shorting clip ACROSS LK3, ensuring that the LK2 clip remains in place. For configuration to +8 dBu reference level, leave LK3 in place ad remove LK2
Installation
The ac adapter, meter; and rack mount assembly should all be separated for installation:
1) Place the rackmount   assembly in your equipment rack Finger  tighten the  four screws:
BATTERY REPLACEMENT
The batteries for the portable section are located on its back   panel   and can be   accessed by removing the 2 screws of the access plate. The M445 requires standard AA (1.2V@60OmAhours) nicad batteries
2) Carefully dock the M445 in the rack assembly. It may be necessary to adjust the rack frame slightly so that the meter will insert and extract easily. Tighten the four rack screws while the meter is docked.
If the unit will not run On batteries ensure the fuse in this compartment is not blown. Replace the fuse with a 0.5 amp slow blow fuse.
3) At the rear of your rack; insert and lock the power connector: Connect youI audio source and power amplifier (if needed) to the barrier strip and plug the unit in:
The meter  will operate when the  power switch is engaged on the front panel.
Note: Before using the meter in portable mode; it should be left docked overnight to ensure that the batteries are fully exercised and charged.
