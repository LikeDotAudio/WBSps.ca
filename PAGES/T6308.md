## What it is

Ten-BNC rear for the M6204A SDI muxer with eight AES inputs: the tenth BNC goes straight to the + leg of the AES4 pair, and the card’s unused SDI OUT2 pin is loaded on the module with 75 Ω. The PNP fits an R10 that the netlist does not have.

## In the simulation

- T6308 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- BNC10 → AES4P traced in copper
- M6204A 8-AES-in pin-out
- R9 dummy load on SDI OUT2
- Extra R10 in the PNP
