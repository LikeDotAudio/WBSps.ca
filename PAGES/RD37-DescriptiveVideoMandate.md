## What it is

Regulatory and cost research on the CRTC described-video rules, modelled together with the audio path a station needed: the M6205C200 card’s real DV / programme-mono routing, GPI switching, metadata fold-down and gain ramps.

## In the simulation

- CRTC hours and Connectus cost model
- Real sample.c DV / PGM-mono mix, per sample
- GPI1 “create DV” and metadata cases
- Document vs shipped code mismatches
- Clickable card DIP switches and T6307 rear
