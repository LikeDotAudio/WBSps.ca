## What it is

One rear module for two cards: five BNCs each, through P101 and P102. All sixteen AES pins of the two connectors are tied together on the board. Four issues; ISS4 (2012|45) is drawn from its Gerbers, and its PNP still loads the 2006|39 inductor values.

## In the simulation

- ISS4 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- Two MEC1 connectors, two ID pairs
- Seven mating cards
- AES pins bused between the cards
- ISS1 ID 30K9 (the T6201 value)
