The DMS1 is a versatile one rack unit assembly that eases the monitoring of audio signals in mixed format installations. Intuitive selection using illuminating pushbuttons allows the user to switch between stereo analog, AES digital and embedded digital audio signals. An onboard demuxer extracts all four embedded AES groups from a serial digital video signal.

The audio signal is displayed on a stereo LED bar-graph VU meter. The analog output of the selected signal that is provided on XLR3 (M) connectors on the rear panel is adjustable via the front panel monitor control. This signal is also available through the front panel headset jack which parallels the monitor output.

All connections to the DMS1 are made in the rear panel. Analog audio connects via a XLR3 (F) pair, 110 Ohm AES signals connect via a XLR3 (F) connector and 75 Ohm connects via a BNC connector. Auto recognition circuitry detects the source impedance of the AES signal and adjusts the input impedance automatically. Only one AES signal 110 or 75 should be connected to the DMS1.

The serial digital video signal connects via 75 Ohm BNC connector, a re-clocked copy of the signal is provided on a second BNC connector, and two demuxed AES outputs are provided.

The DMS1 is equipped with eight front panel pushbutton switches. AUDIO selects the balanced analog audio input, AES selects the digital audio AES input and SDI selects the embedded audio bit stream. The GR1, GR2, GR3, and GR4 select the embedded group and the SWAP button toggles between AES1 and AES2 of the selected group. When SWAP is OFF, AES1 is selected. When SWAP is ON, AES2 is selected. The DMS1 stores all selections in non-volatile memory and on power ON reverts to the last stored selection.

If a signal is lost or corrupted in the AES digital or SDI mode the respective pushbutton LED will flash.

The DMS1 will automatically detect whether the AES/EBU digital signal is balanced (110 Ohms) or unbalanced (75 Ohms). In the event that both a balanced and unbalanced signal is connected simultaneously to the same AES input random selection between the two sources will occur.

A convenient front panel T/R/S headset jack is provided, accompanied with stereo volume control. The speaker feeds are also controlled by the stereo volume control.
