Q3 and associated components form high input impedance emitter follower with the output connected via C8 and C9 to the input of operational amplifier Z1 and selector switch SW2 _ SW2 inserts one of two networks in Z1 feedback loop. In the clockwise rotating positions mid-low frequency boost is produced by the network, C17, R35 _ R36 and R37 connected to SWZA  The degree of boost is determined by the resistive voltage divider network R38 through R43 connected via SW2B. The network establishes platform shelf boost below OOhz in 2db increments to a 1Odb maximum.
The presence boost network is inserted in the feedback loop of Z1 when selector switch SW2 is rotated in the counter-clockwise positions The presence characteristic is produced by bridged-T filter comprising resistors R23 through R32 connected via SW3 in conjunction with C15 and C16. The resistors, selected in pairs by SW3 , determine the notch frequency. Feedback around Z1 is reduced at the notch frequency and therefore signal amplitude increase results at this frequency. The degree of presence boost is determined by the resistive voltage divider network R44 through R49, selected via SW2B_ 1Odb of presence boost is provided in five steps of Zdb per position. Five presence frequencies 7OOhz , 1 . 4Khz , 2.5Khz, 8.4Khz and 5Khz are provided_
Capacitor C19 couples the signal between selector switch SW2B and integrated circuit Z2_ The high frequency boost circuit, comprising R6O C18 and R5O through R55, operates in the feedback loop of Z2 This low pass network produces 1Odb of boost at 15Khz in five steps of 2db per step; the selector switch SWAA provides the required steps.
The low frequency boost circuit also operates in the feedback loop of Z2 and comprises R58, R6O  C26 and resistors R64 through R6g _ These components form a high pass network which produces 1Odb of boost at 3Ohz in five steps of Zdb per step; the selector switch SWIA provides the required steps _
The high frequency cut follows Z2 output coupling capacitor C25 and comprises resistors R70 through R75 and capacitor C27 . SW4B offers five positions of 3db per step attenuation at 1OKhz.
The BOhz notch filter is produced by Q4 , Q5 and associated components . Capacitor C28 couples the signal to FET Q4 gate _ parallel-T Filter is inserted at this high impedance point comprising capacitors C30 through C32 and resistors R78 through R82_ The filter is inserted via switch SW5 which connects the lower section to the series resistors R83 and R84 in Q5 emitter circuit. The filter produces sharp notch which is greater than 3Odb at GOhz.
```
