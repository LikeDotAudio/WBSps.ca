```text
WAAD-BECK SYSTEMS LTD.
CIRCUIT DESCRIPTION
M5Z04A MONO_ INPUT MODULE
Prefade_Listen
The prefade Signal take off point is prior to the program channel switch: It is fed through selector switch SWS to the actively balanced buffer amplifier made up of UIl and its associated components: The output from this amplifier is fed through mixing resistors Rl82 and R183, which feeds the prefade listen signal to the mixing bus via module connector Pl pins 33 and 34.
sense contact on Prefade selector SWS feeds a tally signal to the SA4-077 Logic Control Subassembly.
Insert
The MS2OA Input module insert points provide the means of connecting external processing facilities into the signal path: These points normally connect to patch panel integral to the ST series of consoles
The inline Penny and Giles balanced slide mixing attenuator also connects at the insert point of the module circuitry.
Program Output
The mixing attenuator return connects to P2 pins and E and then to booster amplifier U5 and U6. The booster amplifier feeds the direct outputs, the Pan control R66 and R69 and the post fade Auxiliary Bus The audio signals fed from the Pan control R66 to R69 and its insertion switch SW6 form a left and right stereo bus. The four submaster bus selectors SW7 to SW1O may be assigned a5 required to the stereo outputs. The mix bus outputs appear on connector Pl pins to 16.
Each mix bus selector is provided with contact closure to feed tally information to the SA14-077 Logic Control Subassembly.
The left and right direct outputs appear at P2 pins 8 and J, and P2 pins 9 and K respectively, which follow the Pan Control.
Solo
The solid state solo switches are fed from the left and right stereo bus at the Pan control utput:
FET's Q5 and Q6 along with U7 form a solid state switch and buffer amplifier for the Solo left channel. FETs Q7 and Q8 with the U& form the solid state switch and buffer amplifier for the Solo right channel
Mixing resistors Rl07 and R108 connect to the left Solo Mix bus, while R12S and R126 connect to the right Solo Mix bus_

```
