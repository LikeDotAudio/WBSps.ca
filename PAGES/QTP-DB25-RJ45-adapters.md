## What it is

Passive adapter boards that carry eight balanced channels from a DB25 over two Cat5 cables, one channel per twisted pair. One production panel holds three male TASCAM boards, a shielded male, a female and a Yamaha-pinout board. Pins can be traced end to end, and the cable model shows what the run does to analog and AES signals.

## In the simulation

- Production panel drawn from the Rev 4.4 Gerbers
- Click-to-trace DB25 ↔ RJ45 pins for all four variants
- TASCAM and Yamaha maps read from the EAGLE netlist
- Cat5 cable model for analog and AES3
- A shield jumper wired to nothing
