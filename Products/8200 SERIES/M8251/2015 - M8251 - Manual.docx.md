M8251
VIDEO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER

Just one of many in our line of top quality, modules that fits into the 8200 series card frames. Modules can be a mix of Analog Audio and Video as well as AES/EBU Digital audio.          
___________________________________________

The M8251 is a one by six video distribution amplifier with level trim. All inputs and outputs are made on BNC connectors.




SPECIFICATIONS

Input Impedance
75 Ohm
Input Level
1 Vp-p nominal
Input Return Loss
46dB Typical, @ 3.58M
Output Impedance (6 splits)
75 Ohm
Output level
1 Vp-p, terminated
Response
Better than 0.05 dB @ 3.58M
Differential Gain
Better than 0.15% @ 3.58M
Differential phase
Better than 0.15 deg @ 3.58M
S/N
Better than 60dB, 5MHz B.W.
Power Requirements
± 20V DC 46 mA
Dimensions
1.00" wide x 2.75" high x 10.6" deep
(25.4mm x 70.0mm x 270.00 mm)
Weight
0.517 lbs (235 grams)
