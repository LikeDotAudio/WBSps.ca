2. The ST8201A solder terminal drawn for the M8209 has 11 cups on the left and 12 on the right; the extra bottom-right cup is a second SHIELD. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/M8209 - #Engineering #MF82/M8209_TR3.cdr`
3. Twelve of the 23 ST8201A cups are labelled NOT USED: three rows after OUTPUT 1, two after OUTPUT 2 and one above IN 2, the gaps left by the eight-output layout. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/M8209 - #Engineering #MF82/M8209_TR3.cdr`
4. The sheet draws the ST8201A only; no screw (TB) or BNC option is offered for the M8209. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/M8209 - #Engineering #MF82/M8209_TR3.cdr`
5. M8209_MAN.cdr holds the manual cover (the frame drawing), not operating text. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/M8209 - #Engineering #MF82/M8209_MAN.cdr`
6. The BOM header still reads “D8205” and the PB1/PB2 switch is listed twice as PHA2UEE and PHA-2UEE. — `#MF8200 #SERIES/M8209 - #Engineering #MF82/PCB/m8209_ISS1.bom`
