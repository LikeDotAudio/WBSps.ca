M8204
Stereo Audio Distribution Amplifier
General Description The M8204 stereo audio distribution amplifier is a high performance, actively balanced dual channel distribution amplifier denis gned to provide distribution of stereo and dual channel signals in a cost effective high density package. Each dopoifieo  module comprises two discrete, one input to output audio DA's that are virtually transparent to the program path: Two high quality conductive plastic potentiometers provide continuously variable control over a 36 dB gain range  Signal levels from as low as -22 dBu to
as high as +14 dBu are readily accommodated_ Ultra low noise, balanced preamplifier circuitry results in a Signal to noise ratio Of greater than 100 dB with an exceptional high common mode rejection_ The actively balanced outputs are capable of delivering a maximum of +30 dBu into a 1500 ohm bridging load or +26 dB into a 600 ohm load_
The unit mounts in a standard Ward-Beck MF-82 31/2"' rack-mounting frame, and is fully compatible with the D8212 DA package: It can be mixed and matched with all 8200 series Preamplifiers, Distribution Amplifiers and associated modules
