## What it is

The 2012 attempt to improve the AMS8E monitor speakers. The AMS8E-8 amp board is drawn from its pick-and-place file and modelled from its netlist; switch in the bass-boost values to see the shelf it makes, check clipping headroom, and compare the class-D amplifiers in the folder.

## In the simulation

- AMS8E-8 board from the PNP file, clickable parts
- Issue 3 vs bass-boost frequency response
- Headroom to the LM4766 clip point
- Class AB vs three class-D parts: heat per channel
- Issue-4 tweeter capacitors found in the netlist
