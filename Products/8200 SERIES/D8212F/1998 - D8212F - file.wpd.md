GENERAL

D8212F AUDIO DISTRIBUTION SYSTEM


The D8212F Audio Distribution System consists of an MF82F mounting frame. This frame may house up to twelve WBS M8205 or M8206 series amplifiers and up to two M8245A power supplies. Audio input/output connectors are integral to the frame.

The mounting frame occupies only two units of a standard nineteen inch mounting rack. Its minimal power consumption permits the design of high density distribution systems. Care and consideration should still be given to correct equipment rack ventilation. Adequate air flow will keep the equipment cool and maximize it's operational life.

Audio terminations to the distribution amplifiers are via gold metal contact Edac 516 series connectors. Elco's compatible 38 pin connectors may also be used.
Two separate primary AC power connections, each with a separate fuse, are available at the rear of the D8212F mounting frame. The dual power connections permit redundant M8245A power supplies to be connected to separate primary power sources ensuring the highest overall system reliability possible.


Unpacking

Remove equipment from packing carton and examine for any damage that may have occurred during shipment.

Check that all items ordered are as per packing slip and are accounted for.

WARNING

Do not apply any AC power to the assembly until the complete installation section has been read thoroughly.

Mounting

To simplify system installation and setup it is recommended that all distribution amplifiers be removed and stored in a safe location.

Mount the distribution frame as required in the equipment rack. Do not mount above other heat generating equipment without due consideration for proper ventilation and allowances for proper unrestricted air flow.

Primary AC Power

The two AC power cords are on the right rear section of the MF82F mounting frame.

The primary AC fuses at the rear of the distribution system mounting frame are 1.50 amp slow blow type #3AG.

To configure the audio distribution system for the highest possible primary power source reliability, two separate AC circuits should be utilized all the way back to the main distribution breaker panel, with the breakers connected to different primary AC power phases if at all possible.

Grounding

Refer to drawing D8212-02-002 for additional details.

At the rear of the MF82F mounting frame a separate binding post is provided for the mechanical frame chassis and one for the system electrical ground. These two points are isolated from each other within the mounting frame. These two points should eventually connect together at the audio system station ground point (see drawing B8212-02-002).

The chassis connection should be connected to all other mounting frames and rack cabinetry either directly from the binding post, or via the neutral "U" ground connection (the green wire) integral to the primary AC wiring.


















The use of separate chassis and ground wires returned to a central station ground point will assure a system with the minimal amount of AC and possible RF currents circulating in the audio ground system. Keep audio systems ground connections free from any AC, video, control or possible RF ground returns.

Input/Output Connections

It is normal audio system practice that cable shields be connected at one end to the audio system ground. This is usually at the receiving signal end. A separate input shield ground is provided on each distribution amplifier terconnect assembly, placing the input signal cable shield at the preamplifier potentials of the particular distribution amplifier. This grounding technique can become quite critical in high gain situations where the incoming and outgoing signals have level differences in excess of 20 dB.

A main system ground terminal is provided on each distribution amplifier interconnect assembly where it may be required to connect the shields of high level outgoing cables.

When dressing audio cables at the rear of the MF82F mounting frame always endeavour to keep input and output runs separate from one another as is practical.

To maximize amplifier performance with minimal power dissipation terminate or load only the outputs that are being used.

Power Supply Setup

With all distribution amplifiers removed, apply power to supply #1. Measure the voltage between the rear mounted terminal strip +/-22 volt pins, it should be 44.5 to 45 volts with no load. Remove primary power 


from power supply #1 and apply to power supply #2 only. Confirm again that the no load DC voltage is 44.5 to 45 volts.

Apply power to both power supplies noting that there is little if any change in the output no load DC voltage. Secure the power supply access panel.

The red load current LED indicator will begin to illuminate on the M8245A power supplies. is will normally begin to occur with six or more amplifiers in position.

After approximately 30 minute warm up time, adjust the "VAdjust" trim control of power supply #2 for equal illumination of the red load current LED's on the two power supplies. This shows that the load is equally shared between the two redundant power supplies. 

This adjustment assures that both power supplies are operating with a portion of the total load current, compared to being in an inactive standby state.
