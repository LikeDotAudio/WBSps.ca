## What it is

Dual-card all-audio rear: five Phoenix blocks per card, one single-ended pair (REF IN / IN 1) and four AES pairs. ISS1 (2006|39) put the single-ended block on the BNC9 pins, which no mating card uses; ISS2 moved it to BNC1.

## In the simulation

- ISS3 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- Two cards, two ID pairs
- AA6201A, D6202A/B, D6203A
- ISS1 REF IN on an unused pin
- 887R shared with T6406 and Ross
