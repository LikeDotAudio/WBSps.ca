GENERAL

The D8204A is an AES/EBU distribution amplifier providing both digital and analog stereo outputs.  This unit can provide four digital outputs and two analog stereo outputs simultaneously.  All digital inputs and outputs are unbalanced 75 Ohm, while the analog outputs are actively balanced low impedance. Cable equalization and reclocking techniques enable the D8204A to recover the incoming digital audio signal reliably.
  
Lock, Confidence, Error and EQ LED tallies are located on the front edge of the amplifier card.  The D8204A mounts in any standard Ward-Beck MF82 rack mounting frame, is fully compatible with other products in the 8200 Distribution Series and can be installed in any position within the frame.  To install the D8204A in an MF82 frame, orient the card with pins 1 and A positioned at the top and the letters on the handle reading from the top down.

Various terminal assemblies are available for connections to the D8204A.  The BNC2 is used with the MF82/12 frame and offers BNC connectors for digital inputs and outputs, and screw terminals for analog outputs. The terminal assembly mounts on the rear of the MF82 frame directly behind the D8204 module.

SETUP AND INSTALLATION

Equalization
For most applications, the D8204A only requires installation in the appropriate mounting frame and connection of input and output wiring.  Factory calibrated, the D8204A can recover digital audio signals conforming to AES/EBU specifications and  transmitted over 75 Ohm, coaxial cable up to 600 metres (2000 ft) long.  For cables lengths in excess of 600 metres, we recommend that the input equalization be activated. To activate the cable equalization, simply press the button located on the front edge of the card.  The yellow LED located on the bottom of the EQ switch will illuminate to indicate that cable equalization is activated.

Output Analog Audio Level
The D8204A provides two balanced stereo outputs of the digital input signal.  The output level of the left and right channels may be adjusted separately by adjusting the CHA & CHB potentiometer.


STATUS TALLIES AND MONITOR
Status
The D8204A is equipped with four LED status tallies located on the front edge of the card, above the handle.  The function of these tallies are: 
GREEN LED
LOCK- Indicates that the receiver is locked to the incoming AES/EBU signal.
YELLOW LED
CONFIDENCE - Indicates an eye pattern violation.  It is a warning that the incoming signal is approaching the limits beyond which reliable recovery may be jeopardized.  This is typically caused by the band limiting effects of long cable runs.  To eliminate confidence errors, it may be necessary to activate the equalizer and adjust the gain, to optimize the performance of the receiver.
RED LED
NO LOCK- Indicates that the receiver cannot lock to the incoming signal.  This may mean that there is no signal or the signal is beyond the recoverable range of the receiver and may require equalization.
YELLOW LED
EQUALIZER - Indicates that the cable equalization has been enabled.
