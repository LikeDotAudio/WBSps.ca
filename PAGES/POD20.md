## What it is

Half-rack SD serial digital video distribution amplifier: CLC014 adaptive cable equalizer, CLC016 multi-rate reclocker (143/177/270/360 Mb/s) and two CLC007 drivers feeding six 75 Ω outputs, with lock and rate tallies. Modelled from the ISS2 netlist (2000) and manual.
