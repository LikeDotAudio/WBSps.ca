This controller performs numerous supervisory control functions within the console system. It controls the mute logic facilities for the mixing channels, integrated channel mach ine control start/stop functions, and control of the console monitoring channels M525) . The MS2OOA integrates and controls the follow ing optional features of the St console series; the M524 programmer unit, M526 overload status assembly , the M5202 series of external routing Sw itchers, etc _ Six dual bit parallel multiplexed address/data ports are made available for feeding data in and out of the control system Two data ports are available for discrete control inputs and outputs Sixteen functions per port are made available_ The inputs for these discrete functions are isolated through optocouplers and the outputs are isolated with dry contact relay closures
The logic family of integrated circuits is known as the 74HC series which is the latest in high speed cMOS technology The logic levels for the data ports for this family of integrated circuits and the data ports of the MSZOOA are nominally 3.5 volts or greater for logic level and volt or less for logic level ; this is for volt supply .
