## What it is

Kevin’s October 2012 sketch of a pocket adapter that turns an iPod’s headphone output into AES3, modelled from the sketch. Plug, charge and run down the battery, watch the LED states, and follow a tone from the app to dBFS on the AES output.

## In the simulation

- Adapter redrawn from the concept sketch, clickable
- LED thresholds 10 / 5 / 1 % from the sketch
- Level path from iPod to AES dBFS
- AES3 subframe at the playhead
- V1 fixed 48 kHz vs V2 SRC
