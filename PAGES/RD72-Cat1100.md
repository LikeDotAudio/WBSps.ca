## What it is

Dolby withdrew the CAT552 decoder module in 2013 and named the CAT1100 as its replacement. RD72 put the new 100-pin card next to the old SIMM on the AMS8E-1 issue 5 main board. The model runs the ATmega firmware, five FPGA copies and the card’s host interface, from the bench fixture to the half-finished in-chassis integration.

## In the simulation

- Both decoder sockets drawn from the issue-5 Gerbers and netlist
- Five FPGA copies traced net by net
- Metadata parser that cannot read CAT1100 packets
- Reset line moved to a pin that goes nowhere
- 11-step evaluation tutorial, bench to chassis
