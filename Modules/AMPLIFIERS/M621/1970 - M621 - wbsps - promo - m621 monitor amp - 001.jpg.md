DESCRIPTION
The M621 20 watt audio power amplifier is an ac operated, plug-in module designed for professional applications in the broadcast_ motion picture and sound recording industries The unit represents the continuing development and expansion of the well proven WBS 600 series of 3-1/2 inch modular components and offers a wider scope of selection for monitor amplifiers from this design category. The circuit utilizes differential operational amplifiers to produce an ultra-reliable transformerless design capable of developing a minimum of 20 watts rms to drive an 8 ohm loudspeaker directly. Other load impedances (4, 16 ohms, etc ) may be accommodated with appro- private power output interpolation. A unique protection circuit incorporated within the M621 activates instantaneously during adverse amplifier load conditions or at a pre- determined power output level. The amplifier does not sustain damage even when deliberately shorted at full power output. combination of conditional frame strapping and a variable front panel level control permits the M621 to operate over an input signal range of 20dBm to 18dBm The M621 presents a permanent 20k ohm impedance at the input and is capable of operating from a balanced or unbalanced source. The unit exhibits exceedingly uniform frequency response with Iow noise and distortion over the frequency range 3OHz to 20kHz. An internal muting relay is included to provide amplifier cut-off when an external (closure to ground) command is initiated via muting logic control circuitry, etc. The modest size of 5.1W x 3.4H x 13.70 inches allows three M621 monitor amplifiers to be accommodated in one standard rack mount- ing (19W x 3.5H) MF6 equipment frame.

20k bridging input balanced/unbalanced
