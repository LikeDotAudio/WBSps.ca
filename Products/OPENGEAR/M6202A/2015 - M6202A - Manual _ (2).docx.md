Introduction
Overview
The M6202A is an HD/SD-SDI AES/EBU Audio Demuxer designed for broadcast use.  The M6202A is a three group demuxer and it can extract up to six AES/EBU audio signals from either an SD-SDI or HD-SDI signal. The M6202A will extract embedded audio in accordance with SMPTE 272M & SMPTE 299M standards. Cable equalization and reclocking techniques enable the M6202A to recover the incoming SD-SDI or HD-SDI signal reliably. With the appropriate rear module installed the M6202A can provide either balanced 110 Ohm AES/EBU outputs or unbalanced 75 Ohm AES/EBU outputs. The M6202A is housed in the openGear OG3-FR-C series frames.

Rear Module Options 
There are two variations of the rear module offered.  The T6301A and T6401A are both for use with the twenty slot OG3-FR-C frame.  The T6301A is for use with a single card for installations where maximum output capability is required.  The T6401A is for use with two cards in the OG3-FR-C frame for installations where high density is required. On the T6301A rear module, a retimed output of the SDI signal is provided, as well as the six AES outputs of the three selected groups.  The T6401A offers an SDI input to each card and four AES outputs of the two selected groups. Other variations of rear module may be made available upon discussion with the factory.

Board Installation
Use the following procedure to install the M6202A in an openGear OG3-FR-C Series frame.
Refer to the User Manual of the openGear OG3-FR-C series frame to ensure that the frame is properly installed according to instructions. 
When using the M6202A with the OG3-FR-C frame, please note that the even numbered slots are to be used.   Plug the M6202A modules into slots 2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16,18, and 20 for a maximum of 10 cards.  Slot 1 is the left most slot as you look into the frame from the front.
After selecting the desired frame installation slot, hold the M6202A card by the edges and carefully align the card edges with the slots in the frame.  Then fully insert the card into the frame until the rear connection plugs are properly seated on the midplane and on the rear module.
This completes the procedure for installing the M6202A in an openGear OG3-FR-C Series frame.
