## What it is

The openGear twin of the POD31: an EP1C12 de-embeds SD/HD-SDI audio groups to six AES outputs and routes one pair through a plug-in decoder module (CAT No. 552) for Dolby E or AC-3. The model runs the AT90CAN128 firmware (M6202AE v2.02, with the unreleased M6202A v1.04 for comparison) against SDI lines, HANC packets, SMPTE 337 bursts and the CAT552 reset line, on the real ISS3 gerber artwork with a DashBoard-style panel.

## In the simulation

- One second down to SDI lines, HANC packets, SMPTE 337 bursts and single bits on the FPGA bus
- Real ISS3 card from the gerbers, T6204/T6304/T6306 rear modules, clickable DIP and P5 module
- DashBoard panel driven by the real OIDs, SET handlers and their commented-out branches
- CAT552_RESET from inside the 1 ms timer interrupt, 48 ms stalls, endless resets without a module
- 20 findings: SD LED on HD, settings lost at reboot, DIP read before the FPGA is back
