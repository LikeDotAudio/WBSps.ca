POD24
POWER AMPLIFIER

This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers.  PODS are compact, rugged and affordable. They are suited for tabletop use or rack-mountable singly or in pairs, in any combination.


POD24 is a 10 Watt stereo monitor amplifier. The output level is set with the front panel adjust controls.  In the STEREO mode the Left control adjusts both channels simultaneously.  In the MONO mode the left and right output levels may be adjusted individually.  Connections to POD24 are located on the rear panel.  Balanced inputs are via XLR3(F) connectors and the speaker outputs are made on quick release speaker connectors.
