INSTALLATION: The M404A is designed to mount directly on mixing console front panel- The dimensions are such that the unit may be mounted in line with standard straight line mixing attenuator associated with each mixing channel cutout 1.56w 6L and two tapped holes will be required as shown in drawing 404-04-010 _ The escutcheon of the M404A will overlap the cutout and holes
External connections are made to the 22 Pin PC connector in accordance with drawing 402-02-011 For optimum results, use insulated, shielded twisted pairs for both input and output wiring Dress all audio cables apart from ac and power supply wiring Ground shields at one end only and use separate ground return wire to the system common ground for the power supply and shields as shown in the System Wiring Diagram B402-02-012
The metal frame of the M4O4A equalizer should be bonded to the system ground_ In this case it is permissible to loop through each unit with common ground wire connected via pin 19
When used in systen, such as an audio mixing console, the M4O4A equalizer should be operated from separate power supply than the ones used to provide dc power to program amplifiers cue amplifiers or other similar units operating at high output levels The latter units produce large current swings and the possibility of instabilitya noise or crosstalk should be avoided in systems which combine high gain and wide-band amplifiers
