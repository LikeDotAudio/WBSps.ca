6w rms direct output into 8 ohms

Compact design mounts in standard WBS 3 inch MF6 rack frame
A combination of conditional frame strapping and variable front panel level control permits the M6O6 to operate over an input signal range of ~2OdBm to +18dBm
Self contained power supply
The M606 presents a permanent 2Ok ohm impedance at the input and is capable of operating from a balanced or unbalanced source. The unit exhibits exceedingly uniform frequency response with low noise and distortion over the frequency range 30Hz to 20kHz
Operational amplifier transformerless 1C design
20K bridging input
The modest size of 1.ZW x 3.4H x 13.7D inches allows ten M606 utility amplifiers to be accommodated in one standard rack mounting (19W x 3.5H) MF6 equipment frame_
