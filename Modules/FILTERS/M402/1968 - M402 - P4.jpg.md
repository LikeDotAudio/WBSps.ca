### Page 1 (OCR)

```text
INSTALLATION: The M402 is designed to mount directly on mixing console front panel_ The dimensia s are such that the unit may be mounted in line with a standard straight line mixing attenuator associated with each mixing channel_ cutout 1.56W X 6L and two tapped holes will be required as shown in drawing 402-08-001 The escutcheon of the M402 will overlap the cutout and holes.
External connections are made to the 14 pin female mating connector in accordance with drawing 402-09-001 and Table 1, For optimum results_ use insulated, shielded twisted pairs for both input and output wiring. The optional external insert switch should be wired with shielded twisted pairs and located not more than 24 inches from the module. Dress all audio cables apart from ac and power supply wiring. Ground shields at one end only and use separate ground return wire to the system common ground for the power supply and shields as shown in the System Wiring Diagram 404-09-002 The metal frame of the M402 equalizer should be bonded to the system ground. In this case it is permissible to loop through each unit with a common ground wire connected via pin "N"
When used in system, such as an audio mixing console, the M402 equalizer should be operated from a separate power supply than the ones used to provide dc power to program amplifiers, cue amplifiers or other similar units operating at high output levels _ The latter units produce large current swings and the possibility of instability, noise or crosstalk should be avoided in systems which combine high gain and wide-band amplifiers.
```
