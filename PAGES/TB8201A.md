## What it is

Fixed screw-terminal rear module with inputs A and B and eight balanced outputs on two 9-way blocks (+ column, − column; 1–4 for A, 4–1 for B). Copper-for-copper it has the same contact map as the pluggable TB8201AP, including the 2-way H2 header for the eighth output.

## In the simulation

- ISS1 Gerbers drawn 1:1 (right half of a shared panel)
- Every screw traced through the copper to H1/H2
- Same contact map as the TB8201AP
- Mate with D8204C, D8201A or D8204A
- Ground through the hatched pour
