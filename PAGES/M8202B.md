## What it is

The 600 Ω version of Ward-Beck's remote-sensitivity analog distribution amplifier: ±15 dB of gain set locally or by a 10 k pot up to 1000 ft away. Run the manual's set-up with the real trims on the 14-212 board, turn the remote pot and watch the VCA follow.
