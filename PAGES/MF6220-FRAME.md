## What it is

Ward-Beck's own 2RU, 20-slot openGear frame: a midplane with a slot-ID resistor ladder and buffered 150 Ω reference to every slot, a five-fan alarm door and a rear reference/comms board. Two hot-swap supplies, a Ross FINC in slot 21. Obsolete.

## In the simulation

- Front and rear drawn to scale from the design notes
- Slot-ID ladder decoded per slot
- Power, −7.5 V and fan alarms
- Reference distribution to 21 slots
- CAN, I²C and fan timing over one second
