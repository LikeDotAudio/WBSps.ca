WAAD-BECK SYSTEMS LTD_
GENERAL
M5200 CONTROLLER MODULE
The m52oo is the central, microprocessor based, data processing unit for the ST series of Stereo Television consoles_
This controller performs numerous supervisory control functions with- in the console system. It controls the mute logic facilities for the mixing channels, control of the console monitoring channels (M525) and may be interfaced with an M5201 Machine Control Module to provide control of the input channels status display readouts_
The M52oo integrates and controls the following optional features of the ST console series; the M524A Programmer Module, the M526 Overload Status Module, and the RS8024 external stereo routing switcher_ Interfacing the M5200 Controller Module with an M5201 Machine Control Module will allow the activation of the routing swi tchers alphanumeric source selection readouts. These readouts are installed in the designation area, in Tine with the associated stereo input channels 
Eight dual bit parallel multiplexed address/data ports are made available for feeding data in and out of the control system.
ninth data port, which has access to the internal bus structure of the M5200, is made available to allow for system expansion and customization
The logic family of integrated circuits is known as the 74HC series which is the latest in high speed cMoS technology The logic levels for the data ports for this family of integrated circuits and the data Ports of the M5200 are nominally 3.5 volts or greater for logic level and less than volt for logic Tevel; this is for volt supply _
AM data ports are on 37 pin 'D ' female connectors, while the internal data bus port is accessed through 37 pin 'D' male connector
The M52oo is packaged in standard nineteen inch rack mount assembly occupying one rack unit of vertical space (1.75 inches) and is sixteen inches deep _
The M5200 is powered by an external 12 volt unregulated power supply The power to the controller is via 15 pin male 'D ' connector
