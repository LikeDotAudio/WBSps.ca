M4O4A HI-LO PASS FILTER
DESCRIPTION:
FEATURES:
The M4OAA Hi-Lo Filter is a plug in, high performance module designed to be compatible with the Ward-Beck group of modular console components_ Individual controls provide ten cut-off frequencies for the high and low frequency spectrum: The rate of attenuation is 18dB/octave.
Active circuitry uses operational amplifiers introduces no loss_
1OLF cut and 1OHF cut positions at 18dB/octave
Low frequency cut (high-pass) is effective at the following 3dB points:
70Hz, 1OOHz , 150Hz , 250Hz,500Hz, 1kHz, 2kHz, 3kHz, 4kHz, and SkHz.
Solid-state audio switch rapidly inserts/removes filter circuit in transient-free mode
High frequency cut (low-pass) is effective at the following 3dB points:
500Hz , 1kHz , 2kHz, 3kHz, 4kHz , SkHz, 6kHz, 8kHz , 1OkHz, and 12kHz _
Panel status lamp indicates filter operation
A mode selector provides a third rotary control function which permits the use of the Hi only, Lo only or simultaneous Hi-Lo circuits to achieve a pass-band effect_ The transient-free controls may be pre-set or operated directly during program transmission. A unique solid-state audio switch rapidly inserts or removes the filter elements from the system program path.
Balanced circuit design
The M4OAA utilizes operational amplifiers and is entirely balanced and lossless The active circuit operates with 38dB gain to enhance the flexibility of this unit as a filter Imix-amplifier within a console system_ The use of a simple external resistive network adapts the M4OAA for unity gain operation_
Low profile, compact design_ mounts directly on console panel in-line with mixing attenuator
