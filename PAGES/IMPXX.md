## What it is

Passive AES3 impedance-matching transformers: one transformer per channel converts 110 Ω balanced (XLR or Phoenix) to 75 Ω unbalanced BNC (AES3id). Made as the IMP-1M/1F in-line tube and as 12- and 20-channel rack panels, redrawn in 2013.

## In the simulation

- Eight panel variants drawn from IMPPNLLT and the 2013 drawings
- Plug and unplug each channel
- AES3 bit cells, reflections and eye over cable length
- Return loss for every load
