M6oo UNIVERSAL AUDIO AMPLIFIER
DESCRIPTION: The M6OO is a high performance audio amplifier designed specifically for professional applications in the broadcast and sound recording industries. The unit exhibits exceedingly low input noise (~128dBm) , high output capability (1 watt) , uniform frequency response and exceptionally low total harmonic distortion_ Accordingly, an entire audio system may be designed around the M6OO amplifier. The use of subsidiary plug-in printed circuit cards to accommo date all active circuit elements assures optimum flexibility and convenient unit interchange_
FEATURES:
30 dBm ( IW) output less than 0.25% THD
Exceptionally low input noise (-128 dBm)
Integral input and output transformers provide multi-impedance operation and totally balanced system performance capabilities. The specially designed input transformer is electrostatically and magnetically shielded to minimize suscepti- bility to induced hum and longitudinal noise degradation.
50 70 dB gain conditional strapping on frame receptacle
Integral FET 35kHz low pass filter
The low impedance presented at the amplifier output terminals permits the use of external resistive splitting networks for simple audio distribution applications
Conditional strapping is accomplished on the frame receptacle Thus; M6Oo amplifiers may be readily interchanged within a system without regard to function.
Plug-in active elements
The modest size of 1.ZW x 3.4H x 13.7D inches allows ten M6OO amplifiers to be accommodated in one standard rack mounting ( 19W x 3.5H) MF6 equipment frame. Perforations assure adequate vertical ventilation by natural convection of air through the modules
