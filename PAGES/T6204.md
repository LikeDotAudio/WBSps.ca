## What it is

Two BNCs on top for 75 Ω inputs or SDI, eight Phoenix terminal blocks for balanced AES or audio outputs. The ISS1 board left its ID resistor value blank and had no AN_BMID2 resistor at all; ISS2 adds the allocated 6K98.

## In the simulation

- ISS1 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- D6202A and M6202A pinouts
- Missing ID resistor in ISS1
- Isolated BNC shells
- Card-side ID read
