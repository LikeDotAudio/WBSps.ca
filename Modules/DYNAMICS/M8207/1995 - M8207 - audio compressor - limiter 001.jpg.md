```text
M8207
Audio Compressor/Limiter
General Description
Outstanding performance, superior craftsmanship, and international recognition justifiably describe the M8200 series of distribution amplifiers The M8207 wide band audio compressor continues the pedigree.
The M8207 acts on input signals with input signal levels ranging from 0 to +8 dBu: The low noise circuitry and industry standard gain control element results in wide dynamic range at unity gain. State of the Art common mode rejection provided by the instrumentation amplifier input insures that the M8207 will work in harsh environments_ High compliance output drive from the compressor allows maximum of +30dBu (+27 dBm) to be delivered to the load.
Designed to effect a modest 2:1 compression ratio to the balanced input signal, the compressor is virtually transparent to program audio. Transient signals are controlled by a high speed limiting circuit Ideal for interfacing to digital audio workstations, the M8207 will maintain audio clarity without the WOrry of a digital overload.
The M8207 mounts in a standard Ward Beck MF- 82 rack mounted frame. It is fully compatible with all members of the M8200 series amplifiers, including Preamplifiers, Amplifiers and Oscillators.
The M8207 offers a high impedance bridging input and low output impedance (60 Ohms), ideally suited for constant voltage distribution applications
```
