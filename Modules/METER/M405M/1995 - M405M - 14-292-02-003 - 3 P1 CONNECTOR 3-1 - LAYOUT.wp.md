GENERAL

M405M EXTENDED RANGE VU MONITORING SYSTEM




The M405M has been designed to measure and monitor the average level of an audio signal. This instrument is self-contained and mounts in an EIA standard 19" equipment rack. It is powered by a 24 volt DC wall mount power supply.

A "range" attenuator, providing 5 steps (-10, 0, +4, +8, +10 dBu), accommodates audio input levels from -10 to +10 dBu for a 0 meter reading.
The M405M accepts two inputs (A or B) at the rear of the instrument. Selection between the A or B inputs may be made by a pushbutton selector on the front of the unit.

An internal speaker, complete with level control, is mounted in the front panel to permit the user to listen to the source being metered.  A headset jack is also provided on the front panel for headphone monitoring.
