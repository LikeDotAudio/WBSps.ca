TECHNICAL DATA
TRANSDUCER GAIN 45-6Odb + 0.Sdb (conditional strapping on edge connector)
FREQUENCY RESPONSE +0. 25db from 20hz to 20Khz
EQUIVALENT INPUT NOISE ~124dbm (1Ohz to 1OOKhz bandwidth) -127dbm (2Ohz to 20Khz bandwidth)
M 124 AUDIO AMPLIFIER
HARMONIC DISTORTION Less than 0.25% from 3Ohz to 20Khz Less than 0.5% from 2Ohz to 20Khz (Measured at watt output level)
The M124 is a high performance audio amplifier designed to offer optimum flexibility with minimum space occupancy for custom console applications in the broadcast and sound recording industries: The unit exhibits low input noise (-124 dbm) high output capabi" lity (1 watt) , uniform frequency response and exceptionally low total harmonic distortion In addition, the M124 printed circuit module measures Only 37 X 5h X 1 3/8 (component width) and includes a specially designed input transformer for multiple source impedance operation_
SOURCE IMPEDANCE 37.5/150/600 ohms balanced or unbalanced
INPUT IMPEDANCE Greater than X 10 source impedance
INPUT LEVEL -16 dbm maximum
A unique active, balanced output circuit permits totally balanced systems design without the use of bulky output transformers and the attendent distortion introduced by such components at high output levels The low impedance presented at the amplifier output terminals permits the use of multiple resistive splitting networks with maximum isolation between output lines_
LOAD IMPEDANCE 150 ohms or higher (nominal 600 ohms)
OUTPUT IMPEDANCE Less than 1/10 load impedance
OUTPUT LEVEL +3Odbm , watt maximum
Conditional strapping is accomplished on the card connector Thus, M124 modules may be readily interchanged within system without regard to function An entire console system (monitor amplifiers excepted) may be designed around the M124 plug-in card 
PHASING The low terminal number of the input pair and the low terminal number of the output pair are in phase
POWER REQUIREMENTS 48 volts dc regulated_ 100 mA at watt output level
AMBIENT TEMPERATURE 0O to 60Oc
DIMENSIONS PC Card 37 X 5x X 1 3/8W in.
WEIGHT Ibs net
C AT.
```
