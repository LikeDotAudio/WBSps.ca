## What it is

One board — PIC16C73, EPM7128S CPLD, ADSP-21061 SHARC and two CS8420s — that becomes the D8205 mixer/level DA, D8206 tone generator, D8207 SRC or D8208 level DA depending on one #define. The model runs the v1.104 PIC firmware pass by pass: jumpers, lock, clock plans, the SHARC handshake and the delayed output enable.

## In the simulation

- Board drawn from the real D8200-2 Gerber silkscreen and copper
- Pass-by-pass PIC v1.104 model with 31-pass debounce and 85-pass output enable
- 16-nibble PIC→SHARC control frame and SHARC gain/route maths from the .dat tables
- Switch the build between D8205, D8206, D8207 and D8208
- Findings: TESTING lines that stop Output 2 muting, a dead 44.1 kHz option, swapped CH names
