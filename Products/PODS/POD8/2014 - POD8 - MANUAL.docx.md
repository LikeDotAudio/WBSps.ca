POD8

STEREO AUDIO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER

This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers.  PODS are compact, rugged and affordable. They are suited for tabletop use or rack-mountable singly or in pairs, in any combination.
          

___________________________________________



POD8 is a stereo audio distribution amplifier providing six adjustable stereo outputs of a single stereo input signal.  Twelve separate screwdriver adjust potentiometers, on the front panel, allow the user to trim the levels of the left and right channels of the six outputs independently.  Input and output connections to POD8 are via screw terminals located on the rear panel.
