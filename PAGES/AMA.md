## What it is

The archive copy of the AMA-4 “Audio Monitor System”: the 2002|35 front and rear silk drawings, a 2003|36 manual sketch and the obsolete DWGs. The model runs the AMS4 v2.0x firmware path with the DEMUX option that the 2002 rear shows (SD1–SD8), and documents the front-panel M1 file that differs from the AMA4 folder.
