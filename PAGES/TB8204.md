## What it is

Rear module for the M8204 dual-channel analog DA: A and B balanced inputs and four balanced outputs per channel. Outputs 1–3 of each channel share one card driver through 30 Ω per-leg build-outs on the module; output 4 has its own contacts. Two generations: the 1997 Tango screw board and the 2001 pluggable TB8204P (P-CAD netlist).
