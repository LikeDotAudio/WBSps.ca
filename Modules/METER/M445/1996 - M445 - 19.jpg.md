CIRCUIT DESCRIPTION
SAL4-305 MQTHERBOARDASSEMBLY
Refer to drawing B 14-305-02-001/2
Audio Circuitry
Audio which is amplified (Or attenuated) by these first three stages is switched into US section A and B, where it is unbalanced and either attenuated by 10 dB, Or transmitted with no net gain into the high pass filter switch and the filter itself: The high pass  filter is a  third order butterworth filter with -3dB point at 7SHz. Switch SW4 determines which signal, broadband or filtered enters Ull.
Both the left and right   channels   function similarly. This discussion will pertain to the left channel Differences with the right channel will be noted as necessary.
The M445, owing to its large input sensitivity range, uses three different input amplifiers to spread across the full sensitivity range.
The CS3310 (U11) is a Stereo Digital Volume Control: Internally; Ull contains two ladders which are digitally controlled by a 16 bit control word. The control word is fed serially from the microprocessor to pin of Ul1. All   level transitions are internally timed to occur at audio Zero crossings, eliminating the "zipper" effect. The gain structure of the M445 is set up so that a nominal -15 dBu level at the output of U11 will generate a 0 VU signal on the front panel meter:
The first amplifier; comprised of UL sections A and B, is an instrumentation amplifier input stage with gain of +25 dB. Magnetically latched relay Kl controls audio flow into this amplifier; while U2 controls the output signal into US section A and B. This amplifier stage is active for front panel sensitivity settings of -60 dB to 335 dB.
The second   amplifier consists of U3 and associated components. This is essentially unity gain stage, which also has it's input assigned by Kl. Analog multiplexer U3 simply switches the signal into US or not for front panel sensitivities from -34 dB to 0 dB.
The output of Ull is switched through SWS, which controls the mixing of L+R and L-R signals; if selected. The audio signal is then amplified by U9C (+13 dB) Amplifier UlOB generates balanced audio   signal which  is coupled to the meter; rear panel outputs and the front panel headphone jack through a series of buildout resistors.
A loss stage; consisting of U4 and it's input pad, manages input sensitivities from +] to +30 dB.
