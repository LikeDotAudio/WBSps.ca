WBS
M648 REGULATED POWER SUPPLY
DESCRIPTION: The M648 plug-in regulated power supply is designed primarily for use in WBS consoles and systems The unit provides 48V dc IA regulated output and an auxiliary 24V unregulated output for relay /lamp operation The low internal impedance permits the operation of multiple amplifiers within a system from a common power source without crosstalk or noise interaction.
FEATURES:
Dual Supply 48V Regulated plus 24V relay /lamp power
Over-voltage ad automatic current limit
The standard model features current limit ad over-voltage protection The current limit is electronically activated in the presence of a short or excessive current demand and is automatically restored when normal load conditions are resumed:
Convenient front panel controls and fuses
'Conditional strapping is accomplished on the frame receptacle to achieve either local or remote sensing: In the latter mode the comparison circuitry is referred to a selected external distribution point to eliminate the effects of signal coupling due to lengthy common power leads
Active elements on plug-in P.C. card
The active circuit elements are contained on a subsidiary plug-in P.C. board for maximum flexibility and convenient unit interchange To further simplify maintenance , the series regulator power device is mounted utilizing a insulated chassis socket;
Encapsulated reference amplifier for excellent temperature stability
The modest size of 3.4W x 3.4H x 13.7D inches allows 'five M648 power supplies to be accommodated in one standard rack mounting (19W x 3.5H) MF6 equip- ment frame. Perforations assure adequate vertical ventilation by natural convection of air through the modules
Convection cooled
