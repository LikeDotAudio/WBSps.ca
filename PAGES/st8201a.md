## What it is

A passive MF8200-series rear termination with solder terminals: inputs A and B, eight balanced outputs, a 20-pin and a 2-pin header to the card. Its netlist matches the TB8201AP pluggable module net for net; the schematic carries a copied TB8204P title block.

## In the simulation

- Every terminal traced to its net, header pin and TB8201AP equivalent
- Output 8 on the overflow header H2
- Card-edge net naming (A–N without G, I)
