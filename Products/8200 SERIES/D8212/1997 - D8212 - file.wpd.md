﻿D8212 Audio Distribution System

General 

The basic system comprises an MF-82 frame, up to twelve plug-in amplifiers and dual redundant switch-mode power supplies.
The frame is standard rack mounting and occupies just two vertical rack units of space.  Located on the rear of the MF-82 frame are twelve respective berg headers for each amplifier slot.  There are six different terminal interface assemblies available that conviently attach to the berg headers on the rear of the frame to allow readily available input and output connections..
A choice of high performance 8200 Series DA cards are available to suit various applications.

M8200	MICROPHONE PREAMPLIFIER

M8204/A	STEREO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER
M8205/B	MONO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER
M8206/B	MATRIXING AUDIO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER
M8207	COMPRESSOR LIMITER
M8208	REMOTE LEVEL DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER

Trim controls are conveniently position along the front edge of the cards for easy adjustment.  The ingeniously designed hinged/quick release front panel may be removed and replaced in a matter of seconds for access to the controls.
Two separately fused primary AC power connections are available at the rear of the MF-82 mounting frame.  The system operates on 115/230V, 50/60 Hz requiring 130 VA maximum.  The separate AC power connections ensure the highest overall system reliability possible. 

Grounding

At the rear of the MF82 mounting frame a separate binding post is provided for the mechanical frame chassis and one for the system electrical ground.  These two points are isolated from each other and should eventually connect together at the audio system station ground point.
The chassis connection should be connected to all other mounting frames and rack cabinetry either directly from the binding post, or via the neutral "U" ground connection ( the green wire) integral to the primary AC  wiring.
The use of separate chassis and ground wires returned to a central station ground point will assure a system with the minimal amount of AC and possible RF currents circulating in the audio ground system.  
HINT : Keep audio systems ground connections free from any AC, video, control or possible RF ground returns.
