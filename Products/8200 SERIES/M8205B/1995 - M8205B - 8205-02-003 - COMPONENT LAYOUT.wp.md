GENERAL

M8205B AUDIO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER


The M8205B Audio Distribution Amplifier was designed to be a transparent component to an audio distribution system. Its beyond the state of the art technical performance along with ultra reliability will result in years of trouble free service.

The input circuitry is configured to provide an actively balanced bridging load to the audio source. It rejects large common mode voltages that may exist between the source and destination points of signal path.

The front panel continuously variable sensitivity control covers a 36 dB gain range. This gain range accommodates input signal levels from -22 dBm to +14 dBm for a nominal +8 dBm output signal level. The sensitivity adjustment is normally made via a small screwdriver, optionally this control may be supplied with a small trim setting knob.

The ultra low noise characteristics of the preamplifier circuity result in a signal to noise ration of greater than 104 dB at the unity gain sensitivity setting, decreasing to only 75 dB at a -22 dBm input sensitivity.

The eight actively balanced 60 ohm outputs normally operate at a nominal signal level of +8 dBm. a maximum output capability of +28 dBm is provided with all outputs simultaneously loaded. The output splitter networks are integral to the amplifier card assembly,  providing greater than 85 dB of isolation between individual outputs.

The compact size and low standby power requirements of this amplifier permit high density audio distribution systems to be configured. The MF82 mounting frame houses twelve M8205B Audio Distribution Amplifiers, with two redundant M8245 Power supplies in only two rack units of a standard nineteen inch equipment rack.
CIRCUIT DESCRIPTION

M8205B AUDIO DISTRIBUTION AMPLIFIER


Input

The input signal to the amplifier is applied at connector pins 1 & A, and the input shield connects to pin B.

R1 and R2 sets the amplifier's input impedance to 30,000 ohms. IC U1 with its associated components make up the preamplifier circuitry. CR1 through CR4 provide limiting to excessively applied input signal levels.

C4 and R9 are adjusted to provide optimum common mode signal rejection across the audio band.

The front panel sensitivity control R14A/R14B controls the amount of feedback that is applied around preamplifier IC U1B and buffer amplifier U2A. This two stage sensitivity control arrangement results in very high signal to noise characteristics being maintained over wide gain variations.

The audio output signal from buffer amplifier U2A is coupled to the output power amplifier stages through coupling capacitor C10.

Output

The actively balanced output power amplifier is composed of two sections. The upper half comprising of driver U3 and complimentary power transistors Q5 and Q6, and the lower half consisting of U4 and complimentary power transistors Q2 and Q3.

This power output stage is capable of delivering in excess of 4 watts continuously with no detrimental effects. A shorted output will have very little affect on the remaining outputs.

Q1 and Q4 are bias setting transistors which thermally track the amplifier's ambient temperature, thereby maintaining the correct quiescent currents for the complimentary output power transistor.

DC output offset voltages may be trimmed down to the microvolt region by adjusting R19 and R36. Once set there is little need for making further adjustment, only in the event that either IC U3 or U4 is replaced.  Since the two power output stages are DC coupled R19 should be adjusted first and then R36.

Power Supply Decoupling

The ±22 volt DC supply bus voltages appearing at the amplifier connector pins 15 & S and 13 & P are isolated from the amplifier circuitry via fusing resistors R52 and R53. The incoming  ±22 volt supply voltages are filtered by C18 through C21, after which they connect directly to the amplfier's power output stages.

Q7 and Q8 form the positive and negative supply decouplers for the preamplifier and buffer IC's U1 and U2.  These decoupling circuits filter and drop the supply voltages to ±18 volts.

The typical quiescent supply current for the M8205B is 35 mA. At the full rated output of +28 dBm into eight 600 ohm loads the supply current is 300 mA.
