## What it is

The 2011 feature matrix of 50 Ward-Beck products against 38 I/O and control types, turned into a module-kit model: which blocks recur, how many products a small kit could build, and which existing product fits a requested stand-alone option.

## In the simulation

- All 50 × 38 sheet cells, clickable
- Module-kit coverage at any threshold
- Stand-alone option builder with nearest-product fit
- Real rear silkscreens where cut
- Sheet errors and gaps listed
