## What it is

Passive rear module for the single-input 8200 cards (M8205/M8205B, M8203, D8201, D8202, D8203, D8205–D8207/110): a balanced input, eight balanced output pairs and, from ISS.2, a passive input loop. No parts but terminals: each output goes straight to its own pair of card contacts.

## In the simulation

- ISS.1 and ISS.2 drawn 1:1 from the Tango copper
- Passive input loop: bridge it, or terminate it
- Eight independent output pairs, traced pin by pin
- Silk vs 2012 guide labels — they disagree
- Six mating cards from the rear-module guides
