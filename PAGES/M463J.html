M463J SUBMASTER MODULE

The M463J is designed to accept combined or mixed feeds from previously assigned mixing channel inputs. The assigned mixing channel inputs are amplified and routed to the appropriate . points within the console system. Input and Summing Amplifier The signal originating from the external mlxlng network is applied to the summing current nodes of the input amplifier via edge connector pins I/A. Ql through Q8 form a summing amplifier comprising a balanced input and an unbalanced output. To correct for small system level variations, trim pot R7 is .i ncor por a t e d as part of the preamplifier. The differential balance adjustment control R6 serves to minimize common mode type signals originating ahead of the amplifier. C12 couples the output of the summing amplifier to a low-pass filter consisting of R22, R23, R24, C13, C14, CIS and ZI. The 3 dB roll-off point is located at approximately 40 kHz. The output of ZI is coupled through C18 to the output amplifier 8-109A. 8-109A Output Amplifier and INSERT The 8-109A amplifier raises the nominal signal level to 0 dBm. The output is actively balanced and fed to the "insert point" at pins 15/5. Pins 15/5 are usually connected to a normalled set of insert jack~, with the output side connected to the submaster fader. The fader output is returned to the module on pins 9/K. The feed for the PREFADE outputs is normally picked up at the mixing fader input and is returned to the module via pins 14/R. 8-109F Booster Amplifier a .c ps

This amplifier operates with a gain of 33 dB. It recovers the 15 dB "in-hand" loss incurred by the fader and provides BS
