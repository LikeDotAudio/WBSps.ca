## What it is

Pluggable screw-terminal rear module with two balanced inputs and eight balanced outputs on ten 3-way Phoenix blocks, for the D8204C and D8201A (and wired to a D8204A). Passive: each block goes straight to its own card contacts, the eighth output via a separate 2-way header.

## In the simulation

- ISS1 and ISS2 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- Every screw traced to its EDAC contact
- H2: the extra 2-way header for OUT B1
- The ISS2 Gerber set also carries the RLM24-6 board
- Mate with D8204C, D8201A or D8204A
