WAAD-BECK SysTeMS LTD_
CIRCUIT DESCRIPTION
SA14-096 DISPLAY ASSEMBLY
This assembly mounts to the front panel of the M5200 series of controllers and supports the pertinent display and front panel switch functions of the controller_
The green indicators DS2 and DS3 show when the volt power is present on the SA14-094 motherboard assembly and applied to the daughterboard assembly (5A14-095 or SA14-123 )
The red LED DSI the On Line indicator, shows when the controller is in the Master mode and processing data. This indicator is dependent upon the setting of the Master/Slave switch SW1
The Master/Slave switch SW1 must be left in the Master position to process external data If placed in the Slave position all data ports are transferred into the tri-state high impedance conditions and the microprocessor goes into standby mode _ This requirement or feature is presently not utilized in the control system of the ST series of Stereo Television consoles
Momentary pushbutton switch SW2 is the M5200   microprocessor_
hardware means of resetting
The seven segment status display U1 is software development tool that has been incorporated in the controller operating system The individual segments are used to show the execution of various software routines_ This gives visual indication that the software is executing correctly . The status Latch output which drives the display decimal point, pulses the hardware watchdog circuit. If the software stops operating correctly the decimal point will not be alternately turned off and on , resulting in the watchdog resetting the microprocessor approximately every two seconds
```
