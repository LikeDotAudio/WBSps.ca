## What it is

The most used Ward-Beck openGear rear: ten BNCs for one card. ISS4 (2008|41) puts a 4n7 inductor in series with every BNC and 75 Ω across seven of them for SDI return loss, and AC-couples the four isolated input shells to ground.

## In the simulation

- ISS4 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- Seven card pinouts (D6201A/B, AV6201A, M6202A, M6203A, M6204A)
