Ward Beck ~power supply digikey.com, part number 271-2009-ND Qustral; c has So6 4 tansfotonA$ I Sot my RS (ror JLM audio Ja D;j kct #had 52 @itl +4 JLM Ps It is pre-assembled as in it's an entire power supply, not a kit: But you do need to wire it yourself AC in and the connect + common to the units. It has various AC in options for different voltages (US, UK, HK)
The power lines are already connected to the PCBs (green connectors) The pre s go into the PCBs with the power on the bottom:
Wire the 19-22 and w-Z (power connection) just like any WBS 460 module
Next; jumper 1l to 12.
Jumper M to N. Jumper 13 to 14.
Jumper P to R. Mic in is I+ and A- and shield B and 2. Output: jumper 15 and 16 to + and $ and T to
Run the shield up to the mic shield on B and 2
That's it.
Below is all the info I was able to get on the modules, from Dave Thomas, a guy who $ known for his Ward Beck knowledge (I have personally not used his recommendation on my M46IM pre' = s):
What Dave said:
The M461 have the earlier preamp and transformer used in the M460 modules   The power supply connections are identical to the M470 module but the inputs and outputs are different plus the buss output is down -]Odb from the M470 modules but you should be able to compensate for this by increasing the _ gain of the output card. There are a couple of spare pins that you can also use for a direct balanced output: I would also suggest replacing C6 and C7 on the 4-110 preamp board. This tantalum bypass capacitors have a habit of shorting You can just replace them with 1 Oufd/35wv electrolytic as no audio goes through them: You might have to lay- the electrolytic on their side as they have a higher profile than the electrolytic.
If you jumper W and Z with an 82 ohm resistor then the channel on light will last a lot longer as it will be getting 24 volts instead of 24 volts. You will also have to jumper pin 18 to pin 20 if you want the phase LED to light:
```
