## What it is

A 1RU SELECT SERIES “AMA-4 Audio Monitor System”: the AMS4 monitor (4 analog + 4 AES inputs, display, VU and phase meter, headset) with rear binding posts for external speakers. This model uses the 2015|48 silks and the 2016|49 M1/M2/M3 drawings; the electronics and firmware behaviour come from the sister AMS4.

## In the simulation

- Real front and rear silk on the M1/M2 drilling
- MODE/SOURCE with the AMS4 firmware path (sw_mode, auto_detect, lock_count)
- 75/110 Ω auto-detect and lock delay in slow motion
- Level from input to the speaker posts
- M1/M2/M3 drawn to scale
- Two different M2 ISS 3 files found
