## What it is

Dual-card rear for two stereo converters (D6203A A/D or D6204A D/A): three BNCs and two Phoenix blocks per card. ISS1 (2006|39) wired the left-channel block to the right-channel pins and the right-channel block to unused pins.

## In the simulation

- ISS3 Gerbers drawn 1:1
- Two cards, two ID pairs
- D6203A and D6204A pin-outs
- ISS1 analog channel mix-up
- ISS1 bused AES pins
