M4OSM Meter Monitor System
General Description
The M4OSM MeterMMonitor system is the latest rack mounted audio metering product from Ward-Beck Systems Ltd.
The M4OSM has five selectable input sensitivities; -10 dBu; 0 dBu; +4 dBu, +8 dBu, and +10 dBu, which provide "0" indication on the ballistically correct VU meter_
The M4OSM has been designed for utilization in central equipment and rack room environments for audio level metering and aural monitoring: The high (greater than 20 000 Ohm) input impedance allows the M4OSM to be simply bridged across the source to be monitored. The internal 2 Watt amplifier, complete with level control, provides 'confidence" monitoring of the audio quality.
Two input sources are selected to the circuitry of the M4OSM Interlocked switches with gold contacts provide the front panel source selection   Input connections are made at the rear of the unit on a barrier strip.
The M4OSM requires two standard EIA 19 inch rack units (3.50 inches) for mounting, and utilizes precious metal switching for all audio functions The M4OSM is powered by a standard 24 volt DC power supply, furnished by a wall mount adapter:
