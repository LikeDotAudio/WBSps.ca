POD18
QUAD AUDIO INTERFACE

This is one of many in our line of top quality, low-cost broadcast problem solvers.  PODS are compact, rugged and affordable. They are suited for tabletop use, or rack-mountable singly or in pairs, in any combination.


___________________________________________


POD18 is a 4 channel audio interface that converts 4 consumer grade unbalanced audio signals to balanced broadcast quality signals.  POD18 allows the use of low cost consumer grade record/playback equipment in a professional environment by matching the impedance and signal level to professional broadcast standards.

Front panel trim control lets the user set playback levels of consumer equipment to the professional standards employed.

Connections to POD18, on the rear panel, are male XLR 3 type for the balanced outputs and RCA phono jacks for the unbalanced inputs.
