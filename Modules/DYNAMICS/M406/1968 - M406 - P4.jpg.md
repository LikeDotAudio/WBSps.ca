Lower Concentric group: Inner (small) knob: IN/OUT This two position switch disables or activates the control amplifier input signal and thus determines if the M406 operates as a linear amplifier or compressor/limiter The important factor with this control is that it does not bypass the active audio circuits within the unit but only disengages the compression action
Outer (large) knob: RECOVERY _ The five position recovery selector determines the rate at which the lower level program material is expanded.
OPERATIONAL PROCEDURE: The object of a compressor is to reduce the dynamic range of the subject program material by reducing the higher level material and expanding the lower level material. The purpose of a limiter is to prevent overload distortion resulting from excessive instantaneous peaks _ The M4O6 may be adjusted to operate in either mode
In the compressor mode the ratios are indicated in db_ In the 3/1 ratio a 3db increase in input level will produce Idb increase in output Level. The choice of compression ratio is dependent on the content of the program material and the processing capabilities of the subsequent equipment (recorder transmitter , etc.) 2/1 is conventional for dialogue material , 3/1 and 5/1 ratios may be employed to control more erratic music productions. Set the COMPRESSION switch to the desired ratio.
Set the IN/OUT Switch to "out position_
Initially set the THRESHOLD and OUTPUT controls at 3dclock ~position.
Set the GRNVU switch to VU position.
Patch the compressor in to the appropriate circuit via jacks_
Adjust the output level control until the VU meter reads OVU _ (Note: For installations operating with other system levels an output pad with attenuation equal to the difference should be installed external to the compressor output terminals. Thus,to permit the compressor to operate between insert jacks at ~2Odbm a 2Odb pad should be installed at the M406 output.)
