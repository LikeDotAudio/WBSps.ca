## What it is

This folder’s D6208A.sch is really the D6207A dual-input Dolby decoder platform saved under the D6208A number: two AES inputs, CS8416 receivers, an EP1C12 FPGA and a CAT552 decoder socket feeding six AES outputs. The model runs that platform as design intent and compares the three designs that share the D6208A name.

## In the simulation

- The three D6208A designs side by side
