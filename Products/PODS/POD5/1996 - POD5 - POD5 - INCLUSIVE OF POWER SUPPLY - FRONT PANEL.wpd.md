Location of this unit around RF fields
If this unit is placed on top of or next to video monitors, a buzz may be heard through the unit.  To remedy this:

○	leave as much space between the
monitor and this unit.
○	Ensure that the 'U' ground

To configure your POD for operation from 220 volts ac mains, the following procedure must be followed to the letter BEFORE APPLYING POWER to the POD.

1)	Remove the top cover by removing the four screws which hold it in place.

2)	Locate the power transformer.

3)	Located near the power transformer are four (4) lugs, which look like this:







FOR OPERATION FROM 110 VOLTS ac:

■	Two jumpers must be present, one connection Y1 to Y2, the other connection Y3 	and Y4.  This corresponds to JP2 and JP3 being in place.

FOR OPERATION FROM 220 VOLTS ac:

■	One jumper must be present, connecting Y2 and Y3.  This corresponds to JP1 	being in place.

Please make sure that these jumpers are connected according to the above wiring convention.  Any damage caused by connecting your POD to mains with the jumpers configured incorrectly is not covered by our normal warranty.
