## What it is

The December 2011 cross-reference of Serialboxx MF100 cards against Ward-Beck openGear cards, as a working conversion model: load an MF100 rack, see the openGear frame it becomes, and which functions have no openGear card.

## In the simulation

- MF100 rear drawn to scale, clickable slots
- Live conversion to a 20-slot openGear frame
- 13 functions with no openGear card
- v1 vs v2 spreadsheet differences
- Density and power comparison
