The MID-FREQUENCY cut/boost control R34 derives its signal across the output amplifier , formed by U7 , Q5, Q6 and its related components. Depending on the setting of R34 , an in-phase or out-of- phase proportional signal is fed to the state variable band-pass filter formed by u4, u5 and its associated components. The band-pass center, frequency is determined by C26 and C31 in conjunction with the setting of frequency control RLS/R61 _ frequency range from 400 Hz to 6400 Hz is covered _ The bandwidth of the filter may be altered by Q switch Sws . The 0-factor is set to for HIGH and 1.5 for Low Q.
The band-pass filter output is fed back to pin of U7 , where it is added or subtracted from the main signal depending upon its phase relationship Thus, it affects cut or boost of the audio signal in the mid-frequency region of the audio spectrum.
Both the HIGH FREQUENCY and the Low FREQUENCY sections of the equalizer provide SHELVING type of equalization with maximum cut or boost of 10 dB at the respective corner frequencies (10 kHz and 100 Hz ) The MID FREQUENCY section provides bell-shaped peaking response curve with maximum cut or boost of 10 dB at the selected center frequency
With the EQUALIZER INSERT switch SW4 released the filter amplifiers are inactive and u2 and u7 pass an unequalized signal _
