## What it is

The 2012 concept for a four-channel analog-to-SDI POD, modelled around the TDA9955HL converter in the folder. Pick inputs on a POD-size rear panel, watch a line being digitised, step through the I²C set-up, and test the $250 cost target.

## In the simulation

- POD-size concept panels that drive the model
- Analog line vs 8-bit codes on a scope
- EAV/SAV words live at the playhead
- TDA9955 register set-up and NDIV default
- $250 cost target calculator
