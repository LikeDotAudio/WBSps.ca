Introduction
Overview
The M6204A is an HD/SD-SDI AES/EBU Audio Muxer designed for broadcast use.  The M6204A can insert up to eight AES/EBU audio signals into either an SD-SDI or HD-SDI signal. The M6204A will embed audio in accordance with SMPTE 272M & SMPTE 299M standards respectively. The M6204A supports 24-bit AES audio embedding for HD and 20-bit AES audio embedding for SD.  In addition the M6204A is Dolby compliant and automatically disables the sample rate conversion on the AES inputs to permit seamless Dolby embedding. Cable equalization and reclocking techniques enable the M6204A to recover the incoming SD-SDI or HD-SDI signal reliably. The M6204A is housed in the openGear OG3-FR-C series frames.

Rear Module Options 
There are two variations of the rear module offered, and both are used with the 20-slot OG3-FR-C frame. The T6201A provides an HD/SD-SDI input and up to seven AES/EBU inputs on 75 Ohm BNC connectors. The T6201A also provides two HD/SD-SDI outputs with embedded audio present. The T6301A provides an HD/SD-SDI input and up to seven AES/EBU inputs on 75 Ohm BNC connectors.  The T6301A also provides two HD/SD-SDI outputs with embedded audio present. The T6308A provides an HD/SD-SDI input and up to eight AES/EBU inputs on 75 Ohm BNC connectors.  The T6308A also provides one HD/SD-SDI output with embedded audio present. Other variations of rear module may be made available upon discussion with the factory.

Board Installation
Use the following procedure to install the M6204A in an openGear OG3-FR-C Series frame.
Refer to the User Manual of the openGear OG3-FR-C series frame to ensure that the frame is properly installed according to instructions. 
When using the M6204A with the OG3-FR-C frame, please note that the even numbered slots are to be used.   Plug the M6204A modules into slots 2,4,6,8,10,12,14,16,18, and 20 for a maximum of 10 cards.  Slot 1 is the left most slot as you look into the frame from the front.
After selecting the desired frame installation slot, hold the M6204A card by the edges and carefully align the card edges with the slots in the frame.  Then fully insert the card into the frame until the rear connection plugs are properly seated on the midplane and on the rear module.
This completes the procedure for installing the M6204A in an openGear OG3-FR-C Series frame.
