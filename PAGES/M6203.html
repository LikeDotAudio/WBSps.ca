What it is

A 1-in, 8-out openGear SDI distribution amplifier: GS2974A equaliser, GS2975A multi-rate reclocker (ASI too) and four GS2978 drivers, with an AT90CAN128 that debounces the reclocker’s LOCKED pin, lights the rate LEDs, mutes and disables the outputs, and reports to DashBoard. Modelled down to SDI frames, lines, words and bits, the reclocker’s rate search and every main-loop pass.

In the simulation

  • Card drawn from the ISS3 gerbers with live LEDs; T6301/T6401 rear modules from the CorelDRAW drawings
  • GS2975A rate search, LOCKED/LOS/SS pins and the 101-sample lock debounce, sample by sample
  • SDI lines, EAV/SAV words and scrambled NRZI bits at the playhead; ASI on true vs inverted outputs
  • DashBoard window fed by simulated CAN reports and replies, with reboot and upgrade paths
  • Findings: unused bypass, LOS on the wrong pin, SDI Input alarm mismatch, ASI output list explained from the netlist
