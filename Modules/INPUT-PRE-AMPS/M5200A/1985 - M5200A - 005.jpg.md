M52OOA CONTROLLER MODULE
The MS2OOA is the central microprocessor based, data processing unit for the ST series of   Stereo Television consoles
This controller performs numerous supervisory control functions within the console system. It controls the mute logic facilities for the mixing channels, integrated channel machine con trol start/stop functions, and control of the console monitoring channels (M525) The MS2OOA integrates and controls the follow" ing optional features of the ST console series; the M524 programmer unit, M526 overload status assembly , the M5202 series of external routing switchers, etc Six dual bit parallel multiplexed address/data ports are made available for feeding data in and out of the control system. Two data ports are available for discrete control inputs and outputs Sixteen functions per port are made available_ The inputs for these discrete functions are isolated through optocouplers and the outputs are isolated with dry contact relay closures .
The logic family of integrated circuits is known as the 74HC series which is the latest in high speed cMoS technology_ The logic levels for the data ports for this family of integrated circuits and the data ports of the MS2OOA are nominally 3.5 volts or greater for logic level and volt or less for logic level this is for volt supply .
ninth data port, which has access to the internal bus structure of the MS2OOA , is made available to allow for system expansion and customization.
AII data ports are on 37 pin 'D' female receptacles, while the internal data bus port is accessed through 37 Pin 'D male connector .
The M52OOA is packaged in standard nineteen inch rack mount assembly occupying one rack unit of vertical space (1.75 inches) and is sixteen inches deep _
